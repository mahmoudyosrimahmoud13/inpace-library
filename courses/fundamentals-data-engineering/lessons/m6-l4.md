# Read: Appendix A, Serialization and Compression Technical Details

The technical companion to today's chapter, and to today's project. 

Short and practical: **row-based** formats (CSV, XML, JSON lines, Avro), **columnar** formats (Parquet, ORC, Apache
Arrow in memory), hybrid formats (Hudi, Iceberg), database storage engines, and compression codecs (gzip, bzip2,
Snappy and newer ones). Note the authors' warnings about CSV, and their remark that switching a job from CSV to Parquet
sometimes makes it about 100 times faster.

## What to take away

- Row formats suit whole records; columnar formats suit analytics.
- Fast codecs (Snappy, Zstandard) trade a little size for a lot of speed.
