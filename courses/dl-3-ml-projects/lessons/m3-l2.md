# Surpassing human level, and improving your model

Past human level, progress slows: you can't easily tell bias from variance, and you lose human labels and insight. ML
already surpasses humans on lots of **structured data** problems (ads, recommendations, logistics, loans) and now on
many perception and language tasks.

The summary from the slides:

![Bias, variance and data mismatch](error_gaps.png)

Avoidable bias → bigger model, train longer or better optimizers, better architecture. Variance → more data,
regularization, better architecture. (Data mismatch, the third case, comes tomorrow.)
