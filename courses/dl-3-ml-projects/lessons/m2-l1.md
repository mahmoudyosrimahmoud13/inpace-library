# Choosing dev and test sets

**Choose a dev set and test set that reflect the data you expect to get in the future and consider important to do
well on.** And from the same distribution: the slides' loan story shows months lost when the team optimized on one
kind of applicant and was tested on another.

Sizes: dev big enough to see the differences you care about (1,000 to 10,000 examples is common; 0.1% differences need
more). Test big enough for confidence in the final number; in a big dataset, 1% may be plenty.
