# Training and testing on different distributions

You have 200,000 crisp web images and 10,000 users' phone photos. Don't shuffle them all together (the dev set would be
95% web). Instead: train on the 200,000 web + 5,000 phone; dev and test on 2,500 phone each.

To tell **variance** from **data mismatch**, carve a **train-dev** set out of the training distribution:

| human | train | train-dev | dev | problem |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | 1% | 9% | 10% | variance |
| 0% | 1% | 1.5% | 10% | data mismatch |
| 10% | 11% | 12% | 20% | avoidable bias and data mismatch |

**Addressing data mismatch:** look at dev errors to understand the difference, then make training data more similar
(collect more like dev, or **synthesize** it: add car noise to clean speech). Careful: one hour of car noise looped
over 10,000 hours of speech, and the network may overfit to that one hour.
