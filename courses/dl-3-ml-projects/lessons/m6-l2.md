# Interview drill

1. **Train 2%, dev 15%. What do you do?** First check for data mismatch with a train-dev set. Then: variance fixes, or
   mismatch fixes.
2. **How do you prioritize ideas?** Error analysis: count which categories of mistakes are most common; the share is
   a ceiling on each idea's gain.
3. **Your offline metric improved but the A/B test got worse. Why?** The dev set or metric doesn't match real use;
   fix the target first.
4. **How big should the dev set be?** Big enough to detect the smallest difference you care about.
5. **Fine-tune or train from scratch?** Fine-tune when a related pre-trained model exists and your data is limited;
   scratch when your data is huge and different.
6. **Should you correct mislabelled training data?** Random noise: often not worth it. Systematic errors: yes. Dev and
   test: if label errors are a big share of the errors you see.
