# Transfer learning

Train on task A (lots of data), then reuse the network for task B (less data): replace the last layer, and retrain
just that layer (little data) or all layers (more data). It makes sense when A and B have the **same kind of input**,
A has **much more data**, and A's low-level features help B.

## The 2026 picture

Transfer learning is now the default. You start from a **pre-trained foundation model**: ImageNet-style vision models,
self-supervised models like DINOv2, CLIP for images and text, and large language models. Then **fine-tune** it, or only
train a small head on top, or use **parameter-efficient fine-tuning** (LoRA: train small low-rank updates instead of
all the weights).

The notebook's honest lesson: pre-training on 1,000 clean scans and fine-tuning on 100 phone photos gives 28% dev
error, against 38% for training on the photos from scratch. In our runs, transfer between **different digits** with a
tiny network didn't help: the conditions above matter.
