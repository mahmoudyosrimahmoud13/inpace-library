# Explore: learn a coin's bias

Flip the coin and watch the posterior. Compare the MLE (pink, data only), the MAP estimate (green) and the posterior
mean.

![Explorer: Bayesian updating with a Beta prior](explore_beta_coin.html)

**Try:** a strong wrong prior (α = 20, β = 2 with true μ = 0.3). How many flips does it take before the posterior
"believes" the data?
