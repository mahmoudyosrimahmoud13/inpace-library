# Explore: gradient descent with your own step size

Pick a function, then change the step size γ and the momentum α. Click the picture to choose a starting point.

![Explorer: gradient descent](explore_gradient_descent.html)

**Things to find:**

1. On the round bowl, the step size that reaches the minimum in one step (it's 0.5, since f = x² + y²).
2. On the long valley, the step size where it starts to diverge (about 0.2: the steep direction has curvature 10).
3. On the banana, how much momentum helps.
