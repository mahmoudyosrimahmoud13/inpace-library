## Leaderless replication

Dynamo-style: the client writes to several replicas and reads from several (Figure 5-10). **Read repair** and
**anti-entropy**. **Quorums**: n replicas, w writes, r reads, and why **w + r > n** guarantees overlap (Figure 5-11).
Then the **limitations of quorum consistency**: the list of edge cases where stale values are still returned.
**Sloppy quorums and hinted handoff**. **Detecting concurrent writes**: last write wins, the "happens-before"
relationship, and **version vectors** (Figures 5-12 to 5-14). Read slowly; it comes back in Chapter 9.

## What to take away

- Asynchronous replication is fast, and loses recent writes when the leader fails.
- Replication lag breaks read-your-writes, monotonic reads and consistent prefix reads unless you design for them.
- Leaderless quorums: with w + r > n every read overlaps the latest write, with important exceptions.
