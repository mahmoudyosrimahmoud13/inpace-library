# 25 questions, short answers

1. **What does a neuron compute?** `g(w·a + b)`.
2. **Why non-linear activations?** Otherwise the whole network is linear.
3. **ReLU vs sigmoid in hidden layers?** ReLU: cheap, doesn't saturate for `z > 0`.
4. **What is backprop?** Chain rule backwards through the computation graph; one pass gets all gradients.
5. **Vanishing gradients?** Derivatives shrink through layers; fix with ReLU, init, normalization, residuals.
6. **Softmax vs sigmoid outputs?** One class out of N vs independent labels.
7. **Why `from_logits=True`?** Numerical stability.
8. **What does Adam do?** Per-parameter adaptive steps with momentum.
9. **Train/dev/test?** Fit / choose / report.
10. **High bias fixes?** Bigger model, more features, less regularization.
11. **High variance fixes?** More data, regularization, simpler model, fewer features.
12. **Learning curves?** Error vs training size; tell you whether more data helps.
13. **Baseline?** Human level or a simple model, to judge what "high" error means.
14. **Error analysis?** Count causes in mistakes; work on the biggest.
15. **Augmentation?** Realistic distortions of existing data.
16. **Transfer learning?** Reuse pre-trained layers; fine-tune.
17. **Data drift?** Live data changes from training data; monitor and retrain.
18. **Precision vs recall?** Correct among flagged vs found among actual.
19. **F1?** Harmonic mean of P and R.
20. **ROC-AUC vs PR-AUC?** PR is more telling with rare positives.
21. **Entropy and information gain?** Impurity, and how much a split reduces it.
22. **Why ensembles?** Single trees have high variance; averaging or boosting reduces error.
23. **Bagging vs boosting?** Parallel on bootstrap samples vs sequential on errors.
24. **Trees vs networks?** Tabular vs unstructured data.
25. **Dropout?** Randomly zero units during training to reduce co-adaptation (a regularizer).

## Case question

> "Our churn model has 95% accuracy but the business says it's useless."

Ask what share of customers churn (probably about 5%, so accuracy means nothing). Check precision and recall for
churners, look at the cost of a missed churner vs a wasted offer, choose a threshold by that cost, and check the
model works across customer segments.
