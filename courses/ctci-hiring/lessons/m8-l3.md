# Looking back, and what to do next

You've been through the whole hiring process: how companies decide, your CV and stories, Big O, a method for any
question, every major topic with practice problems, design questions, and offers.

## The checklist

Before your first real interview, check that you can say yes to each line:

- [ ] I have a one-page CV with results in every bullet, and two projects I can discuss in depth.
- [ ] I have 5 to 8 stories ready, and "tell me about yourself" practised out loud.
- [ ] I can give the Big O of my code, time and space, without guessing.
- [ ] I follow the method out loud, every time, even on easy problems.
- [ ] I can write from memory: a hash map solution, linked list reversal, BFS and DFS, binary search, merge sort, a
      memoized recursion and a backtracking search.
- [ ] I have solved at least 50 problems across all the course lists, and redone the ones I needed help with.
- [ ] I have done at least three mock interviews and acted on the feedback.
- [ ] I know what I want from an offer, and how I'll negotiate.
