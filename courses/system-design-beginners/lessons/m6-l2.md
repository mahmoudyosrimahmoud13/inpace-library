# Resources for system design

The video recommends going deeper with books. The best-known one is **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** by
Martin Kleppmann: read it slowly, one chapter a week.

Free places to keep practising:

- Draw one design per week: URL shortener, chat app, news feed, ride sharing, file storage.
- Read engineering blogs of big companies (Netflix, Uber, Discord, Cloudflare) and redraw their diagrams yourself.
