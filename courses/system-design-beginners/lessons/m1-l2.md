# What system design is

**System design** is deciding how the parts of a software system fit together so it does its job for real users:
which services exist, how they talk, where data lives, and what happens when something breaks.

Two levels you'll hear all week:

| Level | Question it answers | What you draw |
|---|---|---|
| High-level design (HLD) | Which big parts exist and how do they talk? | Boxes and arrows: clients, servers, databases, queues, CDNs |
| Low-level design (LLD) | How is one part built inside? | Classes, methods, UML diagrams, code |

**Design patterns** (mentioned in this part) are named, reusable answers to common problems, such as *Observer*,
*Factory* or *Strategy*. You don't need to memorise them yet; you'll meet some in the low-level design days.
