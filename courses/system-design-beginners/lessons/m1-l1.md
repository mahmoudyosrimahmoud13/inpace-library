# Start here

## Your plan: 7 days, about 45 to 60 minutes a day

| Day | Module | Video time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What system design is, and the requirements | about 11 min |
| 2 | The high-level design: API and database | about 11 min |
| 3 | Network protocols and choosing a datastore | about 12 min |
| 4 | Processing and delivering video | about 12 min |
| 5 | Low-level design with UML | about 27 min |
| 6 | Coding the server | about 13 min |
| 7 | The building blocks every interview expects | no video |

Each lesson plays **only its piece** of the video and continues from where you stopped. Watch the piece, then read
the notes under it: they explain the words the video uses quickly and add what a beginner usually misses.

## How the video teaches

The whole video designs **one system: a live streaming service** (think of a sports match streamed to millions of
viewers). It goes from requirements, to the big boxes and arrows (high-level design), to the classes and code of
one part (low-level design). Keep a notebook open and draw along: system design is learned with a pen.

## Before you start

- You should know one programming language and what an API and a database are.
- Paper and pen, or a free drawing tool such as excalidraw.com or draw.io.
