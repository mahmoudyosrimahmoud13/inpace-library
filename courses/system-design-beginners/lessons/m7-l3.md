# A 45-minute interview framework

| Minutes | Step | What you say or draw |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Requirements | functional, non-functional, scope; ask about scale |
| 5-10 | Estimates | users, requests per second, storage per day (rough is fine) |
| 10-15 | API | 3 to 6 endpoints |
| 15-30 | High-level design | boxes and arrows; walk one request through |
| 30-40 | Deep dive | the hardest part: storage, scaling, failures |
| 40-45 | Wrap up | bottlenecks, trade-offs, what you'd do with more time |

## A quick estimate

*10 million daily users, each opens the app 5 times a day:* 50 million requests a day ÷ about 86,400 seconds is about
**600 requests per second** on average; plan for 2 to 3 times that at peak.

## Words that show you think in trade-offs

*"This gives us X, at the cost of Y. Given our requirement Z, I'd accept that."*
