# Watch: K-means clustering

StatQuest runs K-means by hand on a small data set: pick starting centres, assign, update, repeat, and how to choose K
with an "elbow" plot of the total variation. It's section 15.1 with pictures.

One thing to notice: the elbow method compares the distortion J for different K. J always goes down as K grows (with K
equal to the number of points it's zero), which is the over-fitting story from part 1 again.
