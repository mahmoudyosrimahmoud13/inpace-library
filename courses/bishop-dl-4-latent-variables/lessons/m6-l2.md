# Watch: Principal component analysis, step by step

StatQuest builds PCA from scratch with a tiny data set: centre the data, find the line that best fits it, the next one at
a right angle, and how much variation each one explains. It's section 16.1 with pictures.

When he talks about the line that minimizes the distances to the points **and** maximizes the spread of the projections,
that's the book's two derivations (16.1.1 and 16.1.2) giving the same answer.
