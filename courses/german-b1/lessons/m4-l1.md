# Explaining a process

Yasin visits a recycling centre with his son's class. The guide explains what happens to the rubbish, using the passive: wird gesammelt, wird sortiert.

## Listen first: the conversation

**At the recycling centre**

| German | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Führerin:** Herzlich willkommen! Hier seht ihr, was mit eurem Müll passiert. | Welcome! Here you'll see what happens to your rubbish. |
| **Kind:** Was passiert mit den Plastikflaschen? | What happens to the plastic bottles? |
| **Führerin:** Zuerst werden sie in der gelben Tonne gesammelt. Dann werden sie mit Lastwagen hierher gebracht. | First they're collected in the yellow bin. Then they're brought here by lorry. |
| **Yasin:** Und wie wird der Müll sortiert? Von Hand? | And how is the rubbish sorted? By hand? |
| **Führerin:** Nur zum Teil. Das meiste wird von Maschinen sortiert. Danach wird das Plastik gewaschen und klein gemacht. | Only partly. Most of it is sorted by machines. After that, the plastic is washed and shredded. |
| **Kind:** Und was wird daraus gemacht? | And what's made out of it? |
| **Führerin:** Neue Flaschen, Kleidung, sogar Möbel. In Deutschland werden fast alle Flaschen recycelt. | New bottles, clothes, even furniture. In Germany almost all bottles are recycled. |
| **Yasin:** Interessant! Und das Glas wurde früher auch hier sortiert, oder? | Interesting! And the glass used to be sorted here too, right? |
| **Führerin:** Ja, aber seit 2020 wird es in einer anderen Anlage bearbeitet. | Yes, but since 2020 it's been processed in another plant. |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Was passiert mit...? | What happens to...? |
| Zuerst... dann... danach... zum Schluss... | First... then... after that... finally... |
| Es wird gesammelt / sortiert. | It's collected / sorted. |
| von Hand / von Maschinen | by hand / by machines |
| Was wird daraus gemacht? | What's made out of it? |
| der Müll / die Mülltrennung | rubbish / separating rubbish |
| die gelbe Tonne / der Biomüll | the yellow bin (packaging) / organic waste |
| das Pfand | the bottle deposit |
| zum Teil | partly |
| fast alle | almost all |
| Wie funktioniert das? | How does that work? |

## Say it right

**Clear participles.** In the passive, the participle at the end carries the meaning, so say it fully: *wird geSAMmelt, wird sorTIERT, wird reCYcelt* (English loan: "ri-SY-kelt"). Many technical words are loans from English or French; German pronounces them its own way: *recyceln*, *Container*, *Maschine* (ma-SHEE-ne).
