# Level check: can you do B1?

At B1 you're an **independent user**. Answer honestly: *yes*, *almost*, or *not yet*.

## I can...

- give and defend my opinion with connectors (deshalb, trotzdem, obwohl) and correct word order;
- talk about wishes and hypothetical situations, and make polite requests with the Konjunktiv II;
- describe people and things with relative clauses;
- explain how something is made or done with the passive;
- tell my life story and write a simple application letter using the Präteritum;
- explain my goals with um... zu and damit, and ask polite indirect questions;
- understand the main points of conversations, videos and podcasts at natural speed on everyday topics;
- keep a conversation going for several minutes, even with mistakes.

For each *not yet*, return to the unit, re-listen, and redo the voice note.

## Final tasks

1. **Listening:** finish *Nicos Weg B1* on [DW Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com/) and watch two [Easy German](https://www.youtube.com/@EasyGerman) episodes without subtitles. Write a 3-sentence summary and one opinion for each.
2. **Speaking:** record a **4-minute** voice note: present yourself and your career, give your opinion on a topic you care about, say what you'd change if you could, and explain your goals.
3. **Writing:** write a formal email (about 120 words) and an opinion text (about 180 words).

## What next?

You can now learn German **from German**: series with German subtitles, news (DW's *Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten* is a great bridge), books, and conversations with native speakers. For an official certificate, look at the **Goethe-Zertifikat B1**, **telc Deutsch B1** or the **DTZ** (Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer).
