# Giving and defending an opinion

Flatmates Jana and Yasin discuss whether cars should be banned from the city centre. Notice the connectors: obwohl, trotzdem, deshalb.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Cars in the city?**

| German | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Jana:** Hast du gelesen? Die Stadt will das Zentrum für Autos sperren. Ich finde das super. | Have you read it? The city wants to close the centre to cars. I think that's great. |
| **Yasin:** Wirklich? Ich bin da anderer Meinung. Viele Leute brauchen das Auto, zum Beispiel für die Arbeit. | Really? I see it differently. Many people need their car, for work, for example. |
| **Jana:** Das stimmt, aber es gibt Busse und Fahrräder. Außerdem ist die Luft im Zentrum sehr schlecht. | That's true, but there are buses and bikes. Besides, the air in the centre is very bad. |
| **Yasin:** Obwohl ich die Umwelt wichtig finde, denke ich an die Geschäfte. Wenn keine Autos kommen, kommen auch weniger Kunden. | Although I think the environment is important, I'm thinking of the shops. If no cars come, fewer customers come too. |
| **Jana:** Ich glaube nicht, dass das stimmt. In anderen Städten haben die Geschäfte danach mehr verkauft, weil mehr Leute zu Fuß gehen. | I don't think that's true. In other cities, shops sold more afterwards, because more people walk. |
| **Yasin:** Na gut, das ist ein Argument. Trotzdem sollte man an ältere Menschen denken. | OK, that's an argument. Still, we should think about older people. |
| **Jana:** Da hast du recht. Deshalb brauchen wir mehr Busse und Taxis für sie. | You're right there. That's why we need more buses and taxis for them. |
| **Yasin:** Okay, in dem Punkt sind wir uns einig. | OK, on that point we agree. |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ich finde, dass... | I think that... |
| Meiner Meinung nach... | In my opinion... |
| Ich bin anderer Meinung. | I see it differently. |
| Das stimmt. / Das stimmt nicht. | That's true. / That's not true. |
| Da hast du recht. | You're right there. |
| Ich glaube nicht, dass... | I don't think that... |
| außerdem | besides, also |
| trotzdem | still, nevertheless |
| deshalb | that's why, so |
| obwohl | although |
| zum Beispiel | for example |
| Wir sind uns einig. | We agree. |

## Say it right

**Fillers that make you sound natural:** *also...* (so / well), *naja...* (well...), *eigentlich* (actually), *halt* / *eben* (just, simply), *genau!* (exactly!), *oder?* (right?, at the end). Use one or two instead of silence while you think: *Also, eigentlich finde ich das gut, oder?*
