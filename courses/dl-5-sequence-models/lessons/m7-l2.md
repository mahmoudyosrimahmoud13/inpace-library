# Speech recognition and trigger words

Audio → text with attention, or with **CTC** (connectionist temporal classification): the network outputs a character
(or blank) for every short audio frame, and repeated characters not separated by a blank collapse ("ttt_h_eee" →
"the"). **Trigger word** detection ("Alexa") labels the moments right after the word.

Today: large transformer models trained on huge amounts of audio (Whisper-style) recognize speech in many languages
out of the box.
