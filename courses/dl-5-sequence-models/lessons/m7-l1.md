# The attention model

Encoder-decoders squeeze the whole input into one vector, and quality drops for long sentences. **Attention** lets
the decoder look back at **every** encoder state when writing each word, with weights `α<t, t'>` that say how much to
look at input word `t'` when writing output word `t`:

```text
context<t> = Σ_t' α<t,t'> · a<t'>          α = softmax over t' of e<t,t'>
```

`e` comes from a small network of the decoder's state and each encoder state. The weights are learned, and you can
plot them: the slides' date example ("July 20th 1969" → "1969-07-20") shows the model looking at the right parts.
