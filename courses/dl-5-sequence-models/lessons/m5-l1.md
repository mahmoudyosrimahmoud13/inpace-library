# Neural language models, word2vec and negative sampling

Embeddings come from **predicting words from their context**:
- a neural language model predicts the next word from the previous few;
- **skip-gram (word2vec)** picks a context word and predicts a nearby target word;
- softmax over 10,000 words is slow, so **negative sampling** turns it into many cheap yes/no questions: is this
  (context, word) pair real, or a random word? Random "negative" words are sampled with probability proportional to
  frequency^(3/4).
