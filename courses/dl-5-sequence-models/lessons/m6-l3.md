# BLEU score

BLEU compares a machine translation with human references: **modified n-gram precision** (each n-gram counts at most
as often as it appears in a reference), combined for n = 1 to 4, times a **brevity penalty** for short outputs. "the
the the the the the" scores 0.

BLEU is still reported, but it misses meaning. Today, translation and text generation are also judged with learned
metrics (like COMET) and with human or strong-model judges.
