# Interview drill

1. **Why did transformers replace RNNs?** They train in parallel over the sequence and connect any two positions
   directly, so long-range dependencies are easy and GPUs are used fully.
2. **What are Q, K and V?** Learned projections: queries ask, keys are matched against queries, values are what gets
   mixed in by the attention weights.
3. **Why positional encoding?** Attention by itself ignores order.
4. **LSTM vs GRU?** LSTM has separate forget and output gates; GRU merges them and is lighter. Both fix vanishing
   gradients with a gated memory.
5. **Greedy vs beam search vs sampling?** Greedy is fast but short-sighted; beam keeps B candidates (good for
   translation); sampling with temperature/top-p adds variety (chat, creative text).
6. **Cost of self-attention?** It grows with the square of the sequence length; long contexts need efficient kernels
   or other tricks.
7. **How would you build a sentiment classifier today?** Fine-tune a small pre-trained encoder (or a small LLM) on your
   labelled data; check bias and errors on a dev set that looks like real users.
