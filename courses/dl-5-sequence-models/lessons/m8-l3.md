# From the transformer to today's language models

- **Encoder-only** (BERT-style): read text, produce representations; for classification and search.
- **Decoder-only** (GPT-style): causal attention, next-token prediction; today's chat models. Pre-trained on huge text,
  then **instruction-tuned** and aligned with human feedback.
- **Encoder-decoder** (T5-style): translation, summarization.
- Changes since the original paper: **pre-norm** (LayerNorm before each block) or RMSNorm, **rotary positional
  embeddings** instead of sines, **GELU/SwiGLU** feed-forwards, mixture-of-experts layers, and fast attention kernels
  (FlashAttention, behind `scaled_dot_product_attention`).
- Vision transformers apply the same blocks to image patches; speech models to audio frames.

In practice you **start from a pre-trained model** (Hugging Face `transformers`, section 9 of the notebook) and
fine-tune it, often with LoRA, rather than training from zero.
