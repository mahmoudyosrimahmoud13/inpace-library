# Sequence data and notation

Speech, music, text, DNA, video, and sentiment: inputs or outputs (or both) are sequences, often of different lengths.

`x<t>` is the t-th element, `T_x` the input length, `x(i)<t>` element `t` of example `i`. Words become numbers via a
**vocabulary**: one-hot vectors of size 10,000 in the slides (today, **sub-word tokens** from a tokenizer, so any word,
typo or name can be written as pieces; vocabularies of 30,000 to 200,000 tokens).
