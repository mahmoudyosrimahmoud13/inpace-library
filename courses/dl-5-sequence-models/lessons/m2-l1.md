# Language models

A **language model** gives the probability of a sentence: `P(y<1>, …, y<T>)`, built word by word as
`P(y<1>)·P(y<2> | y<1>)·…`. Train an RNN to predict the next word at each step (input at step t is the true word
t−1: "teacher forcing"); the loss is the cross-entropy at each step, summed.

That one idea, **predict the next token**, scaled up with transformers and enormous data, is how today's large
language models are pre-trained.
