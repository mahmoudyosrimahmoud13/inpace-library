# Self-attention

Drop the RNN; let every word attend to every word, **in parallel**. Each word gets a **query** `q`, **key** `k` and
**value** `v` (learned linear projections of its embedding):

```text
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(Q·Kᵀ / √d_k) · V
```

![Self-attention weights](attention.png)

Each row is one word's attention over all words (here with toy embeddings: the two "the" attend to each other, "mat" to
"cat"). Dividing by `√d_k` keeps the softmax from saturating. In PyTorch, one fused, fast call:

```python
out = torch.nn.functional.scaled_dot_product_attention(q, k, v, is_causal=False)
```

The notebook checks a from-scratch version against it, with and without a **causal mask** (a word can't look at later
words: what GPT-style models use).
