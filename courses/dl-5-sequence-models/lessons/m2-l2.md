# Sampling, and character-level models

To generate, **sample** a word from the model's distribution, feed it back in, repeat. Character-level models never
meet unknown words but need longer sequences.

**Temperature** reshapes the distribution: `softmax(z / T)`. Low T: safe, repetitive. High T: surprising, more errors.
The notebook trains a small character model on a short text; at T = 0.5 it recites, at T = 1.5 it starts to misspell
("walkiing"). Today's generators also use **top-k** and **top-p** sampling: only sample among the most likely tokens.
