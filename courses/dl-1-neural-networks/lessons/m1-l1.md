About 1 to 1.5 hours a day, for 8 days.

Code in this course is written for **PyTorch 2.15** (October 2026), **TensorFlow 2.21** and **Keras 3.15**. The slides use TensorFlow 1-style code in places; the notes show the current way.

**How each lesson works:** the slides for that part open at the top of the lesson. Read them, then the notes under them:
the notes explain what the slide skips, show today's PyTorch and Keras code, and point to the experiments in the
notebook. Notebooks are files that come with the course: open them in Google Colab (free, nothing to install), download
them for Jupyter or VS Code, or read their PDF versions with all the outputs.

This is part 1 of 5: **Neural networks and deep learning**. Then: improving deep networks, structuring ML projects, convolutional networks, and sequence models and transformers.

**Credit:** the slides are from the *Deep Learning Specialization* (course 1, Neural Networks and Deep Learning) by DeepLearning.AI, taught by
Andrew Ng, shared under Creative Commons BY-SA 2.0 for non-commercial educational use. Take the full course on
Coursera for the videos and graded labs.

Maths is written in plain text: `W·x` is a matrix product, `Σ` a sum, `^` a power, `[l]` the layer, `(i)` the example.
