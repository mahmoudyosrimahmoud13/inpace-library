# Interview drill

Answer out loud, then check:

1. **Why do we need non-linear activations?** Without them the whole network is one linear function.
2. **What is backprop?** The chain rule applied backwards through the computation graph, reusing cached forward values.
3. **Why random init, and why not huge weights?** Zeros keep units identical; huge weights saturate tanh/sigmoid and
   explode activations in deep ReLU networks.
4. **Shapes of `W[l]` and `dW[l]`?** Both `(n[l], n[l−1])`.
5. **Why vectorize?** Matrix operations run on SIMD/GPU hardware; loops in Python don't.
6. **Sigmoid vs softmax?** Sigmoid for one yes/no (or several independent yes/no labels); softmax for one choice among
   many classes.
7. **Logits?** Raw scores before the final sigmoid/softmax. Losses take logits for numerical stability.
8. **When would you not use deep learning?** Small structured tables (gradient-boosted trees often win), when you need
   a simple explainable rule, or when there's very little data.
