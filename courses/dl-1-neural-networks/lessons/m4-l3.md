# Why the cost is what it is

Where cross-entropy comes from: `p(y | x) = ŷ^y · (1 − ŷ)^(1−y)`. Maximize the likelihood of the training labels;
take the log and flip the sign, and you get the cost. **Minimizing cross-entropy = maximum likelihood.**
