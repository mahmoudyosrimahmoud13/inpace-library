# Why deep?

Early layers find simple patterns (edges, sounds), later layers combine them (eyes, words, faces). And some functions
need **exponentially** more units in a shallow network than in a deep one (circuit theory: the slides' XOR example).

A real comparison, both networks trained the same way on a spiral:

![Shallow and wide vs deeper](depth_vs_width.png)

The deeper network has **more** weights here (609 vs 257) but reaches a far lower loss and a cleaner boundary. Depth
isn't free, though: very deep plain networks are hard to train (part 2 and the ResNets in part 4 explain why).
