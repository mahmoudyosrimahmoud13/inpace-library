# Backpropagation for one hidden layer

```text
dZ[2] = A[2] − Y
dW[2] = dZ[2]·A[1]ᵀ / m          db[2] = mean of dZ[2] over examples
dZ[1] = W[2]ᵀ·dZ[2] * g[1]′(Z[1])   (* is element-wise)
dW[1] = dZ[1]·Xᵀ / m             db[1] = mean of dZ[1] over examples
```

The shapes always match their parameters: `dW[l]` has the shape of `W[l]`. Check that first when you debug.

**Gradient checking:** compare your backprop with `(J(θ + ε) − J(θ − ε)) / 2ε`. The notebook's section 4 gets a relative
difference of about `7·10⁻¹⁰`: correct. Around `10⁻³` would mean a bug.
