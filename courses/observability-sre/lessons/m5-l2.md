# Alerts people trust

Bad alerting burns people out: hundreds of alerts, most ignored, and the real one missed.

## Rules

- **Page only for things that hurt users now and need a human now.** Everything else is a ticket or a dashboard.
- Every page needs a **runbook link** and must be **actionable**.
- Alert on **symptoms** (errors, latency, SLO burn), not causes (CPU 85%). High CPU with happy users is fine.
- If an alert fired and nobody needed to do anything, fix or delete it.

## Burn-rate alerts on the SLO

Burn rate = how fast you're using the error budget (1 = exactly on budget for the window). Alert when it's fast enough to matter:

| Window (long and short) | Burn rate | Budget used | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 h and 5 min | 14.4 | 2% in an hour | **Page** |
| 6 h and 30 min | 6 | 5% in 6 hours | **Page** |
| 3 days and 6 h | 1 | 10% in 3 days | Ticket |

```yaml
# Prometheus alert rule, SLO 99.5% (error budget 0.005)
- alert: CheckoutErrorBudgetBurnFast
  expr: |
    job:http_errors:ratio_rate1h > (14.4 * 0.005)
    and
    job:http_errors:ratio_rate5m > (14.4 * 0.005)
  labels: {severity: page}
  annotations:
    summary: "Checkout burning error budget fast ({{ $value | humanizePercentage }} errors)"
    runbook: "https://wiki.example.com/runbooks/checkout-errors"
```

The short window makes the alert stop soon after the problem ends.

## Alertmanager

Groups related alerts into one notification, routes by labels (`team`, `severity`), silences during maintenance, and **inhibits** child alerts (don't page for 30 services when the database is down).

## Still worth a ticket-level alert

Disk predicted full in 4 hours (`predict_linear`), certificates expiring in 14 days, backups that didn't run, error budget below 25%.
