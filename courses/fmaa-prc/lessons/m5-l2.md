# Revenue Recognition (5-Step Model) and Equity Transactions

## Revenue Recognition — ASC 606 / IFRS 15

The core principle: **recognize revenue when (or as) the entity satisfies performance obligations** by transferring goods or services to a customer, in the amount that reflects the consideration the entity expects to receive.

### The 5-Step Process

**Step 1: Identify the contract with a customer.**
- Must have commercial substance.
- Both parties have approved it.
- Rights and payment terms can be identified.
- Collection is probable.

**Step 2: Identify the performance obligations.**
- Each distinct good or service is a separate performance obligation.
- A good/service is distinct if the customer can benefit from it on its own and it is separately identifiable from other promises in the contract.

**Step 3: Determine the transaction price.**
- The amount expected to be received.
- May include variable consideration (discounts, refunds, royalties) — estimate using expected value or most likely amount.
- Exclude amounts collected on behalf of third parties (e.g., sales tax).

**Step 4: Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations.**
- If a contract has multiple obligations, allocate based on **relative standalone selling prices**.

**Step 5: Recognize revenue when (or as) each obligation is satisfied.**
- **At a point in time:** When control transfers (most goods).
- **Over time:** When the customer simultaneously receives and consumes benefits (most services).

### Common Applications

| Situation | Recognition |
|---|---|
| Sale of goods | When control transfers to customer |
| Service contract | Over the service period |
| Magazine subscription | Over the subscription period |
| Software license | Depends on whether distinct from services |
| Construction contract | Usually over time (percentage of completion) |

## Equity Transactions

**Equity** = Assets − Liabilities. It represents the owners' residual interest.

### Components of Equity (Corporation)

- **Common stock** — recorded at par value (a nominal legal amount).
- **Additional paid-in capital (APIC)** — amount paid above par value.
- **Retained earnings** — cumulative net income not paid as dividends.
- **Treasury stock** — shares repurchased by the company (reduces equity).
- **Accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI)** — unrealized gains/losses.

### Issuing Shares

A company issues 10,000 shares at $50 each (par value $1):

### Income Measurement — Expense Recognition

Expenses are matched to the revenues they help generate:

- **Matching principle** — associate expenses with the revenues they produce (COGS matched to sales revenue).
- **Period expenses** — costs not directly linked to specific revenue; expensed in the period incurred (rent, admin salaries).
- **Systematic and rational allocation** — spread costs over the periods benefited (depreciation).

> **Exam tip:** Under the 5-step model, recognize revenue when *performance obligations are satisfied* — not when cash is received. A prepayment creates a liability (deferred revenue) until the obligation is fulfilled.
