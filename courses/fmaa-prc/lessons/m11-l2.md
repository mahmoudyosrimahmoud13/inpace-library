# Breakeven Analysis and Cost Decision-Making

## Contribution Margin

**Contribution margin (CM)** = Sales price − Variable costs per unit

It is the amount each unit **contributes** to covering fixed costs and then to profit.

**Total contribution margin** = CM per unit × Units sold  
**CM ratio** = CM per unit ÷ Sales price per unit

---

## Breakeven Analysis

The **breakeven point** is the sales volume at which total revenue equals total costs — operating income is zero.

**Formula — Breakeven in units:**
> Breakeven units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution margin per unit

**Formula — Breakeven in dollars:**
> Breakeven sales $ = Fixed costs ÷ CM ratio

**Example:**  
Sales price = $50, Variable cost = $30, Fixed costs = $20,000  
CM per unit = $50 − $30 = $20  
CM ratio = $20 ÷ $50 = 40%

Breakeven units = $20,000 ÷ $20 = **1,000 units**  
Breakeven sales = $20,000 ÷ 0.40 = **$50,000**

---

## Breakeven Chart

```
Revenue ($)
  |          /Revenue line

  |   profit/
BE |-------/
  |  loss / Total cost line
  |      /
  |  FC /
  |----/------------ Units
  0   BE
```

- Left of breakeven → loss (revenue < costs).
- Right of breakeven → profit (revenue > costs).
- The gap between the revenue and total cost lines = profit (or loss).

---

## Target Profit Analysis

To achieve a target operating profit:  
> Units needed = (Fixed costs + Target profit) ÷ CM per unit

**Example (from above):** Target profit = $10,000  
Units needed = ($20,000 + $10,000) ÷ $20 = **1,500 units**

---

## Margin of Safety

**Margin of safety** = Actual (or budgeted) sales − Breakeven sales

It measures how much sales can fall before the company reaches breakeven.

**Margin of safety %** = Margin of safety ÷ Actual sales × 100

A higher margin of safety = lower business risk.

## Relevant Costs for Decision-Making

When making short-term decisions, use **relevant costs** only:

**Relevant costs:**
- Future costs that differ between alternatives.
- Always includes variable costs.
- May include avoidable fixed costs.

**Irrelevant costs:**
- **Sunk costs** — already incurred; cannot be changed. **Always ignore sunk costs.**
- Common fixed costs that will not change regardless of the decision.

### Common Decision Types

**Make vs. buy:** Compare relevant production cost vs. purchase price (plus any qualitative factors like quality and reliability).

**Accept a special order:** Accept if incremental revenue > incremental costs (and spare capacity exists). Ignore fixed costs if they don't change.

**Drop a product/segment:** Drop only if the segment margin is negative (i.e., variable costs + avoidable fixed costs > revenue). If segment margin is positive, keeping it covers some common fixed costs.

**Sell now vs. process further:** Process further if incremental revenue > incremental costs of further processing. Ignore joint costs (sunk at the split-off point).

> **Exam tip:** Breakeven = FC ÷ CM per unit. This formula is very commonly tested. Also know that **sunk costs are always irrelevant** in decision-making — the FMAA tests this repeatedly.
