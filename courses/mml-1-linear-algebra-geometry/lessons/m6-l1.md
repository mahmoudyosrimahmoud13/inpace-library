# Read: orthonormal bases, complements, function spaces (3.5–3.7)

Short sections with big ideas.

- **Orthonormal basis (3.5)**: basis vectors of length 1, all orthogonal. Coordinates become easy: the j-th coordinate
  of x is just ⟨b_j, x⟩. **Gram-Schmidt** turns any basis into an orthonormal one (more in 3.8.3).
- **Orthogonal complement (3.6)**: U⊥ is everything orthogonal to U. In ℝ³, the complement of a plane through 0 is
  its normal line. dim U + dim U⊥ = n. You'll use this for the hyperplanes of SVMs (course 4).
- **Inner product of functions (3.7)**: ⟨u, v⟩ = ∫ u(x)v(x) dx. With it, sin and cos are orthogonal on [−π, π]: the
  idea behind Fourier series. Skim it; it shows how far the geometry reaches.
