# Read: matrices (2.2)

Section 2.2 is the toolbox: matrix addition, multiplication, identity, inverse, transpose, symmetric matrices.

**What to really understand**

- **Multiplication**: entry (i, j) of AB is row i of A **dot** column j of B, so the inner sizes must match:
  (m × n)(n × k) = (m × k).
- **Not commutative**: AB ≠ BA in general (often BA isn't even defined). It *is* associative: (AB)C = A(BC).
- **Inverse**: A⁻¹ is the matrix with AA⁻¹ = A⁻¹A = I. Only square matrices can have one, and not all do. Learn the
  2 × 2 formula (1/(ad − bc))·[[d, −b], [−c, a]]: ad − bc is the **determinant**, coming in chapter 4.
- **Transpose rules**: (AB)ᵀ = BᵀAᵀ and (AB)⁻¹ = B⁻¹A⁻¹. Note the order flips: "socks and shoes".
- **Scalar multiplication** and the "Compact form" of a system Ax = b close the section.

Skim the Hadamard (element-wise) product remark: NumPy's `*` is element-wise, `@` is matrix multiplication. That
difference causes many bugs.
