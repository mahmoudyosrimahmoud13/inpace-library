# Read: rotations (3.9) and further reading (3.10)

![Rotation by 60°: lengths and angles stay the same](rotation.png)

- **Rotation in ℝ²**: R(θ) = [[cos θ, −sin θ], [sin θ, cos θ]]. Read the columns: where e₁ and e₂ go.
- **In ℝ³**, rotate about one axis at a time; the order matters (rotations don't commute in 3D).
- **In n dimensions**, **Givens rotations** turn in one plane and leave the rest alone.
- Rotations are **orthogonal matrices** (AᵀA = I, so A⁻¹ = Aᵀ): they keep lengths and angles, and their determinant
  is +1 (−1 would be a reflection).
