# Explore: a matrix moves the plane

Drag the sliders and watch the grid, the unit square and the two column vectors. The readout shows the determinant,
trace, eigenvalues and rank; you'll meet all of them properly in course 2, but you can already see what they mean.

![Explorer: change a 2 × 2 matrix](explore_transform.html)

![The grid before and after a matrix: the columns of A are where e₁ and e₂ land](transform.png)

**Try:** press "squash flat". The plane collapses onto a line, the square has area 0 and the matrix has no inverse.
That's what a missing inverse *looks like*.
