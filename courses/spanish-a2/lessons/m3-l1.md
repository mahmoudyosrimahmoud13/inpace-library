# Childhood memories

Carmen's grandmother tells her what life was like when she was a child in a village. A new past tense describes how things used to be: -aba, -ía.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Grandma's village**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carmen:** Abuela, ¿cómo era tu vida cuando eras pequeña? | Grandma, what was your life like when you were little? |
| **Abuela:** Vivíamos en un pueblo pequeño. No teníamos tele ni coche. | We lived in a small village. We didn't have a TV or a car. |
| **Carmen:** ¿Y qué hacíais en vuestro tiempo libre? | And what did you do in your free time? |
| **Abuela:** Jugábamos en la calle todo el día. Mi hermano y yo íbamos al río en verano. | We played in the street all day. My brother and I used to go to the river in summer. |
| **Carmen:** ¿Ibas al colegio? | Did you go to school? |
| **Abuela:** Sí, pero el colegio estaba lejos. Caminábamos una hora cada mañana. | Yes, but the school was far away. We walked an hour every morning. |
| **Carmen:** ¡Una hora! ¿Y eras buena estudiante? | An hour! And were you a good student? |
| **Abuela:** Era muy habladora... Un día la maestra me castigó sin recreo. ¡Pero me gustaba mucho leer! | I was very chatty... One day the teacher kept me in at break. But I really liked reading! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cuando era pequeño/a... | When I was little... |
| ¿Cómo era...? | What was... like? |
| Vivíamos en... | We lived in... |
| Jugábamos en la calle. | We used to play in the street. |
| Siempre / normalmente / a menudo | always / usually / often |
| todos los veranos | every summer |
| Me gustaba mucho... | I really liked... |
| No había internet. | There was no internet. |
| antes / ahora | before / now |
| Un día... | One day... |
| de niño/a | as a child |

## Say it right

**Diphthongs**: two vowels in one syllable. When **i** or **u** (unstressed) sits next to another vowel, they glide together: *bueno* (BWE-no), *siempre* (SYEM-pre), *pueblo*, *ciudad*, *aire*, *hoy*. A written accent on i or u splits them into two syllables: *había* (ha-BÍ-a), *río* (RÍ-o), *día*, *tenía*.

Say: *pueblo, siempre, bueno* (one syllable each part) and *había, tenía, río* (split).
