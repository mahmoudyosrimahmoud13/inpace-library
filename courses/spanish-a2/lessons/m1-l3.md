## The grammar of this unit: the pretérito indefinido (regular)

The **preterite** (*pretérito indefinido*) tells what happened at a finished moment in the past: *ayer, el sábado, en 2020, hace dos días*.

| | visitar | comer | salir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | visit**é** | com**í** | sal**í** |
| tú | visit**aste** | com**iste** | sal**iste** |
| él / ella / usted | visit**ó** | com**ió** | sal**ió** |
| nosotros | visit**amos** | com**imos** | sal**imos** |
| vosotros | visit**asteis** | com**isteis** | sal**isteis** |
| ellos / ustedes | visit**aron** | com**ieron** | sal**ieron** |

Notice:

- **-er and -ir verbs share the same endings.**
- The *nosotros* form of -ar and -ir verbs is the same as in the present (*visitamos, salimos*). The context tells you which: *Ayer salimos* = we went out.
- Spelling changes in the **yo** form keep the sound: *llegar → llegué*, *buscar → busqué*, *empezar → empecé*.
- Reflexives keep their pronoun: *me levanté, te quedaste, se acostó*.
- *ver* has no accents: *vi, viste, vio*.
