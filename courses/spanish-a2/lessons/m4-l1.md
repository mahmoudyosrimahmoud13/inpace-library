# In a clothes shop

Yusuf needs a jacket. Listen for comparisons (más... que) and the little words lo and la, which replace a noun.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Buying a jacket**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Dependienta:** Hola, ¿le puedo ayudar en algo? | Hello, can I help you with anything? |
| **Yusuf:** Sí, busco una chaqueta para el invierno. | Yes, I'm looking for a jacket for the winter. |
| **Dependienta:** Tenemos esta negra y esta azul. La negra es más larga y más caliente. | We have this black one and this blue one. The black one is longer and warmer. |
| **Yusuf:** ¿Y cuál es más barata? | And which one is cheaper? |
| **Dependienta:** La azul. Cuesta ochenta euros; la negra cuesta ciento veinte. | The blue one. It costs eighty euros; the black one costs a hundred and twenty. |
| **Yusuf:** Me gusta la negra. ¿Me la puedo probar? Uso la talla M. | I like the black one. Can I try it on? I wear size M. |
| **Dependienta:** Claro, los probadores están al fondo. | Of course, the fitting rooms are at the back. |
| **Yusuf:** Me queda un poco pequeña. ¿La tiene en una talla más grande? | It's a bit small on me. Do you have it in a bigger size? |
| **Dependienta:** Sí, aquí tiene la L. Es tan bonita como la otra. | Yes, here's the L. It's as nice as the other one. |
| **Yusuf:** Perfecta. Me la llevo. ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta? | Perfect. I'll take it. Can I pay by card? |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¿Le puedo ayudar? | Can I help you? |
| Busco... | I'm looking for... |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta / cuestan? | How much does it / do they cost? |
| ¿Me lo/la puedo probar? | Can I try it on? |
| ¿Qué talla usa? | What size are you? |
| Me queda grande / pequeño. | It's too big / small on me. |
| ¿Lo tiene en otro color? | Do you have it in another colour? |
| Me lo/la llevo. | I'll take it. |
| ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta / en efectivo? | Can I pay by card / in cash? |
| Está de rebajas. | It's on sale. |
| ciento veinte, doscientos, quinientos | 120, 200, 500 |

## Say it right

**Rhythm.** Spanish gives each syllable roughly the same length, like a machine gun: *la-ne-gra-es-más-lar-ga*. English (and Arabic) stretch stressed syllables and squeeze the others. Try tapping the table once per syllable while you read the dialogue: one tap, one syllable, even speed. Don't swallow any vowel.
