# Level check: can you do A2?

Read each line and answer honestly: *yes*, *almost*, or *not yet*.

## I can...

- tell what I did yesterday, last weekend or on a trip, with regular and irregular verbs;
- describe how things were and what I used to do as a child;
- tell a short story, mixing the background (imperfect) and the events (preterite);
- compare things and people, and buy clothes or other things in a shop;
- explain what's wrong with me to a doctor and understand the advice;
- give simple advice and instructions;
- book a room or a table by phone and spell my name;
- talk about experiences (*he estado, nunca he...*) and make predictions;
- understand the main point of clear, everyday conversations and short videos on familiar topics.

For each *not yet*, go back to that unit, listen again, and redo the voice note.

## Final tasks

1. **Listening:** watch three Intermediate videos on [Dreaming Spanish](https://www.dreaming.com/) and one [Easy Spanish](https://www.youtube.com/@EasySpanish) episode without subtitles. Summarise each in two sentences in Spanish.
2. **Speaking:** record a **3-minute** voice note: the story of an important day in your life (background and events), how your life is different now, and a plan or prediction for the future.
3. **Writing:** write about 120 words about the best trip you've ever taken.

## What next?

Move on to **Spanish B1**, where you'll give opinions, use the subjunctive, talk about hypothetical situations and handle work and formal situations. Keep up 20 to 30 minutes of listening a day: at this level it makes the biggest difference.
