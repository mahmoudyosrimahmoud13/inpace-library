# Telling the story of a trip

Yusuf went to Granada. The most common verbs are irregular in the preterite: fui, hice, tuve, estuve...

## Listen first: the conversation

**Back from Granada**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carmen:** ¿Adónde fuiste en vacaciones? | Where did you go on holiday? |
| **Yusuf:** Fui a Granada con mi mujer. Estuvimos cuatro días. | I went to Granada with my wife. We were there four days. |
| **Carmen:** ¡Qué envidia! ¿Qué tal fue? | I'm so jealous! How was it? |
| **Yusuf:** Fue increíble. El primer día vimos la Alhambra. Tuvimos que reservar las entradas con un mes de antelación. | It was amazing. On the first day we saw the Alhambra. We had to book the tickets a month in advance. |
| **Carmen:** ¿Y qué más hicisteis? | And what else did you do? |
| **Yusuf:** Paseamos por el Albaicín, tomamos té en una tetería y una noche fuimos a un espectáculo de flamenco. | We walked around the Albaicín, had tea in a tea house and one night went to a flamenco show. |
| **Carmen:** ¿Hizo buen tiempo? | Was the weather good? |
| **Yusuf:** Sí, hizo sol todos los días. Solo tuvimos un problema: ¡no pude dormir por el calor! | Yes, it was sunny every day. We only had one problem: I couldn't sleep because of the heat! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¿Adónde fuiste? | Where did you go? |
| Fui a... con... | I went to... with... |
| Estuvimos cuatro días. | We were there four days. |
| ¿Qué tal fue? Fue increíble. | How was it? It was amazing. |
| Tuvimos que... | We had to... |
| reservar las entradas / el hotel | book the tickets / the hotel |
| ¿Hizo buen tiempo? | Was the weather good? |
| Hizo sol / frío / calor. | It was sunny / cold / hot. |
| No pude dormir. | I couldn't sleep. |
| ¡Qué envidia! | I'm so jealous! (friendly) |
| el primer día / el último día | the first day / the last day |

## Say it right

**Linking.** Spanish speakers join words: when one word ends in a vowel and the next starts with a vowel, they flow together into one syllable. *Fue a la Alhambra* sounds like "fuea-laal-ham-bra". *Fui a Granada* → "fuia-gra-na-da". This is why fast Spanish sounds like one long word.

Practise reading the dialogue lines with linking: *Estuvimos en el hotel*, *tomamos un té*, *la entrada*.
