# Booking a room and talking about experiences

Yusuf phones a hotel in Valencia, then chats with Carmen about places they've been. Listen for he estado, has visitado: the present perfect.

## Listen first: the conversation

**A phone call and a chat**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Recepción:** Hotel Mediterráneo, buenas tardes. ¿Dígame? | Hotel Mediterráneo, good afternoon. Hello? |
| **Yusuf:** Buenas tardes. Quería reservar una habitación doble para el fin de semana del quince. | Good afternoon. I'd like to book a double room for the weekend of the fifteenth. |
| **Recepción:** ¿Para cuántas noches? | For how many nights? |
| **Yusuf:** Para dos noches, el viernes y el sábado. ¿El desayuno está incluido? | For two nights, Friday and Saturday. Is breakfast included? |
| **Recepción:** Sí. Son ciento cuarenta euros por noche. ¿A nombre de quién? | Yes. It's a hundred and forty euros a night. In whose name? |
| **Yusuf:** Yusuf Haddad. Le deletreo el apellido: hache, a, de, de, a, de. | Yusuf Haddad. Let me spell the surname: H, A, D, D, A, D. |
| **Carmen:** ¿Vas a Valencia? ¿Has estado allí antes? | You're going to Valencia? Have you been there before? |
| **Yusuf:** No, nunca he estado. ¿Y tú? | No, I've never been. And you? |
| **Carmen:** Sí, he ido tres veces. Tienes que probar la paella. Te encantará. | Yes, I've been three times. You have to try the paella. You'll love it. |
| **Yusuf:** Seguro que sí. ¡Este año he viajado más que nunca! | I'm sure I will. This year I've travelled more than ever! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¿Dígame? / ¿Sí? | Hello? (answering the phone) |
| Quería reservar... | I'd like to book... |
| una habitación doble / individual | a double / single room |
| ¿Para cuántas noches? | For how many nights? |
| ¿Está incluido el desayuno? | Is breakfast included? |

## Say it right

**The alphabet, for spelling on the phone:** a, be, ce, de, e, efe, ge, hache, i, jota, ka, ele, eme, ene, eñe, o, pe, cu, erre, ese, te, u, uve, uve doble, equis, i griega (or ye), zeta. Spell your name and surname aloud three times. Phone numbers are often read in pairs: 6 12 34 56 78 → *seis, doce, treinta y cuatro, cincuenta y seis, setenta y ocho*.
