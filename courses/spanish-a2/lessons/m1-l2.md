# What did you do?

Monday morning at work. Carmen and Yusuf talk about their weekend. Notice the new verb endings: -é, -aste, -ó.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Monday at the office**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carmen:** ¡Buenos días, Yusuf! ¿Qué tal el fin de semana? | Morning, Yusuf! How was the weekend? |
| **Yusuf:** Muy bien. El sábado visité a mi primo en Toledo. Paseamos por el centro y comimos en un restaurante muy bueno. | Very good. On Saturday I visited my cousin in Toledo. We walked around the centre and ate in a very good restaurant. |
| **Carmen:** ¡Qué bonito! ¿Y el domingo? | How lovely! And on Sunday? |
| **Yusuf:** El domingo me quedé en casa. Limpié, cociné y por la noche vi una serie. ¿Y tú? | On Sunday I stayed at home. I cleaned, cooked and in the evening watched a series. And you? |
| **Carmen:** Yo trabajé el sábado por la mañana. Luego salí con unas amigas y bailamos hasta las tres. | I worked on Saturday morning. Then I went out with some friends and we danced until three. |
| **Yusuf:** ¡Hasta las tres! ¿Y a qué hora te levantaste el domingo? | Until three! And what time did you get up on Sunday? |
| **Carmen:** A las doce. ¡No hice nada en todo el día! | At twelve. I didn't do anything all day! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué tal el fin de semana? | How was the weekend? |
| ¿Qué hiciste? | What did you do? |
| No hice nada. | I didn't do anything. |
| ayer / anoche | yesterday / last night |
| el sábado pasado / la semana pasada | last Saturday / last week |
| hace dos días | two days ago |
| Me quedé en casa. | I stayed at home. |
| Salí con unos amigos. | I went out with some friends. |
| Lo pasé muy bien / fatal. | I had a great / awful time. |
| luego / después | then / afterwards |
| ¡Qué bonito! / ¡Qué pena! | How lovely! / What a pity! |

## Say it right

The stress now changes the tense: **hablo** (I speak, stress on *HA*) vs **habló** (he spoke, stress on *LÓ*); **trabajo** vs **trabajó**; **llegue** vs **llegué**. The written accent tells you, so read it.

Say these pairs: *visito / visitó*, *paso / pasó*, *cocino / cocinó*, *limpio / limpió*.
