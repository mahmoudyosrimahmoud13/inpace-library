# The board and the starting position

Chess is played by two people on a board of 64 squares, 8 by 8. One side has the light pieces and is called **White**; the other has the dark pieces and is called **Black**. White always moves first, then the players take turns, one move each.

## Squares have names

The columns are called **files** and have letters, **a** to **h**, from White's left to right. The rows are called **ranks** and have numbers, **1** to **8**, from White's side to Black's side. Every square has a name made of its file and rank: **e4**, **a1**, **h8**.

Two quick checks when you set up the board:

- **"Light on the right."** Each player has a light square in the bottom right corner (h1 for White, a8 for Black).
- **"Queen on her color."** The white queen starts on a light square (d1), the black queen on a dark square (d8).

## The starting position

Each side has 16 pieces: 1 king, 1 queen, 2 rooks, 2 bishops, 2 knights and 8 pawns. The back rank goes **rook, knight, bishop, queen, king, bishop, knight, rook**, and the pawns stand in front.

## The goal

You win by **checkmating** the enemy king: attacking it so that it has no way to escape. You never actually take the king. Lesson 4 explains checkmate in detail. A game can also end in a **draw** (nobody wins), or when a player **resigns** (gives up) or runs out of time.

## Some words you will meet

- **Center:** the four middle squares d4, e4, d5, e5. Pieces in the center control more of the board.
- **Kingside:** files e to h. **Queenside:** files a to d.
- **Diagonal:** a slanting line of squares of one color, like a1 to h8.

## Try it

1. On which square does the white king start? And the black queen?
2. Name the square in front of the white queen's pawn after it moves two squares.

**Answers:** 1. The white king starts on **e1**, the black queen on **d8**. 2. The queen's pawn starts on d2; two squares forward is **d4**.
