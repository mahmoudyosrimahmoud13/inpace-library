## The grammar of this unit: tener, articles and agreement

**Tener** means "to have". Spanish also uses it for **age**: *Tengo treinta años* is literally "I have thirty years". Never say *Soy treinta*.

| tener | |
|---|---|
| yo | **tengo** |
| tú | **tienes** |
| él / ella / usted | **tiene** |
| nosotros | **tenemos** |
| vosotros | **tenéis** |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **tienen** |

**Every noun is masculine or feminine.** Most nouns ending in **-o** are masculine, most ending in **-a** are feminine. The word for "the" and "a" changes with them:

| | masculine | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| the (one) | **el** hermano | **la** hermana |
| the (more) | **los** hermanos | **las** hermanas |
| a / some | **un** perro, **unos** perros | **una** casa, **unas** casas |

**Adjectives agree** with the noun: *mi padre es alto*, *mi madre es alta*, *mis hermanos son altos*. Adjectives ending in **-e** or a consonant don't change for gender: *inteligente*, *joven*.

**My, your, his:** *mi / mis*, *tu / tus*, *su / sus* (his, her, their, your polite): *mi hermana, mis hermanas*.

**Numbers 0 to 20:** cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once, doce, trece, catorce, quince, dieciséis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve, veinte. Then: 30 treinta, 40 cuarenta, 50 cincuenta.

## Speak: your voice note

Record a 45-second voice note about your family, or a family you know: who they are, their names, ages, jobs, and one adjective for each.

Record it in inPace as a voice note (or on your phone). Listen back once, fix one thing, and record it again. Saying the same task twice makes the second try faster and more accurate.

A model answer (yours can be different):

> En mi familia somos cinco. Mi padre se llama Karim, tiene cincuenta y dos años y es profesor. Mi madre se llama Hala; es muy simpática. Tengo una hermana y un hermano. Mi hermana tiene veinte años y es estudiante. Mi hermano es alto y un poco tímido.
