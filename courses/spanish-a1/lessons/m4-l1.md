# Asking the way

Omar needs a pharmacy and asks a woman in the street. He uses usted because she's a stranger.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Where's the pharmacy?**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Omar:** Perdone, ¿hay una farmacia por aquí? | Excuse me, is there a pharmacy around here? |
| **Señora:** Sí, hay una en la calle Mayor. | Yes, there's one on Calle Mayor. |
| **Omar:** ¿Está lejos? | Is it far? |
| **Señora:** No, está muy cerca, a cinco minutos. Siga todo recto y gire a la derecha en el semáforo. | No, it's very close, five minutes away. Go straight on and turn right at the traffic lights. |
| **Omar:** Todo recto y a la derecha en el semáforo... | Straight on and right at the lights... |
| **Señora:** Eso es. La farmacia está al lado del banco, enfrente del parque. | That's it. The pharmacy is next to the bank, opposite the park. |
| **Omar:** Muchas gracias, señora. | Thank you very much, madam. |
| **Señora:** De nada. ¡Que tenga un buen día! | You're welcome. Have a nice day! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Perdone / Perdona | Excuse me (polite / friendly) |
| ¿Hay un / una... por aquí? | Is there a... around here? |
| ¿Dónde está...? | Where is...? |
| Está cerca / lejos. | It's near / far. |
| todo recto | straight on |
| a la derecha / a la izquierda | on the right / on the left |
| Gire a la derecha. | Turn right. |
| al lado de | next to |
| enfrente de | opposite |
| entre... y... | between... and... |
| a cinco minutos | five minutes away |
| la estación / el banco / el supermercado | the station / the bank / the supermarket |

## Say it right

**c, z and g** change sound depending on the next vowel:

- **ca, co, cu** = *k*: *casa*, *cosa*, *cuatro*. **que, qui** = *k* too (the u is silent): *queso*, *aquí*.
- **ce, ci** and **z**: in most of Spain like English *th* in *think*: *cerca*, *cinco*, *plaza*; in Latin America and parts of southern Spain like *s*. Both are correct.
- **ga, go, gu** = *g* as in *go*: *gato*. **ge, gi** = the rough *j* sound: *gente*, *gimnasio*.

Say: *cerca, cinco, aquí, queso, gente, gire, izquierda*.
