# Making plans for the weekend

Lucía invites Omar to the cinema. Listen for voy a, vas a: that's how Spanish talks about plans.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Are you coming?**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lucía:** ¿Qué vas a hacer el fin de semana? | What are you going to do at the weekend? |
| **Omar:** El sábado por la mañana voy a visitar a unos amigos. ¿Y tú? | On Saturday morning I'm going to visit some friends. And you? |
| **Lucía:** Voy a ir al cine con mi compañera de piso. ¿Quieres venir? | I'm going to the cinema with my flatmate. Do you want to come? |
| **Omar:** ¡Sí, claro! ¿A qué hora? | Yes, of course! At what time? |
| **Lucía:** La película empieza a las ocho. Podemos quedar a las siete y media en la plaza. | The film starts at eight. We can meet at half past seven in the square. |
| **Omar:** Perfecto. ¿Y el domingo? | Perfect. And on Sunday? |
| **Lucía:** El domingo voy a descansar. ¡Estoy muy cansada! | On Sunday I'm going to rest. I'm very tired! |
| **Omar:** Buena idea. Pues nos vemos el sábado. | Good idea. See you on Saturday, then. |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| el fin de semana | the weekend |
| ¿Qué vas a hacer? | What are you going to do? |
| Voy a + verbo | I'm going to + verb |
| ¿Quieres venir? | Do you want to come? |
| ¡Claro! / ¡Vale! | Of course! / OK! |
| No puedo, lo siento. | I can't, sorry. |
| quedar | to meet up (with friends) |
| ¿A qué hora quedamos? | What time shall we meet? |
| Nos vemos el sábado. | See you on Saturday. |
| hoy / mañana / esta noche | today / tomorrow / tonight |
| lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves, viernes, sábado, domingo | Monday to Sunday |
| la semana que viene | next week |

## Say it right

**Intonation.** In yes/no questions your voice **rises** at the end: *¿Quieres venir?* ↗. In questions with a question word (*qué, dónde, cuándo*) it usually **falls**: *¿Qué vas a hacer?* ↘. Statements fall too: *Voy a descansar.* ↘

The word order is often the same in a statement and a question: *Quieres venir* (you want to come) vs *¿Quieres venir?* Only the melody (and the ¿? in writing) shows the difference. Practise both.
