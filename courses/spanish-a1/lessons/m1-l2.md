# Meeting someone new

Omar, from Cairo, meets Lucía at a language exchange in Madrid. Read the conversation with the meaning next to it. Don't translate word by word: get the idea, then look at how it's said.

## Listen first: the conversation

**At a language exchange**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lucía:** ¡Hola! ¿Qué tal? | Hi! How are you? |
| **Omar:** Muy bien, ¿y tú? | Very well, and you? |
| **Lucía:** Bien, gracias. Me llamo Lucía. ¿Cómo te llamas? | Fine, thanks. My name is Lucía. What's your name? |
| **Omar:** Me llamo Omar. Encantado. | My name is Omar. Nice to meet you. |
| **Lucía:** Encantada. ¿De dónde eres, Omar? | Nice to meet you. Where are you from, Omar? |
| **Omar:** Soy de Egipto, de El Cairo. ¿Y tú? | I'm from Egypt, from Cairo. And you? |
| **Lucía:** Soy de México, pero vivo aquí, en Madrid. | I'm from Mexico, but I live here, in Madrid. |
| **Omar:** ¡Qué bien! Yo también vivo en Madrid. Soy ingeniero. | Great! I live in Madrid too. I'm an engineer. |
| **Lucía:** Yo soy profesora de inglés. Bueno, ¡hasta luego! | I'm an English teacher. Well, see you later! |
| **Omar:** ¡Hasta luego, Lucía! | See you later, Lucía! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Say it right

Spanish has only **five vowel sounds**, and they never change: **a** (as in *father*), **e** (as in *met*), **i** (as in *machine*), **o** (as in *more*, but short), **u** (as in *rule*). Keep them short and pure: *me llamo* is "meh YAH-moh", not "may YAH-moe".

- **h** is always silent: *hola* = "OH-la", *hasta* = "AS-ta".
- **ll** sounds like the *y* in *yes*: *me llamo*.
- **ñ** is like *ny* in *canyon*: *España*.
- **j** is a strong, rough *h* from the throat: *José*, *Japón*.

Say these five times, slowly then fast: *Hola, me llamo Omar. Soy de Egipto. Encantado.*
