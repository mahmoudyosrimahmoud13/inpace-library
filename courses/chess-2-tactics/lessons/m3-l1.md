# Removing the defender, deflection and overloading

These tactics attack the **defenders** instead of the target.

## Removing the defender

If a piece or a square is defended only once, **capture or chase away the defender**, then take the target.

```
8  . . . . . . k .
7  . . . . . p p p
6  . . . . . n . .
5  . . . p . . B .
4  . . . . . . . .
3  . . N . . . . .
2  . . . . . P P P
1  . . . . . . K .
   a b c d e f g h
```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/6k1/5ppp/5n2/3p2B1/8/2N5/5PPP/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

The pawn on d5 is attacked by the knight on c3 and defended only by the knight on f6. **Bxf6** removes the defender (gxf6), and then **Nxd5** wins the pawn. Black's kingside pawns are also damaged.

## Deflection

**Deflection** forces a defending piece to leave its post, usually with a sacrifice it must accept.

```
8  . . r . . . k .
7  . . . . . p p p
6  . . . . . . . .
5  . . . . . . . .
4  . . . Q . . . .
3  . . . . . . . .
2  . q . . . P P P
1  . . . R . . K .
   a b c d e f g h
```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/2r3k1/5ppp/8/8/3Q4/8/1q3PPP/3R2K1_w_-_-_0_1)

Black's rook on c8 guards the back rank. **Qd8+!** offers the queen: the rook must take (**Rxd8**), and now nothing guards the back rank: **Rxd8#**. White gave the queen and won the game.

## Decoy

A **decoy** lures a piece (often the king) onto a bad square, where a fork or mate follows. For example, a rook sacrifice on h8 that drags the king to h8, followed by a queen check that wins.

## Overloading

A piece is **overloaded** when it has two jobs and can only do one. Attack one of its duties; when it reacts, the other duty falls.

Example: a black queen on d7 defends both the rook on d8 and the knight on f5. White takes the knight: if the queen recaptures, the rook on d8 is lost; if not, Black is simply a piece down.

## How to find them

Ask about every enemy piece: **"What is it defending?"** If the answer is "something important, and only it", that defender is the real target.

## Try it

Black's king is on g8 behind pawns f7, g7, h7. Black's only defender of the back rank is a rook on e8, and it also has to protect a knight on e5 that your queen attacks. White has a rook on e1. What do you try?

**Answer:** **Rxe5!** If the rook on e8 recaptures (...Rxe5), it leaves the back rank, and your queen or rook may mate there; if it doesn't, you've won a knight. The rook on e8 was **overloaded**.
