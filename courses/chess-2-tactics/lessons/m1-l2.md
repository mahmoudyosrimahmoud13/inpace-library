# Forks: one piece, two targets

A **fork** is one piece attacking two (or more) enemy pieces at the same time. The opponent can only save one.

## The knight fork

Knights are the best forkers, because their L-shaped move is hard to see, and they can attack a queen or a rook without being attacked back.

![Chess board](board-39a05dc25d.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/r3kb1r/pp3ppp/8/3N4/8/8/PPP2PPP/R3K2R_w_KQkq_-_0_1)

White plays **Nc7+ (knight to c7, check)**: check to the king on e8 and an attack on the rook on a8. The king must move, and White takes the rook. A fork with check is especially strong, because the opponent has no time to do anything else.

A fork of king and queen is called a **royal fork**. A fork of king, queen and rook is a **family fork**.

## The pawn fork

A pawn that moves forward attacks two squares diagonally. If two enemy pieces stand on those squares, the pawn wins one.

![Chess board](board-d8d4c70d28.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/4k3/8/3n1n2/8/4P3/8/8/4K3_w_-_-_0_1)

**e5!** attacks both knights. Neither knight can take the pawn, so White wins a knight for a pawn.

## The queen fork

The queen moves in eight directions, so it often attacks two things at once. Checks are the most common way.

![Chess board](board-2a54f92e0d.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/4k3/pp3ppp/8/8/1b6/8/PP3PPP/3Q2K1_w_-_-_0_1)

**Qa4+ (queen to a4, check)** checks the king along the diagonal and attacks the undefended bishop on b4. After the king moves (or Black blocks with ...b5), **Qxb4 (queen takes on b4)** wins the bishop. Notice how the loose bishop made the fork possible.

## How to find forks

1. Find enemy pieces that are **undefended**, or more valuable than your attacker, and the **king**.
2. Ask: is there a square from which one of my pieces attacks two of them?
3. Check that square is **safe** (or that the fork is with check).

## Try it

![Chess board](board-55d56dc8a6.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/4q1k1/5p1p/6p1/3N4/8/8/5PPP/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move. Win the queen.

**Answer:** **Nf6+ (knight to f6, check)**: the knight checks the king on g8 and attacks the queen on e8. After the king moves, **Nxe8 (knight takes on e8)**.
