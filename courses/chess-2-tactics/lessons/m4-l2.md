# Practice set: six puzzles

Solve each one **without moving the pieces**. Then check the answer below it.

## Puzzle 1

![Chess board](board-7a6cdca624.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/4R1K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and mate.

**Answer:** **Re8# (rook to e8, checkmate)**. Back-rank mate.

## Puzzle 2

![Chess board](board-55d56dc8a6.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/4q1k1/5p1p/6p1/3N4/8/8/5PPP/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win material.

**Answer:** **Nf6+ (knight to f6, check)** forks the king and the queen. After the king moves, **Nxe8 (knight takes on e8)**.

## Puzzle 3

![Chess board](board-5ac4d996bc.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/q7/8/2k5/8/8/8/4B3/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win material.

**Answer:** **Bf3+ (bishop to f3, check)**, a skewer on the long diagonal, then **Bxa8 (bishop takes on a8)**.

## Puzzle 4

![Chess board](board-4fa08bef49.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/3q2k1/5ppp/8/3B4/8/8/5PPP/3R2K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win material.

**Answer:** **Bxf7+ (bishop takes on f7, check)**, a discovered attack: the bishop gives check and uncovers the rook on the queen. **Kxf7 (king takes on f7) Rxd8 (rook takes on d8)**.

## Puzzle 5

![Chess board](board-07f3ee564d.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/r7/1P4k1/8/8/8/8/8/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win.

**Answer:** **bxa8=Q (pawn takes on a8 and becomes a queen)**: the pawn captures the rook and promotes at the same time. Don't play b8=Q?, because ...Rxb8 takes it.

## Puzzle 6

![Chess board](board-be602b56a7.png)

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess to play it out](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/5r1k/6pp/7N/q2Q4/8/8/6PP/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and mate in two.

**Answer:** **Qg8+! (queen to g8, check) Rxg8 (rook takes on g8) Nf7# (knight to f7, checkmate)**, the smothered mate.

## What's next

Tactics improve with steady practice, not marathons. Aim for **15 to 20 puzzles a day**, five days a week, and review the ones you missed. The third course turns to openings, plans and endgames.

## Your practice

Do 20 Lichess puzzles a day for a week and write your puzzle rating at the start and the end.
