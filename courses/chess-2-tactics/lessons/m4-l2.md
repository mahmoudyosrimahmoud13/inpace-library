# Practice set: six puzzles

Solve each one **without moving the pieces**. Then check the answer below it.

## Puzzle 1

```
8  . . . . . . k .
7  . . . . . p p p
6  . . . . . . . .
5  . . . . . . . .
4  . . . . . . . .
3  . . . . . . . .
2  . . . . . P P P
1  . . . . R . K .
   a b c d e f g h
```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/4R1K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and mate.

**Answer:** **Re8#**. Back-rank mate.

## Puzzle 2

5  . . . N . . . .
4  . . . . . . . .
3  . . . . . . . .
2  . . . . . P P P

```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/4q1k1/5p1p/6p1/3N4/8/8/5PPP/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win material.

**Answer:** **Nf6+** forks the king and the queen. After the king moves, **Nxe8**.

## Puzzle 4

```
8  . . . q . . k .
7  . . . . . p p p
6  . . . . . . . .
5  . . . B . . . .
4  . . . . . . . .
3  . . . . . . . .
2  . . . . . P P P
1  . . . R . . K .
   a b c d e f g h
```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/3q2k1/5ppp/8/3B4/8/8/5PPP/3R2K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win material.

**Answer:** **Bxf7+**, a discovered attack: the bishop gives check and uncovers the rook on the queen. **Kxf7 Rxd8**.

## Puzzle 5

8  r . . . . . . .
7  . P . . . . k .

5  . . . . . . . .
4  . . . . . . . .
3  . . . . . . . .

```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/r7/1P4k1/8/8/8/8/8/6K1_w_-_-_0_1)

White to move and win.

**Answer:** **bxa8=Q**: the pawn captures the rook and promotes at the same time. Don't play b8=Q?, because ...Rxb8 takes it.

## What's next

Tactics improve with steady practice, not marathons. Aim for **15 to 20 puzzles a day**, five days a week, and review the ones you missed. The third course turns to openings, plans and endgames.

## Your practice

Do 20 Lichess puzzles a day for a week and write your puzzle rating at the start and the end.
