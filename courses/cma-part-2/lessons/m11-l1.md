# Final review: formula sheet and exam strategy

## Formula sheet

**Time value of money**
- FV = PV(1 + r)ⁿ; PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ
- PV annuity = PMT × [1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ] ÷ r; perpetuity = PMT ÷ r
- Effective rate = (1 + i/m)ᵐ − 1

**Section A**
- Current, quick, cash ratios; debt/equity; times interest earned = EBIT ÷ interest
- DSO = 365 ÷ (sales ÷ avg AR); days inventory = 365 ÷ (COGS ÷ avg inventory)
- Cash conversion cycle = days inventory + DSO − DPO
- ROE = net margin × asset turnover × equity multiplier
- EPS = (NI − pref. div.) ÷ weighted avg shares; P/E = price ÷ EPS
- Sustainable growth = ROE × (1 − payout)
- DFL = EBIT ÷ (EBIT − interest); DTL = DOL × DFL

**Section B**
- CAPM: k = rf + β(rm − rf)
- Gordon: P₀ = D₁ ÷ (k − g); preferred = D ÷ k
- kd after tax = yield × (1 − t)
- WACC = Σ weight × cost; break point = amount ÷ weight
- EOQ = √(2DS ÷ H); reorder point = daily usage × lead time + safety stock
- Cost of skipping a discount = [d ÷ (1 − d)] × [365 ÷ (pay day − discount day)]
- Effective loan rate = interest ÷ usable funds

**Section C**
- Break-even units = FC ÷ CM per unit; sales = FC ÷ CM ratio
- Target units = (FC + pre-tax target) ÷ CM; pre-tax = after-tax ÷ (1 − t)
- Elasticity = %ΔQ ÷ %ΔP
- Target cost = target price − target profit
- EV = Σ outcome × probability; EVPI = EV(perfect info) − EV(best action)

**Section E**
- Operating CF = (R − cash costs)(1 − t) + depreciation × t
- NPV = PV inflows − investment; PI = PV inflows ÷ investment
- Payback = years until cumulative cash flow = investment

**Section F**
- IMA: principles HFOR (Honesty, Fairness, Objectivity, Responsibility); standards CCIC (Competence, Confidentiality, Integrity, Credibility)
- Resolution: policy → supervisor (or next level) → ethics counselor → attorney → resign
- Fraud triangle: pressure, opportunity, rationalization

## Exam-day strategy

**Multiple choice (3 hours, 100 questions)**
- About 1.8 minutes each. Mark hard ones and move on; come back at the end.
- Read the **last line first** to know what's being asked, then the data.
- Eliminate obviously wrong options; many wrong answers are the result of a common mistake (forgetting tax, using D₀ instead of D₁, using the coupon instead of the yield).
- No penalty for wrong answers: never leave a question blank.

**CBQs (2 cases, about 30 minutes each)**
- Skim the questions before reading the case.
- Show formulas and workings: partial credit counts.
- For "explain" or "recommend" parts, give the conclusion first, then two or three reasons tied to the case's numbers.
- Watch the clock: don't spend 45 minutes on the first case.
- Remember the gate: you need at least 50% of the MCQ points for CBQs to count, so MCQs come first in your preparation.

## Your last two weeks

1. Two full **timed mock exams** under real conditions.
2. Review every wrong answer and update your **error log**.
3. Re-drill the weakest section (most people: Section B and capital budgeting cash flows).
4. Read the IMA Statement one more time.
5. Sleep well before the exam. Tired brains make arithmetic errors.

## After passing

With Parts 1 and 2 passed, a bachelor's degree, two years of relevant experience and IMA membership, you earn the **CMA** designation. Keep it with 30 hours of continuing education a year (including 2 in ethics).

Good luck.
