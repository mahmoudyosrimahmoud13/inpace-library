# Start here: CMA Part 2 and how to pass it

**CMA Part 2: Strategic Financial Management** is the second exam of the US CMA (Certified Management Accountant) from the IMA. Part 1 is about planning, performance and control inside the business; Part 2 is about **analyzing, financing and deciding**.

## The exam (2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| Multiple-choice | 100 questions in 3 hours |
| Case-based questions (CBQs) | 2 sets, about 30 minutes each (they replaced the essays) |
| Total time | 4 hours |
| Score | 0 to 500; **360 to pass** |
| Weight | MCQs about 75%, CBQs about 25% |
| Gate | You must earn at least 50% of the MCQ points for your CBQs to be scored |
| Windows | January–February, May–June, September–October |

Always check the current rules on the IMA website before you book: they change from time to time.

## What you should know before starting

- Financial statements and ratios at the level of the library course **Financial statements: read them, build them, analyze them**.
- CVP, relevant costs and budgets at the level of **Cost and management accounting**, and of **CMA Part 1**.
- A financial calculator or Excel for time value of money (next lesson).

About **12 to 15 hours a week**. Most candidates need 150 to 170 hours for Part 2.

## How to study for a CMA exam

1. **Learn the concept**, then **do questions immediately**. Reading alone is the most common reason people fail.
2. Keep an **error log**: every question you miss, why, and the rule you forgot. Review it weekly.
3. **Mix topics** in later weeks: the exam doesn't tell you which section a question is from.
4. **Time yourself**: about 1.8 minutes per MCQ. Skip and come back rather than getting stuck.
5. For CBQs, practice **reading a case and pulling numbers out of tables** quickly. Show the formula, not just the answer.

## How each lesson here works

Each lesson gives the concept, the formulas, a worked example, and exam traps. Each module ends with a quiz in CMA style. Do the module's practical task in Excel: it builds the speed you need for CBQs.
