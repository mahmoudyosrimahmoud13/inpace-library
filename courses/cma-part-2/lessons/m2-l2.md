# Section A: profitability, DuPont and market ratios

## Profitability

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gross margin | Gross profit ÷ Net sales |
| Operating margin | Operating income (EBIT) ÷ Net sales |
| Net margin | Net income ÷ Net sales |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA ÷ Net sales |
| Return on assets (ROA) | Net income ÷ Average total assets |
| Return on equity (ROE) | Net income ÷ Average common equity |

For ROE to **common** shareholders, subtract preferred dividends from net income and use common equity only.

## DuPont

> **ROA = Net margin × Asset turnover**
> **ROE = Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier**

Example: net income 90, sales 1,000, average assets 600, average equity 300.
- Net margin 9%; asset turnover 1.67; equity multiplier 2.0
- ROA = 9% × 1.67 = **15%**; ROE = 15% × 2.0 = **30%**

The **extended (five-step) DuPont** splits net margin further: ROE = (NI/EBT) tax burden × (EBT/EBIT) interest burden × (EBIT/Sales) × (Sales/Assets) × (Assets/Equity). Use it to see whether ROE comes from operations, low interest, low tax or high leverage.

## Market ratios

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Earnings per share (basic) | (Net income − Preferred dividends) ÷ Weighted average common shares |
| Price/earnings (P/E) | Market price per share ÷ EPS |
| Earnings yield | EPS ÷ Price (the inverse of P/E) |
| Dividend payout | Dividends per share ÷ EPS |
| Dividend yield | Dividends per share ÷ Price |
| Book value per share | Common equity ÷ Common shares outstanding |
| Market-to-book (price-to-book) | Price ÷ Book value per share |

### Weighted average shares, the classic trap

1,000,000 shares on January 1; 200,000 issued on April 1; 100,000 bought back on October 1.

Weighted average = 1,000,000 + 200,000 × 9/12 − 100,000 × 3/12 = 1,000,000 + 150,000 − 25,000 = **1,125,000**.

**Stock dividends and splits** are applied **retroactively** to the start of the period (and to comparative years), not weighted.

**Diluted EPS** includes potential shares (convertible bonds and preferred, options, warrants) if they would lower EPS. For convertible bonds, add back the after-tax interest to the numerator and add the shares to the denominator.

### Example

Net income 2,500,000; preferred dividends 250,000; weighted shares 1,125,000; price 40; dividends per share 0.80.

- EPS = 2,250,000 ÷ 1,125,000 = **2.00**
- P/E = 40 ÷ 2 = **20**
- Payout = 0.80 ÷ 2 = **40%**; dividend yield = 0.80 ÷ 40 = **2%**

## Growth from retention

> **Sustainable growth rate = ROE × Retention ratio** (retention = 1 − payout)

ROE 15%, payout 40% → g = 15% × 0.6 = **9%**: how fast the company can grow without new equity or more leverage.

## Common-size and trend analysis

- **Vertical (common-size):** each line as a % of sales (income statement) or total assets (balance sheet). Compares companies of different sizes.
- **Horizontal (trend):** each line as a % change from a base year.

## Try it

Net income 600, preferred dividends 100, weighted shares 250, price 30, dividends per common share 0.60. Find EPS, P/E, payout and dividend yield. If ROE is 12%, what is the sustainable growth rate?

**Answers:** EPS **2.00**; P/E **15**; payout **30%**; yield **2%**; g = 12% × 0.70 = **8.4%**.
