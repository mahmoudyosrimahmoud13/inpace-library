# اختبار تجريبي A2

## القراءة

*Dear Hana,*
*I'm writing from Hurghada! We arrived on Tuesday and we've already been to the beach three times. The hotel is
bigger than the one we stayed in last year, but it isn't as quiet. Yesterday, while we were snorkelling, we saw a
turtle! Tomorrow we're going to visit the desert. Dad says we have to leave at 5 in the morning, so I'll probably sleep
in the car. If the weather is good, we'll stay until Sunday.*
*Love, Salma*

1. When did Salma arrive? 2. How is this hotel different from last year's? 3. What happened while they were snorkelling?
4. Why will Salma probably sleep in the car? 5. What will they do if the weather is good?

## الكتابة (80 كلمة)

اكتب إيميلًا لصديق عن رحلة حقيقية أو متخيلة: ماذا فعلت، ماذا رأيت، خطتك للأيام القادمة.

## الإجابات

1. On Tuesday. 2. It's bigger, but it isn't as quiet. 3. They saw a turtle. 4. Because they have to leave at 5 in the
morning. 5. They'll stay until Sunday.
