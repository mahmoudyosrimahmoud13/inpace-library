# الاحتمال والطلبات

## might / may: ممكن

```text
I might go to the party. I'm not sure.
It may rain later.
She might not come.
```

## الطلبات المهذبة

```text
Can you help me?            (عادي)
Could you help me, please?  (أكثر أدبًا)
Would you mind opening the window?   (أكثر أدبًا، وبعدها ing)
Can I / Could I / May I use your phone?
```

## العروض والاقتراحات

```text
Shall I carry your bag?      Would you like some tea?
Let's go to the beach.       Why don't we take a taxi?
```
