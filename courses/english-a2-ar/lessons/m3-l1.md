# أكبر من، الأكبر

| الصفة | المقارنة | التفضيل |
|---|---|---|
| قصيرة: cheap | cheap**er** than | the cheap**est** |
| تنتهي بـ e: nice | nic**er** | the nic**est** |
| ساكن-علة-ساكن: big | big**ger** | the big**gest** |
| تنتهي بـ y: easy | eas**ier** | the eas**iest** |
| طويلة: expensive | **more** expensive | the **most** expensive |
| شاذة: good / bad / far | better / worse / further | the best / the worst / the furthest |

```text
Cairo is bigger than Alexandria.        This phone is more expensive than that one.
It's the best restaurant in town.       Today is worse than yesterday.
```

أخطاء شائعة: more bigger ❌، the most cheapest ❌، better from ❌ (استخدم than).
