# المضارع التام أم الماضي البسيط؟

| Present perfect | Past simple |
|---|---|
| الوقت غير محدد | الوقت محدد ومنتهٍ |
| I've been to Paris. | I went to Paris **in 2019**. |
| Have you seen the new film? | Did you see it **last night**? |

مع yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago ← **past simple** دائمًا. *I have seen him yesterday* ❌

## حوار

```text
A: Have you ever been to Sharm?    B: Yes, I have.
A: When did you go?                B: I went there two years ago.
A: Did you like it?                B: Yes, it was amazing.
```

السؤال الأول عن التجربة (perfect)، والتفاصيل بالماضي البسيط.

## تمرين

1. I ___ (never / try) Indian food. 2. She ___ (buy) a new car last week. 3. ___ you ___ (finish) the report yet?
4. We ___ (just / arrive). 5. They ___ (live) in Tanta in 2015.

## الإجابات

1. 've never tried 2. bought 3. Have / finished 4. 've just arrived 5. lived
