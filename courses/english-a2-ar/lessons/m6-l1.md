# a lot of, much, many, a few, a little

| معدود (books) | غير معدود (money) |
|---|---|
| many books (في السؤال والنفي) | much money (في السؤال والنفي) |
| a lot of books | a lot of money |
| a few books (قليل وكافٍ) | a little money (قليل وكافٍ) |
| few books (قليل جدًا، سلبي) | little money (قليل جدًا، سلبي) |

```text
How many people came?      Not many.        We have a lot of time.
I don't have much time.    I have a few friends here.     Can I have a little sugar?
```
