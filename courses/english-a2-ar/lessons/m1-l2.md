# مراجعة الماضي البسيط وأسئلته

```text
I visited my grandmother last weekend.       She didn't come to the party.
Where did you go on holiday?                 We went to Dahab.
Who did you meet? / Who called you?          (who فاعل: بدون did)
```

## who و what: مع did أم بدون؟

- السؤال عن **المفعول**: *Who **did** you see?* (رأيت مَن؟)
- السؤال عن **الفاعل**: *Who **saw** you?* (مَن رآك؟) بدون did.

## تمرين

1. What time ___ you ___ (leave) work yesterday? 2. Who ___ (tell) you the news? 3. I ___ (lose) my keys last night.
