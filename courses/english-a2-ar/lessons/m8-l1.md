# رسائل، هاتف، وسفر

## إيميل قصير

```text
Hi Omar,

Thanks for your message. I'm sorry I didn't reply earlier, I was very busy at work.
I'd love to come on Saturday. What time should I be there? Shall I bring anything?

See you soon,
Mona
```
