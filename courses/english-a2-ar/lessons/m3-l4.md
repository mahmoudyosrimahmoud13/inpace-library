# صف الأشخاص والأماكن

## الأشخاص

```text
What does she look like?   She's tall and slim. She has long dark hair and brown eyes.
What is he like?           He's friendly, funny and a bit shy.   (الشخصية)
```

**look like** للشكل، **be like** للشخصية.

## الأماكن

```text
It's a lively city with old buildings, crowded streets and great food.
It's quieter than Cairo, but it isn't as cheap.
```

## تمرين

1. My car is ___ (old) than yours. 2. This is the ___ (good) film I've ever seen. 3. The exam was ___ (difficult) than I thought.
4. I'm not tall ___ to reach it. 5. What ___ your new manager like?

## الإجابات

1. older 2. best 3. more difficult 4. enough 5. is
