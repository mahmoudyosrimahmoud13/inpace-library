# احكِ قصة

الكلمات التي تربط القصة:

```text
First, ...   Then, ...   After that, ...   Suddenly, ...   In the end, ... / Finally, ...
```

## قصة نموذجية

*Last summer I was on holiday in Alexandria with my family. One evening we were walking along the Corniche when
suddenly it started to rain. We didn't have umbrellas, so we ran into a small café. While we were waiting, the owner
brought us hot tea and told us stories about the city. In the end, it was the best evening of the holiday.*

## تمرين: اختر الزمن

1. I ___ (sleep) when you ___ (call). 2. While we ___ (drive), we ___ (see) a camel. 3. What ___ you ___ (do) at 9 last night?

## الإجابات

1. was sleeping / called 2. were driving / saw 3. were / doing
