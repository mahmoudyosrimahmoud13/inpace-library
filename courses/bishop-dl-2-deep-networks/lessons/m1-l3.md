# Watch: The essential main ideas of neural networks

StatQuest shows a tiny network fitting a curved pattern of drug doses: each hidden unit bends and scales an activation
function, and the output adds the pieces together. It's section 6.2 drawn by hand, and it makes "hidden units are
learned basis functions" very concrete.

Notice that the video uses the **softplus** activation, a smooth relative of ReLU. Any non-linear activation works; what
matters is that the network can place and shape its own bends.
