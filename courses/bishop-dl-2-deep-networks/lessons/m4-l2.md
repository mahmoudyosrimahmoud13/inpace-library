# Watch: Backpropagation, intuitively

3Blue1Brown's third deep learning video explains what backpropagation *does* before any formulas: each training example
"wants" certain nudges to the weights, and those wishes flow backwards through the network. Its sequel, **Backpropagation
calculus** (www.youtube.com/watch?v=tIeHLnjs5U8), does the chain rule for a tiny network, one neuron per layer.

Watch the first, then the second if you'd like the calculus. The δ in the book is the quantity he writes as ∂C/∂z in the
second video: how sensitive the cost is to a unit's weighted input.
