# Dates and receivables aging

Dates are numbers in Excel (days since 1900), which is why you can subtract them.

## Date functions you'll use

| Formula | Gives |
|---|---|
| `=TODAY()` | Today (changes daily) |
| `=DATE(2026, 3, 31)` | A date from parts |
| `=EOMONTH(A2, 0)` | Last day of A2's month |
| `=EOMONTH(A2, -1)+1` | First day of A2's month |
| `=EDATE(A2, 3)` | Same day, 3 months later |
| `=A2 + 30` | 30 days later (due date for "net 30") |
| `=NETWORKDAYS(A2, B2)` | Working days between |
| `=YEAR(A2)`, `=MONTH(A2)`, `=TEXT(A2, "yyyy-mm")` | Parts, or a month label for grouping |

Use a fixed **report date** in an input cell instead of `TODAY()` for month-end reports, so the report doesn't change when opened tomorrow.

## Building an aging schedule

Table `Invoices`: Customer | Invoice | Date | Amount | Paid

1. **Balance:** `=[@Amount]-[@Paid]`
2. **Due date (net 30):** `=[@Date]+30`
3. **Days overdue** at the report date (`ReportDate` named cell): `=MAX(ReportDate-[@[Due date]], 0)`
4. **Bucket:**
   `=IFS([@Balance]<=0, "Paid", [@[Days overdue]]=0, "Not due", [@[Days overdue]]<=30, "1-30", [@[Days overdue]]<=60, "31-60", [@[Days overdue]]<=90, "61-90", TRUE, "Over 90")`

Or with a small bucket table (0 → Not due, 1 → 1-30, 31 → 31-60, 61 → 61-90, 91 → Over 90) and an approximate XLOOKUP: `=XLOOKUP([@[Days overdue]], Buckets[From], Buckets[Name], , -1)`. Easier to change later.

5. **Summary** by customer and bucket: a pivot (Rows: Customer, Columns: Bucket, Values: Balance), or SUMIFS:
   `=SUMIFS(Invoices[Balance], Invoices[Customer], $A2, Invoices[Bucket], B$1)`

## From aging to the allowance

Add the expected loss rate per bucket (from *Financial statements*, Day 5: 1%, 5%, 10%, 12.5%) and multiply:

`=SUMPRODUCT(BucketTotals, LossRates)`

That's the year-end allowance for doubtful accounts, with the working paper the auditor will ask for.

## Highlight what matters

Conditional formatting → Highlight cells rules:
- Over 90 days: red fill
- Balance above a credit limit (looked up per customer): orange

## Try it

Make 15 invoices across 5 customers with different dates and part payments. Build the aging with buckets, a summary by customer, and the allowance using the loss rates above. Change the report date by a month and watch invoices move between buckets.
