# Capstone: a month-end workbook

Build one workbook that does a small company's month end, using everything in this course. It's also a great portfolio piece for job interviews: bring it on a laptop.

## The company

Pick a business (or use Nour's bakery). Invent about **60 journal lines** for one month: sales on cash and credit, purchases, salaries, rent, a loan payment, an asset purchase, customer and supplier payments.

## Sheets to build

1. **Readme:** what the workbook does, inputs, how to update it.
2. **Inputs:** report date, company name, VAT rate, loss rates for aging, loan details.
3. **COA:** chart of accounts with type and statement mapping.
4. **Journal:** a Table with validation on codes, account names by XLOOKUP, and per-entry balance checks.
5. **TB:** the trial balance by SUMIFS for the month, with a check.
6. **Ledger:** pick an account, see its lines and running balance (FILTER).
7. **Aging:** customer invoices, buckets, summary and the allowance.
8. **Bank rec:** books vs bank matching with COUNTIFS, and the reconciliation statement.
9. **Assets and loan:** depreciation schedule and loan amortization; the month's depreciation and interest feed journal entries.
10. **Statements:** income statement and balance sheet from the mapped TB.
11. **Ratios and dashboard:** 5 ratios, a trend chart and slicers on a sales pivot.
12. **Budget:** next quarter's driver budget with scenarios and Goal Seek for break-even.
13. **Checks:** every check in one place, with one "ALL OK" cell.

## Quality bar

- Inputs colored; no typed numbers inside formulas.
- Every list is a Table; formulas are consistent down columns.
- All checks are 0 and the Checks sheet says ALL OK.
- Someone else can update next month by pasting new data and pressing Refresh All.

## Review yourself

Ask a friend to change one journal amount, one mapping and one bank line without telling you which. Can your checks find all three?
