# Statements and ratios from a trial balance

Most accounting systems export a trial balance. Turning it into statements quickly and reliably is a core skill.

## The mapping table

Add a **Line** column to the chart of accounts that says where each account goes:

| Code | Name | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1010 | Cash | Cash |
| 1100 | Accounts receivable | Receivables |
| 1110 | Allowance for doubtful accounts | Receivables |
| 1300 | Inventory | Inventory |
| 1500 | Equipment | PPE |
| 1510 | Accumulated depreciation | PPE |
| 2010 | Accounts payable | Payables |
| 4010 | Sales | Revenue |
| 4020 | Sales returns | Revenue |
| 5010 | Cost of goods sold | COGS |
| 6010 | Salaries | Operating expenses |

Several accounts can share a line (receivables net of the allowance).

## The statement sheet

Bring the trial balance in as a Table `TB` (Code, Debit, Credit) and add a **Balance** column, `=[@Debit]-[@Credit]` (debits positive, credits negative), and the **Line** with XLOOKUP from the mapping.

Then each statement line is one SUMIFS:

`=SUMIFS(TB[Balance], TB[Line], "Revenue")*-1`

(Multiply credit-balance lines by −1 so revenue shows positive.)

Build the income statement (Revenue, COGS, Gross profit, Operating expenses, Operating profit, ...) and the balance sheet from these lines. Checks:

- Every TB account has a Line: `=COUNTIFS(TB[Line], "")` must be 0.
- Balance sheet balances (after adding the year's profit to retained earnings).
- Total of all mapped lines = 0 (debits equal credits).

Next month, paste the new trial balance and everything updates.

## Ratios

On a `Ratios` sheet, reference the statement lines:

| Ratio | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gross margin | `=GrossProfit/Revenue` |
| Net margin | `=NetProfit/Revenue` |
| Current ratio | `=CurrentAssets/CurrentLiabilities` |
| Receivable days | `=Receivables/Revenue*365` |
| Debt to equity | `=TotalLiabilities/TotalEquity` |

Use `IFERROR(..., "-")` only for cases like zero revenue in a new company.

## A simple dashboard

- A **line chart** of monthly revenue and profit (Insert → Chart, from a month-by-month table of SUMIFS).
- **Conditional formatting** arrows or colors on ratios against targets.
- A **sparkline** per row (Insert → Sparklines) for a 12-month trend in one cell.

Keep it to one screen: 4 to 6 numbers that matter, a trend chart, and the checks.

## Try it

Take Green Valley's numbers from *Financial statements* (or any trial balance you have), build the mapping, the statements with SUMIFS, the checks and a ratios sheet. Then change one account's mapping on purpose and watch a check catch it.
