# Bank reconciliation in Excel

Every month, your Cash account and the bank statement should agree, after allowing for timing. The **bank reconciliation** proves they do, and catches errors and fraud.

## Why they differ

| Item | In your books, not yet in the bank | In the bank, not yet in your books |
|---|---|---|
| Deposits in transit | ✓ (recorded, bank hasn't credited yet) | |
| Outstanding checks | ✓ (written, not cashed yet) | |
| Bank charges, interest | | ✓ |
| Direct transfers from customers | | ✓ |
| Errors | either side | either side |

## Setting it up

Sheet `Books`: your Cash ledger lines (Date, Ref, Description, Amount: + in, − out).
Sheet `Bank`: the bank statement export (Date, Ref, Description, Amount).

Clean both first: `TRIM` descriptions, make sure amounts are numbers, same sign convention.

## Matching with COUNTIFS

In `Books`, a column **In bank?**:

`=IF(COUNTIFS(Bank[Amount], [@Amount], Bank[Ref], [@Ref])>0, "Matched", "Open")`

If references aren't reliable, match on amount and a date window:

`=IF(COUNTIFS(Bank[Amount], [@Amount], Bank[Date], ">="&[@Date]-3, Bank[Date], "<="&[@Date]+5)>0, "Matched", "Open")`

Do the same in `Bank` against `Books`. Filter "Open" on both sides: those are your reconciling items. Duplicated amounts (two payments of 500) need a human look: COUNTIFS shows 2.

## The reconciliation statement

| | |
|---|---|
| **Balance per bank statement** | 48,300 |
| Add: deposits in transit | 6,200 |
| Less: outstanding checks | (9,100) |
| **Adjusted bank balance** | **45,400** |
| **Balance per books** | 45,520 |
| Add: customer transfer not recorded | 300 |
| Less: bank charges | (120) |
| Less: error (check for 1,580 recorded as 1,280) | (300) |
| **Adjusted book balance** | **45,400** |

Check cell: adjusted bank − adjusted books = 0.

The items on the **books side** need journal entries (record the transfer, the charges, fix the error). Items on the bank side just wait to clear next month; if a "deposit in transit" is still open after weeks, investigate.

## Make it a monthly template

Inputs: statement balance and book balance; the open items list fills from the two sheets with `FILTER`:

`=FILTER(Books, Books[In bank?]="Open")`

Next month, paste the new exports and the reconciliation rebuilds itself.

## Try it

Make 15 book lines and 14 bank lines for one month, with 2 deposits in transit, 2 outstanding checks, a bank charge, a customer transfer and one transposition error. Match them with COUNTIFS and build the reconciliation statement until the check is zero. List the journal entries needed.
