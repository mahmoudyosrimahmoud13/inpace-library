# Depreciation schedules and loan tables

## Depreciation functions

The machine from *Financial statements*: cost 50,000, salvage 5,000, life 5 years.

| Method | Formula | Year 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Straight-line | `=SLN(50000, 5000, 5)` | 9,000 |
| Double-declining balance | `=DDB(50000, 5000, 5, 1)` | 20,000 |
| Fixed declining balance | `=DB(50000, 5000, 5, 1)` | 18,450 |

Build a schedule with years 1 to 5 down the rows, period in column A:

| Column | Formula |
|---|---|
| Depreciation | `=DDB(Cost, Salvage, Life, A2)` |
| Accumulated | `=SUM($B$2:B2)` |
| Book value | `=Cost-C2` |

`DDB` stops at salvage by itself. Check: total depreciation = cost − salvage.

**Monthly, for a fixed asset register:** straight-line per month = `=SLN(Cost, Salvage, Life*12)`. A register Table with one row per asset (purchase date, cost, life, salvage) and a column for this year's depreciation lets you post one journal entry for all assets.

## Loan tables

A 100,000 loan at 12% a year, repaid monthly over 3 years.

- **Monthly payment:** `=PMT(12%/12, 36, -100000)` = **3,321.43**
- Interest in month 1: `=IPMT(12%/12, 1, 36, -100000)` = **1,000.00**
- Principal in month 1: `=PPMT(12%/12, 1, 36, -100000)` = **2,321.43**

(The minus sign on the loan amount makes the payment come out positive.)

**Amortization table** (month in column A):

| Month | Opening balance | Payment | Interest | Principal | Closing balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100,000.00 | 3,321.43 | 1,000.00 | 2,321.43 | 97,678.57 |
| 2 | =F2 | =Payment | =B3*Rate/12 | =C3-D3 | =B3-E3 |

Copy down 36 rows. The last closing balance must be 0 (or a few cents from rounding). That's your check.

From this table you get:
- interest expense for each year: `=SUMIFS(D:D, Year, 2026)`
- the **current portion** of the loan at year end: principal due in the next 12 months, `=SUM` of the next 12 principal cells

Exactly the split the balance sheet needs.

## Other useful finance functions

- `=FV(rate, periods, payment, -pv)`: what savings grow to
- `=NPV(rate, flows) + initial flow` and `=IRR(flows)`: investment appraisal (the CMA course goes deep)
- `=EFFECT(12%, 12)`: the real annual rate of 12% compounded monthly (12.68%)
