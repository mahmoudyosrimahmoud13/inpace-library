# Making a plan for the weekend

Anna invites Samir to a museum. Listen for können, müssen and wollen: the second verb goes to the end.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Do you have time on Saturday?**

| German | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anna:** Samir, hast du am Samstag Zeit? Ich will ins Museum gehen. | Samir, are you free on Saturday? I want to go to the museum. |
| **Samir:** Am Samstagvormittag muss ich arbeiten. Aber am Nachmittag kann ich. | On Saturday morning I have to work. But in the afternoon I can. |
| **Anna:** Super! Das Pergamonmuseum ist bis sechs Uhr geöffnet. | Great! The Pergamon Museum is open until six. |
| **Samir:** Toll, da gibt es auch Kunst aus Syrien und dem Irak, oder? | Great, there's also art from Syria and Iraq there, isn't there? |
| **Anna:** Ja, genau. Wann treffen wir uns? | Yes, exactly. When shall we meet? |
| **Samir:** Können wir uns um drei Uhr am Eingang treffen? | Can we meet at three at the entrance? |
| **Anna:** Ja, das passt. Willst du danach noch einen Kaffee trinken? | Yes, that works. Do you want to have a coffee afterwards? |
| **Samir:** Gern! Dann bis Samstag! | Sure! See you on Saturday, then! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hast du am Samstag Zeit? | Are you free on Saturday? |
| Ich kann nicht. Ich muss arbeiten. | I can't. I have to work. |
| Willst du mitkommen? | Do you want to come along? |
| Gern! / Leider nicht. | Sure! / Unfortunately not. |
| Wann treffen wir uns? | When shall we meet? |
| Treffen wir uns um drei? | Shall we meet at three? |
| Das passt (mir). | That works (for me). |
| Bis Samstag! / Bis dann! | See you Saturday! / See you then! |
| Montag, Dienstag, Mittwoch, Donnerstag, Freitag, Samstag, Sonntag | Monday to Sunday |
| am Montag / am Wochenende | on Monday / at the weekend |
| heute / morgen / übermorgen | today / tomorrow / the day after tomorrow |
| einen Termin machen | to make an appointment |

## Say it right

**Sentence melody.** Statements and W-questions fall at the end: *Wann treffen wir uns?* ↘ Yes/no questions rise: *Hast du Zeit?* ↗ In a sentence with a modal verb, the stress usually falls on the last word, the infinitive: *Ich muss ARbeiten. Wir können uns am EINgang treffen.*
