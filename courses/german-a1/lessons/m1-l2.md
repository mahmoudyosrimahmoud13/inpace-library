# Meeting someone new

Samir, from Damascus, starts a German course in Berlin. He meets Anna, his classmate. Read with the meaning next to it; get the idea, then look at how it's said.

## Listen first: the conversation

**First day of the course**

| German | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Anna:** Hallo! Ich bin Anna. Wie heißt du? | Hi! I'm Anna. What's your name? |
| **Samir:** Hallo, Anna. Ich heiße Samir. | Hi, Anna. My name is Samir. |
| **Anna:** Freut mich, Samir. Woher kommst du? | Nice to meet you, Samir. Where are you from? |
| **Samir:** Ich komme aus Syrien, aus Damaskus. Und du? | I'm from Syria, from Damascus. And you? |
| **Anna:** Ich komme aus Polen, aber ich wohne schon zwei Jahre hier in Berlin. | I'm from Poland, but I've been living here in Berlin for two years. |
| **Samir:** Ich wohne auch in Berlin, in Neukölln. Was bist du von Beruf? | I live in Berlin too, in Neukölln. What do you do for a living? |
| **Anna:** Ich bin Krankenschwester. Und du? | I'm a nurse. And you? |
| **Samir:** Ich bin Ingenieur. Ich suche hier Arbeit. | I'm an engineer. I'm looking for work here. |
| **Anna:** Viel Glück! Oh, der Kurs beginnt. Bis später! | Good luck! Oh, the course is starting. See you later! |
| **Samir:** Tschüss, bis später! | Bye, see you later! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hallo! / Guten Tag! | Hi! / Hello (more formal) |
| Guten Morgen! / Guten Abend! | Good morning! / Good evening! |
| Ich bin... / Ich heiße... | I'm... / My name is... |
| Wie heißt du? / Wie heißen Sie? | What's your name? (friendly / polite) |
| Freut mich. | Nice to meet you. |
| Woher kommst du? | Where are you from? |
| Ich komme aus... | I'm from... |
| Ich wohne in... | I live in... |
| Was bist du von Beruf? | What do you do for a living? |
| Ich bin Ingenieur / Lehrerin. | I'm an engineer / a teacher (f). |

| Danke! Bitte! | Thanks! You're welcome! |
| Tschüss! / Auf Wiedersehen! | Bye! / Goodbye (formal) |

## Say it right

German spelling is very regular. The key sounds:

- **ä** like *e* in *bed*; **ö** say *e* with rounded lips; **ü** say *ee* with rounded lips: *Tschüss*, *München*.
- **ch** after *a, o, u*: a rough sound in the throat, as in Scottish *loch*: *acht, Buch*. After *i, e, ä, ö, ü* and consonants: a soft hiss, like a whispered *h* in *huge*: *ich, nicht, München*.
- **w** = English *v*: *wohnen, Wie*. **v** = English *f*: *Vater*. **z** = *ts*: *zehn*. **j** = English *y*: *ja*.
- **ei** = English *eye*: *heißen, nein*. **ie** = *ee*: *Wie, Sie*. **eu** = *oy*: *freut, neu*.

Say: *Ich heiße Samir. Wie heißen Sie? Freut mich. Tschüss!*
