# Read: eigenvectors, low-rank approximations and high dimensions (10.4–10.5)

- **Computing PCA (10.4)**: eigendecomposition of S, or, better in practice, the **SVD of the data matrix** X:
  the right-singular vectors are the principal directions and σᵢ²/N are the eigenvalues. Projecting onto them is the
  best low-rank approximation from course 2 (Eckart-Young).
- **Iterative methods**: the **power iteration** finds the top eigenvector by repeatedly multiplying and normalizing
  (that's how PageRank works).
- **High dimensions (10.5)**: with N ≪ D (100 images of a million pixels), eigendecompose the small N × N matrix XXᵀ
  instead, and map its eigenvectors back with Xᵀ.
