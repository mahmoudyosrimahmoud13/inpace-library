# Read: model selection (8.6)

How do you choose between models of different complexity?

- **Nested cross-validation (8.6.1)**: an inner loop picks hyperparameters, an outer loop estimates how well the whole
  procedure generalizes.
- **Bayesian model selection (8.6.2)**: compare models by their **evidence** (marginal likelihood) p(data | model). It
  automatically penalizes complexity: a flexible model spreads its probability over many datasets, so it gives less to
  the one you saw (**Occam's razor**).
- **Bayes factors (8.6.3)**: the ratio of two evidences, like the odds update in the medical test video.
- **AIC and BIC (8.6.4)**: cheap approximations that penalize the number of parameters.
