# Read: maximum likelihood as orthogonal projection (9.4) and further reading (9.5)

The geometric view closes the loop with course 1: the prediction Φθ_ML = Φ(ΦᵀΦ)⁻¹Φᵀy is the **orthogonal projection** of
y onto the column space of Φ. The residual is orthogonal to every feature column. With orthonormal features
(9.4's example), the projection is just Σ (φₖᵀy) φₖ.

9.5 points to generalized linear models (logistic regression, and so on) and Gaussian processes, which are Bayesian
linear regression with infinitely many features.
