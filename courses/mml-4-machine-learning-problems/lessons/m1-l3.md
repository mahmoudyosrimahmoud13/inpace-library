# Read: empirical risk minimization (8.2)

The "function" view of learning, in four steps:

1. **Hypothesis class**: which functions we allow, e.g. f(x, θ) = θᵀx (affine/linear models).
2. **Loss function** ℓ(y, ŷ): squared loss, hinge loss, cross-entropy… The **empirical risk** is the average loss on the
   training data: R_emp = (1/N) Σ ℓ(yₙ, f(xₙ, θ)).
3. **Regularization**: add a penalty like λ‖θ‖² to stop the model from fitting noise. The book shows why this reduces
   overfitting and links it to MAP (tomorrow).
4. **Cross-validation**: estimate generalization by training on K − 1 folds and validating on the remaining one, K times.
