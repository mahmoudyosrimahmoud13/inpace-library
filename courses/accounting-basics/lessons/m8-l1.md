# Practice set: one month at Karim's bike repair

Time to do a whole cycle yourself. Use a spreadsheet (or paper) and do every step. The full answers are in the next lesson. **Don't look before you've finished.**

## The business

Karim opens a bike repair shop on July 1. His chart of accounts:

| No. | Account | No. | Account |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1010 | Cash | 2100 | Wages payable |
| 1100 | Accounts receivable | 2200 | Unearned revenue |
| 1200 | Parts | 3010 | Karim, capital |
| 1300 | Prepaid insurance | 3100 | Karim, drawings |
| 1500 | Tools | 4010 | Repair revenue |
| 1510 | Accumulated depreciation, tools | 5010 | Wages expense |
| 2010 | Accounts payable | 5020 | Phone and internet expense |
| | | 5030 | Insurance expense |
| | | 5040 | Parts expense |
| | | 5050 | Depreciation expense |

## July's transactions

| # | Date | Transaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Jul 1 | Karim invests $10,000 cash in the business. |
| 2 | Jul 1 | Buys tools for $3,600 cash. They should last 3 years, with no salvage value. |
| 3 | Jul 2 | Pays $1,200 for 6 months of insurance (July to December). |
| 4 | Jul 3 | Buys parts on credit, $800. |
| 5 | Jul 8 | Repairs bikes for cash, $1,500. |
| 6 | Jul 12 | Repairs a delivery company's bikes on account, $900. |
| 7 | Jul 15 | Pays his helper $600. |
| 8 | Jul 18 | The delivery company pays $500 of what it owes. |
| 9 | Jul 22 | Pays the parts supplier $500. |
| 10 | Jul 25 | A customer pays $400 in advance for a full restoration to be done in August. |
| 11 | Jul 30 | Karim takes $300 for personal use. |
| 12 | Jul 31 | Pays the phone and internet bill, $120. |

## Information for the adjustments (July 31)

- One month of insurance has been used.
- A count shows $350 of parts still on the shelf.
- Depreciation on the tools for July (straight line).
- The helper has earned $150 for the last days of July, to be paid in August.

## Your tasks

1. **Journalize** the 12 transactions.
2. **Post** them to ledger accounts (T-accounts or running balances).
3. Prepare the **unadjusted trial balance**.
4. Journalize and post the **four adjusting entries**.
5. Prepare the **adjusted trial balance**.
6. Prepare the **income statement**, the **statement of owner's equity** and the **balance sheet** for July.
7. Journalize the **closing entries** and prepare the **post-closing trial balance**.

## Hints, if you get stuck

- Transaction 10 is not revenue yet. What does Karim owe the customer?
- Tools: 3,600 ÷ 36 months.
- Parts used = parts bought − parts left.
- Cash at July 31 should be **6,080**. If yours isn't, recheck the cash entries before going on.
- Your unadjusted trial balance totals should be **13,100**.

When your balance sheet balances, open the next lesson and compare every step.
