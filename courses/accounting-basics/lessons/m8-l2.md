## 7. Closing

1. Debit Repair revenue 2,400 / Credit Income summary 2,400
2. Debit Income summary 1,620 / Credit each expense (750, 450, 200, 120, 100)
3. Debit Income summary 780 / Credit Karim, capital 780
4. Debit Karim, capital 300 / Credit Karim, drawings 300

**Post-closing trial balance:** Cash 6,080 + AR 400 + Parts 350 + Prepaid insurance 1,000 + Tools 3,600 = **11,430** debits; Accumulated depreciation 100 + AP 300 + Unearned 400 + Wages payable 150 + Capital 10,480 = **11,430** credits.

## The usual mistakes

- Recording the $400 advance (10) as revenue. It's earned in August.
- Recording the $500 collection (8) as revenue again.
- Expensing all the insurance or all the tools in July.
- Treating drawings as an expense: profit would show 480 instead of 780.
