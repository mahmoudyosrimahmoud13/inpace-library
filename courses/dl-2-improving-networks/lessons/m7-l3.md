# Programming frameworks, then and now

The slides list frameworks of their time and show TensorFlow 1 code (`tf.Session`, placeholders). That's gone. Today:

- **PyTorch 2.x** for most research and open models; `torch.compile` speeds models up with one line.
- **Keras 3** on TensorFlow, JAX or PyTorch; `model.fit` for the common case, `tf.GradientTape` or a PyTorch loop when
  you need control.
- **JAX** for large-scale research (Google uses it for its biggest models).

The slides' criteria for choosing still hold: ease of programming, speed, and truly open with good governance.
