# Other ways to regularize, and a real experiment

- **Data augmentation:** flip, crop, rotate, distort: cheap "new" examples (part 4).
- **Early stopping:** stop when the dev error starts rising. Simple, but it mixes two jobs (fitting and not
  overfitting); many people prefer L2 plus training to the end, or early stopping with `restore_best_weights`.

A real run, the same network on 100 noisy training examples:

![Regularization experiment](regularization.png)

Without regularization the training loss goes to 0 while the dev loss climbs: memorization. Weight decay keeps the
dev loss flat and low; dropout helps too. The notebook repeats this on handwritten digits (dev accuracy 90.3% → 93.6%
with dropout).
