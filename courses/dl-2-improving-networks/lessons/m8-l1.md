The same tools in Keras: L2 and dropout, optimizers, AdamW with a warmup-cosine schedule plus batch norm plus early
stopping (stopped after 29 epochs at 97.8% test accuracy in our run), `tf.data`, and mixed precision.

**Mixed precision** in one sentence: compute most things in 16-bit floats (bfloat16 or float16) for speed and memory,
keep a 32-bit copy of the weights for accuracy. On modern GPUs it's close to free speed.
