# Momentum, RMSprop and Adam

- **Momentum:** step along an average of recent gradients (`β ≈ 0.9`). Zig-zags cancel out, the consistent direction
  builds up speed.
- **RMSprop:** divide each gradient by the root of an average of its **squares** (`β₂ ≈ 0.999`). Directions with big
  gradients get smaller steps, flat directions bigger ones.
- **Adam** = momentum + RMSprop + bias correction. Defaults: `β₁ = 0.9`, `β₂ = 0.999`, `ε = 10⁻⁸`; tune the learning rate.

![Optimizers on a narrow valley](optimizers.png)

Plain gradient descent zig-zags across the valley; momentum overshoots but gets there; RMSprop and Adam take a direct
path. In practice: **AdamW** with a tuned learning rate (often 1e-3 to 3e-4) for most networks; SGD with momentum still
competes well on some vision tasks.
