# Vanishing and exploding gradients, and initialization

In a deep network, signals are multiplied layer after layer. Weights a bit above 1 make them **explode**; a bit below 1
make them **vanish**. Careful initialization keeps the variance steady:

![Initialization](init.png)

| Activation | Initialization | Variance of each weight |
|---|---|---|
| ReLU | He (Kaiming) | `2 / n[l−1]` |
| tanh | Xavier (Glorot) | `1 / n[l−1]` or `2 / (n[l−1] + n[l])` |

PyTorch: `nn.init.kaiming_normal_(w, nonlinearity="relu")`. Keras: `Dense(..., kernel_initializer="he_normal")`.
The notebook trains a 10-layer ReLU network: zeros and tiny weights stay stuck at 8% dev accuracy (chance), He
initialization reaches 93%.

Since then, **residual connections** (part 4) and **normalization layers** (day 7) made very deep networks trainable
even with imperfect initialization.
