# Present perfect continuous

**have/has been + ing**: نشاط بدأ في الماضي ومستمر حتى الآن (أو انتهى للتو وأثره ظاهر). التركيز على **المدة**.

```text
I've been learning English for three years.
She's been working here since 2021.
How long have you been waiting?
You look tired. Have you been running?      (انتهى للتو، والأثر ظاهر)
```

## for أم since؟

- **for** + مدة: for two hours, for ten years
- **since** + نقطة بداية: since Monday, since 2019, since I was a child

## simple أم continuous؟

| Present perfect simple | Present perfect continuous |
|---|---|
| النتيجة / العدد | المدة / النشاط نفسه |
| I've written three emails. | I've been writing emails all morning. |
| I've read the book. (أنهيته) | I've been reading the book. (لم أنهه) |

أفعال الحالة لا تأخذ ing: *I've known him for years* (لا: have been knowing).
