# 30 phrasal verb شائعًا

فعل + حرف = معنى جديد غالبًا. احفظها في جمل:

| Phrasal verb | المعنى | مثال |
|---|---|---|
| find out | يكتشف | I found out the truth. |
| give up | يستسلم / يقلع | Don't give up. / He gave up smoking. |
| look after | يرعى | She looks after her mother. |
| look for | يبحث | I'm looking for a job. |
| look forward to | يتطلع إلى | I look forward to hearing from you. |
| get on (with) | يتفاهم | I get on well with my boss. |
| put off | يؤجل | We put off the meeting. |
| turn down | يرفض / يخفض | She turned down the offer. |
| run out of | ينفد | We've run out of milk. |
| set up | يؤسس | He set up a company. |
| carry on | يستمر | Carry on working. |
| come up with | يبتكر فكرة | She came up with a great idea. |
| bring up | يربي / يطرح موضوعًا | I was brought up in Assiut. |
| pick up | يلتقط / يتعلم بسهولة | I'll pick you up at 8. / I picked up some Italian. |
| take off | تقلع / يخلع | The plane took off. / Take off your shoes. |
| work out | يحل / يتمرن | I can't work out the answer. / I work out at the gym. |
| deal with | يتعامل مع | I'll deal with it. |
| fill in | يملأ | Fill in this form. |
| get over | يتعافى من | He got over the flu. |
| point out | يلفت الانتباه | She pointed out a mistake. |

بعضها ينفصل والمفعول في الوسط: *turn it down*, *pick me up*. مع الضمير **يجب** الفصل: *turn down it* ❌.
