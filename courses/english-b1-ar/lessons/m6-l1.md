# who, which, that, whose, where

| الكلمة | لـ | مثال |
|---|---|---|
| who / that | الأشخاص | The man who lives next door is a doctor. |
| which / that | الأشياء | The phone which I bought is broken. |
| whose | الملكية | That's the girl whose father is a pilot. |
| where | المكان | This is the café where we met. |
| when | الزمن | I remember the day when we moved. |

## حذف who/which/that

إذا كانت **مفعولًا** (بعدها فاعل) ممكن تحذفها: *The film (that) we watched was great.*
إذا كانت **فاعلًا** لا تحذفها: *The man who called you...* (لا: The man called you was...)

## الجمل غير المحددة (بفواصل)

معلومة إضافية عن شيء معروف، **which** وليس that:
*My brother, who lives in Dubai, is visiting us. / Cairo, which is very crowded, is my favourite city.*
