# تمرين

1. If I ___ (be) rich, I'd travel the world. 2. If she ___ (not / miss) the bus, she would have arrived on time.
3. I wish I ___ (can) speak French. 4. I wish I ___ (not / eat) so much last night.
5. What ___ you ___ (do) if you lost your passport abroad? 6. If you mix red and blue, you ___ (get) purple.

## الإجابات

1. were 2. hadn't missed 3. could 4. hadn't eaten 5. would / do 6. get
