# نقل الجمل

عندما تنقل كلام شخص بعد ما قاله، الزمن غالبًا **يرجع خطوة للخلف**:

| قال | ننقل |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | He said (that) he **was** tired. |
| "I **work** in a bank." | She said she **worked** in a bank. |
| "I**'m leaving**." | He said he **was leaving**. |
| "I **saw** her." | He said he **had seen** her. |
| "I**'ve finished**." | She said she **had finished**. |
| "I **will** call." | He said he **would** call. |
| "I **can** help." | She said she **could** help. |

وتتغير كلمات الوقت والمكان: today ← that day، tomorrow ← the next day، yesterday ← the day before، here ← there.

## say أم tell؟

- **say** (something): *He said that he was busy.*
- **tell** + شخص: *He told **me** that he was busy.* (لا: He said me ❌)
