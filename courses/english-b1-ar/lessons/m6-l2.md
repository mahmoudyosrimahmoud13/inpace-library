# must be, can't be, might be

الاستنتاج من دليل:

| ثقة | الحاضر | الماضي |
|---|---|---|
| متأكد أنه صح | He **must be** tired. | He **must have been** tired. |
| ممكن | She **might/may/could be** at home. | She **might have missed** the bus. |
| متأكد أنه غلط | That **can't be** true. | He **can't have seen** me. |

```text
The lights are off. They must be asleep.
She's not answering. She might be driving.
You've just eaten! You can't be hungry.
His car is here, so he can't have left.
```

نفي must هنا هو **can't**، لا mustn't.
