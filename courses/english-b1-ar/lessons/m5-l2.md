# نقل الأسئلة والأوامر

## الأسئلة: ترتيب جملة عادية، بدون do/did

```text
"Where do you live?"      → She asked me where I lived.
"Are you married?"        → He asked if/whether I was married.
"Have you seen my keys?"  → She asked if I had seen her keys.
```

## الأوامر والطلبات: tell/ask + شخص + to

```text
"Close the door."         → He told me to close the door.
"Please don't be late."   → She asked us not to be late.
```

## أفعال نقل أخرى

```text
He promised to call.   She suggested going out.   He advised me to rest.   They refused to pay.   She admitted lying.
```
