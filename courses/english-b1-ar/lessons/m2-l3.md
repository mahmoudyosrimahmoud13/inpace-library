# تمرين

1. By the time we arrived, the film ___ (start). 2. I ___ (never / see) the sea before I moved to Alex.
3. She was tired because she ___ (study) all night. 4. When I opened the fridge, I realised someone ___ (eat) my cake.
5. After he ___ (finish) his degree, he travelled to Germany.

## الإجابات

1. had started 2. had never seen 3. had been studying 4. had eaten 5. had finished (أو finished، المعنى واضح بـ after)
