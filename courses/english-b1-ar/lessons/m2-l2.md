# احكِ بحرفية

الحكاية الجيدة تمزج 4 أزمنة:

| الزمن | دوره |
|---|---|
| past simple | الأحداث الرئيسية بالترتيب |
| past continuous | الخلفية والجو |
| past perfect | ما حدث قبل القصة |
| past perfect continuous | نشاط طويل قبل لحظة |

## قصة نموذجية

*It was raining heavily and I was driving home after a long day. I had been working for twelve hours and I was
exhausted. Suddenly, I realised that I had left my phone at the office. By the time I got back, the building had
closed, so I had to call the security guard from a café. In the end, he let me in, and I finally got home at midnight.*

## أدوات الحكي

```text
At first, ...   Eventually, ...   As soon as ...   By the time ...   It turned out that ...   Luckily / Unfortunately, ...
```
