# القراءة والكتابة

## القراءة

*When Karim Adel was offered a promotion that would have doubled his salary, everyone expected him to accept it.
Instead, he turned it down. "I had been working sixty hours a week for three years," he explains. "I realised I
hadn't seen my children grow up." Karim used to believe that success meant money; now he thinks it means time.
Six months ago he set up a small online bakery with his wife, which is run from their kitchen. "If I had taken the
promotion, I would have earned more, but I wouldn't be this happy," he says. Not everyone agrees: his former manager
told him he was making a big mistake. "He might be right," Karim admits, "but I don't wish I'd done it differently."*

1. Why was everyone surprised by Karim's decision? 2. What had his working life been like?
3. How has his idea of success changed? 4. Where is the bakery run from? 5. Does Karim regret his decision? How do you know?

## الكتابة (120 إلى 150 كلمة)

اختر: (أ) قصة تبدأ بـ *I had never felt so nervous before.* أو (ب) مقال: *Money is more important than free time. Do you agree?*

## الإجابات

1. Because the promotion would have doubled his salary. 2. He had been working sixty hours a week for three years.
3. He used to think it meant money; now he thinks it means time. 4. From their kitchen.
5. No. He says "I don't wish I'd done it differently."
