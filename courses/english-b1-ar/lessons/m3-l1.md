# Zero و second conditional

## Zero: حقائق عامة

*If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.* (if + present, present)

## Second: موقف خيالي أو بعيد عن الواقع الآن

**If + past simple, would + فعل**

```text
If I had more time, I would learn Spanish.       (لكن ليس عندي وقت)
If I were you, I'd take the job.                 (نصيحة: were مع كل الضمائر)
What would you do if you won a million pounds?
```

## first أم second؟

- *If I **get** the job, I'**ll** move to Cairo.* (ممكن فعلًا)
- *If I **got** a job in Japan, I'**d** move there.* (بعيد، خيالي)
