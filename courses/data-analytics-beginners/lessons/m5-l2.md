# Charts that tell the truth

Pick the chart by the question:

| Question | Chart |
|---|---|
| How does something change over time? | **Line** |
| How do categories compare? | **Bar / column** (sorted) |
| What are the parts of a total? | stacked bar (or a pie, only with 2-4 parts) |
| Are two numbers related? | scatter |

![A line chart of sales by month next to its pivot table](calc_10_monthly_chart.jpg)

The line shows a clear **summer peak** (July and August) and that July's jump is bigger than June to July should be:
part of it is the **10% price rise**. Saying that is what makes you an analyst, not a chart maker.

## Rules

- Start the value axis at **zero** for bar charts (bars compare lengths).
- One message per chart, written in the title: "Sales peak in July and August", not "Sales chart".
- Remove what doesn't help: 3D, shadows, rainbow colours.
