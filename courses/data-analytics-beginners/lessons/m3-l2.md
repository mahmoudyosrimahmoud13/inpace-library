# Answering 'by branch': SUMIFS and friends

Make a new sheet called `Summary` and list the four branches in A10:A13. Then:

```text
B10  =SUMIFS(Data!$J$2:$J$20839, Data!$C$2:$C$20839, A10)       total sales for this branch
C10  =COUNTIFS(Data!$C$2:$C$20839, A10)                          orders for this branch
D10  =AVERAGEIFS(Data!$J$2:$J$20839, Data!$C$2:$C$20839, A10)    average order for this branch
```

`SUMIFS(what to add, where to look, what to match, ...)`. You can add more pairs:
`=SUMIFS(sales, branch, "Maadi", category, "Coffee")`.

## The $ signs

`$J$2:$J$20839` is an **absolute** reference: it doesn't move when you copy the formula down. `A10` is **relative**:
it becomes A11, A12, A13 as you copy. Press **F4** while the cursor is in a reference to cycle through the options.
Getting `$` right is the most common beginner bug, and a common interview question.
