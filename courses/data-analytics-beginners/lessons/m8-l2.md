# Interview questions for junior data analysts

Answer each out loud in under a minute.

1. **Walk me through how you'd analyze a new dataset.** Understand the question and the grain; inspect types,
   missing values, duplicates; clean and document; summarize; chart; check; write the takeaway.
2. **VLOOKUP vs XLOOKUP vs INDEX/MATCH?** XLOOKUP is the modern choice; INDEX/MATCH works everywhere; always exact match.
3. **What's a pivot table and when is it not enough?** Fast summaries; not enough when steps must repeat (use Power
   Query or Python) or data is huge (use a database).
4. **How do you handle missing values?** Find out why; fill with a clear label, leave, or remove, and say which.
5. **Measure vs calculated column in Power BI?** Measures are calculated per visual with filters; calculated columns
   are stored per row. Prefer measures for numbers you aggregate.
6. **What is a star schema?** A fact table with dimension tables around it, one-to-many relationships.
7. **Mean vs median?** The median isn't pulled by extremes; report it for skewed data like income or order size.
8. **Correlation vs causation?** Two things moving together don't prove one causes the other (summer raises both
   cold drinks and sales).
9. **How do you check your work?** Totals match the source; the same answer from two methods; sense checks.
10. **Tell me about a time data changed a decision.** Use STAR: situation, task, action, result, with a number.

## SQL: the next step

Every analyst job asks for SQL. The same questions in SQL:

```sql
SELECT branch, SUM(sales) AS sales, COUNT(*) AS orders, AVG(sales) AS avg_order
FROM coffee_sales_2025
GROUP BY branch
ORDER BY sales DESC;
```

If you understood SUMIFS, pivot tables and groupby, you already understand GROUP BY.
