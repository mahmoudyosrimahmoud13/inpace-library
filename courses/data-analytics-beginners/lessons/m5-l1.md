# Pivot tables: answers in seconds

> **About the screenshots:** the spreadsheet pictures show **LibreOffice Calc**, a free program that works like Excel
> (same formulas, same ideas). Where a button or menu has a different name in Excel, it's written next to the step
> as **Excel:**. Everything here works in Excel, Google Sheets and Calc.

A pivot table summarizes thousands of rows by dragging fields:

- Select a cell in the data, then **Insert → Pivot Table** (**Excel:** Insert → PivotTable).
- Drag `branch` to **Rows**, `category` to **Columns**, `sales` to **Values** (Sum).

![A pivot table: sales by branch and category, and a second one by month](calc_09_pivot.jpg)

The second pivot table puts `month` in Rows: sales by month in one step.

## What pivot tables teach you here

- Coffee is more than half of all sales in every branch.
- Zamalek sells the most pastry and cold drinks: a branch with more time-to-sit customers?
- Change **Sum** to **Count** to get orders instead of sales, or **Average** for average order.

Pivot tables don't update by themselves when the data changes: **right-click → Refresh**. In Excel, building them on
a Table means new rows are included when you refresh.
