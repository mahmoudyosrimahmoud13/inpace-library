# Decisions with IF

Monthly average: `F10 =B10/12`. Is the branch on target?

```text
G10  =IF(F10>=E10, "On target", "Below target")
```

![IF writing On target or Below target for each branch](calc_07_if.jpg)

Two branches (Maadi, Nasr City) are on target on average; two (Zamalek, Heliopolis) are not. But careful: Zamalek
has the **highest sales** and still misses, because its target is the highest. "Biggest" and "best" are different
questions, and good analysts say which one they're answering.

Useful relatives: `IFS` (several conditions), `AND`, `OR`, `IFERROR(formula, "something")` to hide errors you
expect.
