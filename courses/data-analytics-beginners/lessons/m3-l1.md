# Your first formulas, and a month column

Every formula starts with `=`. Cells are referenced by column and row: `J2` is the sales of the first order.

| Formula | Answers |
|---|---|
| `=SUM(J2:J20839)` | total sales |
| `=COUNT(A2:A20839)` | how many orders |
| `=AVERAGE(J2:J20839)` | average order value |
| `=MAX(J2:J20839)` / `=MIN(...)` | biggest / smallest order |
| `=ROUND(x, 0)` | round a number |

## Add a month column

To answer anything "by month", make a column for it. In K1 type `month`, in K2:

```text
=TEXT(B2, "YYYY-MM")
```

then double-click the little square at the corner of K2 to fill it down to the last row.

![A month column made with the TEXT formula, shown in the formula bar](calc_04_month_formula.jpg)

Notice the **formula bar** at the top: it shows the formula, while the cell shows its result. (Calc writes `;`
between arguments; Excel in English uses `,`. Both are fine in their own program.)
