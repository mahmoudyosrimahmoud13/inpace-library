# Sorting

Sort by sales, biggest first: click a cell in the `sales` column, then **Data → Sort Descending**
(**Excel:** Data → Sort Z→A).

![Orders sorted by sales, biggest first](calc_03_sorted.jpg)

The biggest orders are 4 cheesecakes or 4 smoothies after the July price rise. That's an insight already: the largest
orders are **multi-item** orders of the priciest products.

**Warning:** always sort the **whole table**, not one column. Sorting one column alone breaks every row apart.
Using filters or a Table protects you from that.
