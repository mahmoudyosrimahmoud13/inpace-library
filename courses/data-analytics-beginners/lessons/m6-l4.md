# Build the report

A one-page report that answers "how are the branches doing?":

| Area | Visual | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| Top | 4 **cards** | Total Sales, Orders, Average Order, vs Target % |
| Left | **Line chart** | Date[Month] on the axis, Total Sales |
| Right | **Clustered bar** | branch, Total Sales (sorted), Monthly Target as a second value |
| Bottom | **Matrix** | branch on rows, category on columns, Total Sales |
| Side | **Slicers** | branch, category, customer_type |

## Make it good

- Click a bar: the other visuals **cross-filter**. Check that's what you want (Format → Edit interactions).
- Titles that say the message.
- Conditional formatting on the matrix (background colour by value), and on vs Target % (red below 0).
- A **tooltip** page or drill-through for the product details of one branch.

## Share it

**Home → Publish** to the Power BI service, then share with colleagues or embed it in Teams. Scheduled refresh needs
the data somewhere the service can reach (SharePoint, OneDrive, a database, or a gateway for files on a PC).
