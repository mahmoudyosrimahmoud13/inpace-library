# Watch: Let's build GPT, from scratch

Andrej Karpathy builds a character-level GPT in PyTorch, step by step, and trains it on the works of Shakespeare. It's
about two hours, and it's the natural next step after your sorting transformer: same architecture, real text.

Everything in it should now feel familiar:

- His bigram baseline is your Zen of Python model from day 4.
- His "mathematical trick in self-attention" is the causal mask from day 3: a lower-triangular weight matrix.
- His `Head`, `MultiHeadAttention`, `FeedFoward` and `Block` classes are your `CausalSelfAttention` and
  `TransformerLayer`, with residual connections and layer norm in the same places.

The differences are speed and scale: PyTorch runs on a GPU and his model has millions of parameters. If you want to train
on real text yourself, this is the way to go from here.
