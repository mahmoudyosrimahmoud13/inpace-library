# Watch: But what is a convolution?

3Blue1Brown explains convolution from scratch: for lists of numbers, for probabilities, and for images (blurring and
edge detection). The image part is exactly the operation of section 10.2.

Two details to connect with the book:

- In maths, a convolution **flips** the kernel before sliding it. Neural networks usually don't flip (strictly, they
  compute a cross-correlation), and since the kernel is learned, it makes no difference.
- The edge-detection kernels in the video are the kind of filter a CNN's first layer learns by itself (section 10.3.2).
