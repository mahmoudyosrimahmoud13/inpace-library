# Watch: Theoretical foundations of graph neural networks

Petar Veličković, one of the inventors of graph attention networks, explains GNNs from first principles: permutation
invariance and equivariance, message passing, and how attention and convolution on graphs fit together. It's a full
lecture (about an hour); the first half covers chapter 13.

It's the best companion to section 13.3.6: listen for how he derives the form of a GNN layer from the symmetry it must
respect, rather than inventing it.
