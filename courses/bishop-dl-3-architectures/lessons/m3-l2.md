# Watch: Transformers, the tech behind LLMs

3Blue1Brown's overview of how a GPT-style model goes from text to the next word: tokens, embeddings, attention blocks,
MLP blocks, and the final softmax over the vocabulary. It covers the big picture of chapter 12 before the details.

Pay attention to the part about **embeddings as directions**: words with related meanings point in related directions.
That's the "representation" idea from chapter 6, now for words (section 12.2.1).
