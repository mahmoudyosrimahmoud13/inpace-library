## The big idea: everything can be tokens

Once you can turn data into a sequence of vectors, the same transformer works on it.

- **Vision transformers** (12.4.1): cut an image into patches (say 16 × 16 pixels), flatten each patch into a vector,
  add position embeddings, and feed the sequence of patches to a transformer encoder. With enough data, this matches or
  beats CNNs, even though it has much less built-in knowledge about images. Compare that with chapter 10: less inductive
  bias needs more data.
- **Generative image transformers** (12.4.2): turn an image into a sequence of discrete tokens and generate it one token
  at a time, like text.
- **Audio** (12.4.3): turn sound into a spectrogram, an image of frequencies over time, and treat it like an image or a
  sequence.
- **Text-to-speech** (12.4.4) and **vision-and-language** models (12.4.5): connect two kinds of tokens. A model can read
  an image's tokens and write text about it, or the other way round.
