# Watch: Attention in transformers, step-by-step

The follow-up video zooms into the attention block itself: queries, keys and values, the attention pattern, masking, and
multiple heads. It's section 12.1, animated.

Match his notation to the book's:

- His query, key and value matrices are the book's W⁽q⁾, W⁽k⁾ and W⁽v⁾ (section 12.1.4).
- His division by the square root of the key dimension is the scaling of section 12.1.5.
- His **masking**, which stops later words from influencing earlier ones, is the causal attention of section 12.3.1:
  you'll need it for the sorting transformer.
