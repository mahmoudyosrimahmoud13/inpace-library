# Interview drill: 15 questions

Answer out loud, then check.

1. **Bias vs variance?** Error from wrong assumptions (too simple) vs sensitivity to the training set (too flexible).
2. **Why squared error for regression?** Smooth, convex for linear models, and it's maximum likelihood under Gaussian
   noise.
3. **Why log loss for classification?** Convex for logistic regression; maximum likelihood for Bernoulli labels;
   punishes confident mistakes.
4. **What does the learning rate do and how do you choose it?** Step size; try a ×3 grid and read the cost curve.
5. **Batch vs mini-batch vs stochastic?** All, some, one example per step; mini-batch balances speed and stability.
6. **Why scale features?** Faster, more stable gradient descent; needed for distance methods; fit on train only.
7. **Normal equation vs gradient descent?** Exact but `O(n³)` and needs invertibility vs iterative and scalable.
8. **L1 vs L2?** Sparse weights vs small weights.
9. **How do you detect overfitting?** Training error much lower than validation error.
10. **Is logistic regression a regression?** It's a classifier that regresses the log-odds.
11. **What's the decision boundary?** Where `w·x + b = 0`; linear unless you add non-linear features.
12. **How do you handle a categorical feature?** One-hot encoding (part 2).
13. **What's data leakage?** Information from outside the training data (like test-set statistics or the future)
    reaching the model.
14. **Your training cost goes up. What do you check?** Learning rate, the sign of the update, the gradient code
    (compare with numerical gradients), the data.
15. **How do you check a gradient implementation?** Gradient checking: compare with `(J(w + ε) − J(w − ε)) / 2ε`.
