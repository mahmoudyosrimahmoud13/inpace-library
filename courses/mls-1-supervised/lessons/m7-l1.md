# Underfitting and overfitting

| | Training error | New-data error | Called |
|---|---|---|---|
| Too simple (a line for a curve) | high | high | **underfit**, high **bias** |
| Just right | low | low | **generalizes** |
| Too flexible (a degree-10 curve for 10 points) | very low | high | **overfit**, high **variance** |

Ways to fight overfitting:

1. **More data**: often the best fix.
2. **Fewer features** (feature selection): may throw away useful information.
3. **Regularization**: keep all features but shrink the weights.
