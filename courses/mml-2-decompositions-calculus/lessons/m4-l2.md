# Explore: the SVD in the matrix explorer

Open the explorer and enter any matrix. The image of a circle is an ellipse; the **singular values** are the lengths of
its half-axes. (The explorer shows eigenvalues; for a symmetric matrix with positive eigenvalues, like the "symmetric"
preset, they equal the singular values.)

![Explorer: a matrix moves the plane](explore_transform.html)

**Check:** for the "squash flat" preset one singular value is 0: the ellipse collapses to a line, matching rank 1.
