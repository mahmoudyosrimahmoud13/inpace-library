# Read: Cholesky decomposition (4.3)

A **symmetric positive definite** matrix can be written as **A = LLᵀ** with L lower triangular and a positive
diagonal: a "square root" of A.

- Compute it column by column (the book's 3 × 3 example shows the pattern: diagonal entries are square roots, the
  rest come from simple divisions).
- **Why ML cares**: to draw samples from a Gaussian N(μ, Σ), take standard normal z and use μ + Lz with Σ = LLᵀ
  (the 2D Gaussian explorer in course 3 does exactly this). It also gives det(A) cheaply: det A = (∏ lᵢᵢ)², and solves
  systems with A fast.
