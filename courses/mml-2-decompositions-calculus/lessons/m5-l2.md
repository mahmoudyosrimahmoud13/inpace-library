# Read: matrix phylogeny and further reading (4.7–4.8)

Section 4.7 has a "family tree" of matrices (the book's figure): square / non-square, invertible, symmetric,
positive definite, orthogonal, diagonal… and which decompositions apply to which. Use it as a summary: try to say,
for each family, which decomposition you'd reach for and why.

Then look at the chapter's exercises: try the determinant ones, one eigenvalue exercise, and the SVD exercise.
