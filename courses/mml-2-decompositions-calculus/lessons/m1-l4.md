# Explore: watch the determinant

Open the explorer again and watch the shaded square: its area is |det A|, and it turns pink when det A < 0 (the plane
is flipped).

![Explorer: a matrix moves the plane](explore_transform.html)

**Try:** set a = 1, d = 1 and slide b. A shear changes the shape of the square but not its area: det stays 1.
