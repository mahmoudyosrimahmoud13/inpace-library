# Read: differentiation of univariate functions (5.1)

Chapter 5 gives you the tool that **training** relies on: gradients. It starts in one dimension.

- **Derivative** = limit of the difference quotient (the slope of the secant becomes the slope of the tangent).
- **Taylor polynomials (5.1.1)**: approximate a function near a point with a polynomial built from its derivatives.
  The first-order one (a line) is the basis of gradient descent; the second-order one (a parabola) of Newton's method.
- **Differentiation rules (5.1.2)**: product, quotient, sum, and above all the **chain rule**:
  (g ∘ f)′(x) = g′(f(x))·f′(x). Backpropagation is the chain rule applied systematically.
