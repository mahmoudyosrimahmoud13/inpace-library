# A full mock loop

Before real final rounds, run the whole loop in one or two days with friends playing the interviewers:

| Round | Length | Material |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | 20 min | your pitch, salary range (part 1) |
| Coding | 45 min | a fresh Medium problem (part 2) |
| AI deep dive | 60 min | questions from modules 2 to 7, your projects |
| System design | 60 min | a practice prompt (module 8) |
| Behavioral | 45 min | your story bank (part 1) |

Each interviewer fills a short form: hire / no hire, one strength, one thing to fix. Fix the weakest round first, then repeat it within a week.
