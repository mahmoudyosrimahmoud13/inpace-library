# A full mock loop

Before real final rounds, run the whole loop in one or two days with friends playing the interviewers:

| Round | Length | Material |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | 20 min | your pitch, salary range (part 1) |
| Coding | 45 min | a fresh Medium problem (part 2) |
| AI deep dive | 60 min | questions from modules 2 to 7, your projects |
| System design | 60 min | a practice prompt (module 8) |
| Behavioral | 45 min | your story bank (part 1) |

Each interviewer fills a short form: hire / no hire, one strength, one thing to fix. Fix the weakest round first, then repeat it within a week.

## The feedback form

Give each mock interviewer the same five questions, scored 1 to 4:

1. **Problem framing:** asked about requirements and success before solving?
2. **Technical depth:** mechanisms and trade-offs, not just names?
3. **Communication:** clear structure, thinking out loud, checked understanding?
4. **Evidence:** used numbers and examples from real projects?
5. **Handling pressure:** recovered from a hint or a mistake calmly?

Plus: hire or no hire, and the one thing that would change the decision.

## After the mock

Write down every question you struggled with on the same day, and the answer you wish you'd given. Turn the three weakest into flashcards in inPace, and practise them out loud until they take under 2 minutes each. Then book the next mock for a week later, focused on the weakest round.
