# The project deep dive and the defence round

The field guide notes the defence round can matter more than the code. Prepare these answers for each of your projects, out loud:

- Walk me through the architecture. Why each component?
- What was the hardest bug, and how did you find it?
- How do you know it works? Show me the evals.
- What does it cost per request, and how would you halve it?
- What happens at 100× the traffic?
- How could someone attack it, and what stops them?
- What would you do with two more weeks?
- What would you do differently from the start?
