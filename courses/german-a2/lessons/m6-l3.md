# Level check: can you do A2?

Read each line and answer honestly: *yes*, *almost*, or *not yet*.

## I can...

- tell what I did yesterday, last weekend or on a trip, with haben and sein in the Perfekt, and war / hatte;
- use the dative after mit, nach, zu, bei, von, aus and with helfen and gefallen;
- compare things and people, and buy clothes;
- explain what's wrong with me to a doctor and understand the advice;
- give reasons with weil, and say what I think with dass;
- describe my home and say where things are and where they go;
- write short personal messages and simple emails;
- understand the main point of clear, everyday conversations and short videos on familiar topics.

For each *not yet*, go back to that unit, listen again, and redo the voice note.

## Final tasks

1. **Listening:** finish *Nicos Weg A2* on [DW Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com/) and watch one [Easy German](https://www.youtube.com/@EasyGerman) episode without subtitles. Summarise each in two sentences in German.
2. **Speaking:** record a **3-minute** voice note: tell the story of an important day or trip, describe where you live, and explain why you're learning German (weil...).
3. **Writing:** write about 120 words: an email to a friend about your new flat or job.

## What next?

Move on to **German B1**, where you'll give opinions, use the Konjunktiv II, relative clauses and the passive, and handle work situations. For an official certificate, look at the **Goethe-Zertifikat A2** or **telc Deutsch A2**. Keep listening 20 to 30 minutes a day.
