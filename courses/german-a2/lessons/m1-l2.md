# What did you do?

Monday at work. Lena and Tarek talk about their weekend. Notice hat... gemacht: the past is built with haben and a participle at the end.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Monday morning**

| German | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lena:** Guten Morgen, Tarek! Wie war dein Wochenende? | Morning, Tarek! How was your weekend? |
| **Tarek:** Sehr schön! Am Samstag habe ich meinen Cousin in Leipzig besucht. Wir haben die Stadt angeschaut und viel gelacht. | Very nice! On Saturday I visited my cousin in Leipzig. We looked around the city and laughed a lot. |
| **Lena:** Toll! Und was hast du am Sonntag gemacht? | Great! And what did you do on Sunday? |
| **Tarek:** Am Sonntag habe ich die Wohnung geputzt und gekocht. Abends habe ich einen Film gesehen. Und du? | On Sunday I cleaned the flat and cooked. In the evening I watched a film. And you? |
| **Lena:** Ich habe am Samstag gearbeitet. Danach habe ich mit Freundinnen gegessen und wir haben bis zwei Uhr getanzt. | I worked on Saturday. After that I had dinner with friends and we danced until two. |
| **Tarek:** Bis zwei Uhr! Und am Sonntag? | Until two! And on Sunday? |
| **Lena:** Am Sonntag habe ich gar nichts gemacht. Ich habe bis Mittag geschlafen! | On Sunday I didn't do anything at all. I slept until midday! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Wie war dein Wochenende? | How was your weekend? |
| Was hast du gemacht? | What did you do? |
| Ich habe nichts gemacht. | I didn't do anything. |
| gestern / gestern Abend | yesterday / last night |
| letzte Woche / letztes Wochenende | last week / last weekend |
| vor zwei Tagen | two days ago |
| Ich habe meine Familie besucht. | I visited my family. |
| Wir haben viel gelacht. | We laughed a lot. |
| Es war schön / langweilig. | It was nice / boring. |
| danach / dann | after that / then |
| Ich habe lange geschlafen. | I slept in. |

## Say it right

**Unstressed ge- and -e.** In participles, *ge-* is short and weak: *gemacht* = "g'MAHKT", *gekocht*, *gesehen* ("g'ZAY-en"). The stress is on the verb stem. Also, an *-e* at the end of a word is a short, relaxed sound, never silent: *habe, Woche, Danke*.

Say: *Ich habe gearbeitet. Wir haben gekocht. Ich habe einen Film gesehen.*
