# Telling the story of a trip

Tarek went to Munich. Verbs of movement use sein in the Perfekt: ich bin gefahren, wir sind gegangen.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Back from Munich**

| German | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lena:** Du warst in München, oder? Wie war's? | You were in Munich, weren't you? How was it? |
| **Tarek:** Super! Ich bin am Freitag mit dem Zug gefahren. Die Fahrt hat fünf Stunden gedauert. | Great! I went by train on Friday. The journey took five hours. |
| **Lena:** Und was hast du dort gemacht? | And what did you do there? |
| **Tarek:** Am Samstag bin ich durch die Altstadt gegangen und habe das Deutsche Museum besucht. Es ist riesig! | On Saturday I walked through the old town and visited the Deutsches Museum. It's huge! |
| **Lena:** Bist du auch in die Berge gefahren? | Did you go to the mountains too? |
| **Tarek:** Ja, am Sonntag sind wir zum Tegernsee gefahren und sind zwei Stunden gewandert. | Yes, on Sunday we went to the Tegernsee and hiked for two hours. |
| **Lena:** Und das Wetter? | And the weather? |
| **Tarek:** Am Samstag hat es geregnet, aber am Sonntag war es sonnig. Nur ein Problem: Mein Zug zurück ist zu spät gekommen! | On Saturday it rained, but on Sunday it was sunny. Just one problem: my train back was late! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Wie war's? | How was it? |
| Ich bin mit dem Zug gefahren. | I went by train. |
| Ich bin geflogen. | I flew. |
| Die Fahrt hat fünf Stunden gedauert. | The journey took five hours. |
| Ich war drei Tage in München. | I was in Munich for three days. |
| Wir sind gewandert. | We went hiking. |
| Es hat geregnet. / Es war sonnig. | It rained. / It was sunny. |
| Der Zug ist zu spät gekommen. | The train was late. |
| in die Berge / ans Meer | to the mountains / to the sea |
| das Hotel / die Jugendherberge | the hotel / the youth hostel |
| Es hat mir gut gefallen. | I really liked it. |

## Say it right

**Compound nouns.** German joins nouns into one long word: *Altstadt* (old town), *Jugendherberge* (youth hostel), *Hauptbahnhof* (main station). The **first part** gets the main stress: *ALT-stadt*, *HAUPT-bahnhof*, *JU-gendherberge*. Read long words in parts first, then together: *Haupt-bahn-hof* → *Hauptbahnhof*.
