# Watch: Bias and variance

StatQuest's short take on bias and variance, with a straight line and a squiggly line fitted to the same points.
The straight line is the high-bias, low-variance model; the squiggle is the opposite.

After watching, connect it to the book: the "sweet spot" in the video is the value of λ that minimizes the sum of
squared bias and variance in the book's Figure 4.8. In the next lesson you'll compute both quantities yourself.
