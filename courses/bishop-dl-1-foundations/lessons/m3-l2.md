# Watch: Entropy, clearly explained

StatQuest builds entropy from "surprise", the same way section 2.5.1 builds it from the information content
`−log p(x)`. Watch for:

- Why surprise is the **log of the inverse** of a probability, and not just the inverse.
- Why entropy is the **expected** surprise: each surprise weighted by how often it happens.
- How a fair split has the most entropy, and a lopsided one has less. That's the book's 3-bit versus 2-bit example.
