# Watch: The normal distribution, clearly explained

A short, friendly look at the one-dimensional Gaussian: what the mean and standard deviation do to the curve, and how
the curve relates to real measurements. Use it to refresh your intuition before the multivariate version.

After watching, picture the two-dimensional case from section 3.2.1: the bell becomes a hill, the "width" becomes a
covariance matrix, and the contour lines are ellipses tilted along the eigenvectors.
