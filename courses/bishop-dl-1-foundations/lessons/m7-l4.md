# Wrap-up: write your report, and what comes next

## Write a one-page report

A project isn't finished until someone else can understand it. Write one page (a notebook cell, a document, or this
module's task box) as if for a doctor who knows no machine learning. Use these headings:

1. **The question**: classify biopsies as malignant or benign, and decide what to do with each one.
2. **The data**: 569 tumours, 30 measurements, 37% malignant; split 60 / 20 / 20.
3. **Two models**: a generative one (Gaussian classes, section 5.3) and logistic regression (section 5.4). One
   sentence each on what they assume.
4. **Results**: test accuracy, cross-entropy and AUC for both, and one sentence on why the differences are small
   compared with the size of the test set.
5. **The decision rule**: the loss matrix you chose, the threshold it implies, and the reject option, with the share of
   cases referred and the errors among the rest.
6. **Limits**: a small, old data set; probabilities that may not be calibrated on new hospitals' data; and why a human
   must stay in the loop.

## What you can do now

Look back at what these five chapters gave you:

- Probability as the language of uncertainty: the sum and product rules, Bayes' theorem, densities, the Gaussian.
- Where error functions come from: squared error is Gaussian noise, cross-entropy is Bernoulli or categorical labels,
  and regularization is a prior on the weights.
- Single-layer networks for regression and classification, solved in closed form or by gradient descent.
- How to turn a model's probabilities into decisions with a loss matrix, a reject option and an ROC curve.

## Next: part 2

Every model in this course had **fixed** basis functions. Chapter 6 makes the basis functions themselves learnable,
which gives deep neural networks, and chapters 7 to 9 show how to train them: gradient descent, backpropagation and
regularization. In part 2's big project you'll write your own small automatic-differentiation library, and use it to
train a network that reads handwritten digits.
