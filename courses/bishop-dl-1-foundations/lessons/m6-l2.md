# Watch: ROC and AUC, clearly explained

StatQuest builds an ROC curve one threshold at a time, the same construction as section 5.2.6. Keep the book's cancer
example in mind while you watch:

- Each point on the curve is one choice of threshold, with its own true positive rate and false positive rate.
- A screening test usually wants a high true positive rate (don't miss sick people), even at the price of more false
  positives, which a second test can sort out. That choice is the loss matrix from section 5.2.2 in disguise.
