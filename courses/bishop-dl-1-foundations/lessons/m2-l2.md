# Watch: Bayes theorem, the geometry of changing beliefs

3Blue1Brown draws Bayes' theorem as areas in a square, which makes the screening example from section 2.1 feel
obvious. While you watch:

- His "hypothesis" is the book's Y (has cancer or not), and his "evidence" is X (the test result).
- The small area of people who have the condition *and* test positive, divided by the whole area of positive tests,
  is exactly the 9 / 39 calculation from the last lesson.
- Notice how much the answer depends on the **prior**. A rare condition makes even a good test give mostly false
  alarms.
