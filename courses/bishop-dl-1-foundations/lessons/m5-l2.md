# Watch: Ridge (L2) regression

StatQuest explains ridge regression with a tiny data set of mouse weights and sizes. It's the regularizer from section
4.1.6 (`(λ/2)‖w‖²`, called weight decay in the book), seen from a statistician's side.

Watch for the moment where the penalty makes the line *less* steep, accepting a little more error on the training data
to predict new data better. That trade is exactly what the next reading, on bias and variance, makes precise.
