# Watch: Maximum likelihood, clearly explained

StatQuest walks through maximum likelihood with a normal distribution: slide the curve along, and watch the likelihood
of the data go up and down until it peaks. It's the picture behind section 2.3.2.

While you watch, notice:

- "Likelihood" is the probability of the **data**, viewed as a function of the **parameters**. The data stays fixed
  and the parameters move.
- The peak for the mean lands on the average of the measurements, which is the result the book derives with calculus.
