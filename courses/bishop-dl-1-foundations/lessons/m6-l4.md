# Watch: Logistic regression

StatQuest's overview of logistic regression: the S-shaped curve, what it predicts, and how it's fitted by maximum
likelihood. It matches section 5.4.3.

One difference in language: StatQuest fits it with "maximum likelihood", the book talks about minimizing the
"cross-entropy error". They're the same thing: the cross-entropy error is the negative log of the likelihood.
