# Watch: But what is a neural network?

The book's chapter 1 tells you *what* deep learning has done. This video from 3Blue1Brown shows *what a network is*:
layers of numbers, weights and biases, and an activation function, using handwritten digits as the example.

As you watch, connect it to chapter 1:

- The "weights and biases" in the video are the **parameters** w from section 1.2. The digit network has about 13,000
  of them instead of 10.
- "Learning" means the same thing as in the curve-fitting example: tune the parameters to make an error function small.
- The hidden layers are what section 1.3 calls **learned representations**.

You'll build a network like this one yourself, from scratch, in part 2 of this series.
