# EfficientNet, and what came after

**EfficientNet** scales resolution, depth and width **together** with one compound coefficient, to fit any compute
budget.

Since the slides:
- **Vision transformers (ViT)** cut the image into 16×16 patches and treat them like words (part 5). With enough
  pre-training data they match or beat CNNs.
- **ConvNeXt** modernized CNNs with transformer-era tricks and stays competitive.
- In practice you choose a **pre-trained backbone** by accuracy, speed and size: torchvision, `timm`, and
  `keras.applications` offer dozens with one line.
