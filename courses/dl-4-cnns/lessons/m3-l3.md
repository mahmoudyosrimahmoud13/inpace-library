# 1×1 convolutions and Inception

A **1×1 convolution** is a small fully connected layer applied at every pixel: it mixes channels and can shrink them
(28×28×192 → 28×28×32). Inception uses it as a cheap **bottleneck** before expensive 3×3 and 5×5 convolutions: the
slides' example drops the cost from about 120 million to 12.4 million multiplications.

An **Inception module** runs 1×1, 3×3, 5×5 and pooling side by side and concatenates the results: let the network
choose which filter size helps.
