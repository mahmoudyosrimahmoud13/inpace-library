# Region proposals (R-CNN family)

R-CNN: propose regions likely to contain objects, then classify them. Fast R-CNN and **Faster R-CNN** (a network makes
the proposals) sped it up. Two-stage detectors are often more accurate, one-stage (YOLO) faster; the gap has narrowed.
`torchvision.models.detection.fasterrcnn_resnet50_fpn_v2` gives you one, pre-trained.
