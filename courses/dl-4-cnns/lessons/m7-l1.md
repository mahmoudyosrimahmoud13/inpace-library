# Verification, recognition and one-shot learning

**Verification:** is this person who they claim to be (1:1)? **Recognition:** who is this, among K people (1:K)? Much
harder: a 99% accurate verifier makes many mistakes over 100 people.

**One-shot learning:** you have one photo per employee. Instead of a classifier (retrain for every new hire), learn a
**similarity function** `d(img1, img2)`: small for the same person, large for different people.
