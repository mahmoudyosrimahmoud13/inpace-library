# Interview drill

1. **Why do CNNs need fewer parameters than dense networks on images?** Parameter sharing and sparse connections.
2. **Output size of a convolution?** `⌊(n + 2p − f) / s⌋ + 1`.
3. **Why do ResNets work?** Skip connections make the identity easy to learn and let gradients flow through deep
   networks.
4. **What's a 1×1 convolution for?** Mixing and reducing channels cheaply.
5. **How would you build a classifier with 500 labelled images?** Pre-trained backbone, freeze most of it, augment,
   train the head, then maybe unfreeze the last block with a small learning rate.
6. **What is NMS and when isn't it needed?** It removes duplicate boxes; set-prediction detectors like DETR avoid it.
7. **CNN or vision transformer?** CNNs are efficient with little data and on small devices; ViTs shine with lots of
   pre-training. Many practical systems use whichever pre-trained backbone fits the budget.
