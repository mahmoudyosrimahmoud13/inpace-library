# Watch: Generative adversarial networks (Computerphile)

Computerphile explains GANs as two networks competing against each other, one making fakes and one spotting them. It's
a friendly companion to section 17.1.

Connect it to the book: the network that spots fakes is the discriminator, trained with the cross-entropy of equation
17.6, and the back-and-forth between the two is why section 17.1.2 says no single number tells you training is going well.
