# Openings for Black: against 1. e4 and 1. d4

## Against 1. e4: play 1... e5

**1. e4 e5** gives you the same kind of open, natural positions you play as White. Develop with **...Nc6, ...Nf6, ...Bc5 or ...Be7**, and castle.

- Against the **Italian (3. Bc4)**: play **3... Bc5** or **3... Nf6**, and mirror White's calm plan with ...d6, ...O-O, ...a6, ...h6.
- Against the **Ruy Lopez (3. Bb5)**: play **3... a6 4. Ba4 Nf6 5. O-O Be7**, then ...b5, ...d6 and ...O-O. Don't fear 4. Bxc6 dxc6 5. Nxe5?: **5... Qd4!** forks e5 and e4 and wins the pawn back.
- Against the **Scotch (3. d4)**: **3... exd4 4. Nxd4 Nf6** (or ...Bc5), and develop.

## Against 1. d4: the Queen's Gambit Declined

**1. d4 d5 2. c4 e6 3. Nc3 Nf6 4. Bg5 Be7 5. e3 O-O 6. Nf3**

```
8  r n b q . r k .
7  p p p . b p p p
6  . . . . p n . .
5  . . . p . . B .
4  . . P P . . . .
3  . . N . P N . .
2  P P . . . P P P
1  R . . Q K B . R
   a b c d e f g h
```

*Black to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/rnbq1rk1/ppp1bppp/4pn2/3p2B1/2PP4/2N1PN2/PP3PPP/R2QKB1R_b_KQ_-_2_6)

Black's setup is solid: the pawn on d5 holds the center, the knight on f6 and bishop on e7 break the pin, and the king is safe. Plans for Black:

- Finish development with **...Nbd7**, **...h6**, **...b6 and ...Bb7** (the bishop on c8 is Black's problem piece; give it a job).
- Free your position with a pawn break: **...c5** or **...e5**.

## A trap to win a piece: the Elephant Trap

**1. d4 d5 2. c4 e6 3. Nc3 Nf6 4. Bg5 Nbd7 5. cxd5 exd5 6. Nxd5? Nxd5! 7. Bxd8 Bb4+ 8. Qd2 Bxd2+ 9. Kxd2 Kxd8**

```
8  r . b k . . . r
7  p p p n . p p p
6  . . . . . . . .
5  . . . n . . . .
4  . . . P . . . .
3  . . . . . . . .
2  P P . K P P P P
1  R . . . . B N R
   a b c d e f g h
```

*White to move.* [Open on Lichess](https://lichess.org/analysis/standard/r1bk3r/pppn1ppp/8/3n4/3P4/8/PP1KPPPP/R4BNR_w_-_-_0_10)

White thought the knight on f6 was pinned, but the pin on the queen was only **relative**. Black gave the queen, got it back with a check, and finished a whole piece up.

## Against other first moves

Against **1. c4**, **1. Nf3** or anything else, use the same setup: **...d5, ...Nf6, ...e6, ...Be7, ...O-O**. One setup that works against everything saves you hours.

## Try it

After 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5 a6 4. Bxc6 dxc6 5. Nxe5, what does Black play?

**Answer:** **5... Qd4!**: the queen attacks the knight on e5 and the pawn on e4 at once, and Black wins the pawn back.
