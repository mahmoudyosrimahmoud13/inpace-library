# LinkedIn and GitHub that work for you

## LinkedIn

- **Headline:** the job you want plus your proof, e.g. "AI Engineer · RAG and evaluation · Python, FastAPI, Docker". Not "Student" or "Looking for opportunities".
- **About:** 3 short paragraphs: what you build, your best project with one number, what you're looking for.
- **Featured:** DocAnswer's demo and repository.
- **Open to work** set to recruiters only, if you're employed.
- **Post your build:** a short write-up of what you learned with your eval table. The field guide quotes engineers who got interviews from building in public.

## GitHub

- A profile README: one line about you, links to your 2 to 3 best projects.
- **Pin** your best repositories; archive or hide half-finished tutorials.
- Every pinned repo: a clear README, passing CI, no secrets, a licence.
- Regular, real commits beat a green square pattern. Reviewers open the code.
