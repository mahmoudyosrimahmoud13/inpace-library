# Write your projects into your CV

If you don't yet have AI work experience, your **Projects** section is the evidence. Put DocAnswer first, with a link. Templates (replace every number with yours):

**DocAnswer**: question answering over [1,200 pages of X] · [live demo] · [code]

- Built a retrieval-augmented assistant with cited answers and refusals for [audience], using Python, FastAPI, BM25 + embeddings with reciprocal rank fusion, and Docker.
- Raised retrieval hit rate@5 from [0.64] to [0.88] on a [60]-question golden set by moving from vector-only to hybrid search and tuning chunk size.
- Built an evaluation harness (golden sets + LLM judge, [90]% agreement with human labels) that runs in CI; [correct] answers rose from [71]% to [86]% across [4] measured iterations.
- Deployed on Hugging Face Spaces at [about 1,900] tokens and [p95 2.1 s] per question.

Write the CV in English for international roles even if you're applying in Arabic-speaking markets, unless the post asks otherwise; keep an Arabic version for local employers who request it.

You'll add **TinyKV** (part 2) under a short "Interview practice" line at most, and your **TriageBot** agent (part 3) as the second main project.
