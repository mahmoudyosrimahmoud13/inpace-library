# How AI changed interviews

Three trends to plan for:

1. **More in person.** The field guide cites InterviewQuery data: in-person rounds rose from 24% (2022) to 38% (2025). Google said in 2025 it was bringing back in-person interviews, partly because of AI-assisted cheating. Practise on a whiteboard and on paper.
2. **Some rounds now allow AI.** Meta announced in 2025 it was trialling AI-enabled coding interviews; Microsoft is reported to run an AI-assisted round and a separate no-AI round. They judge how you direct and check the tool.
3. **Flooded applications.** As AI-written applications pile up, referrals and direct outreach matter more, and recruiters notice CVs that read like a chatbot wrote them.

## What hiring managers look for (the guide's summary)

Evaluation frameworks; cost and latency reasoning; trade-off fluency; observability; safety and guardrails; solid Python and data structures; and honesty about gaps ("I need a hint" beats bluffing). The rest of this series trains exactly these.
