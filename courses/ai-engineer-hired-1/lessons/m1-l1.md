# Start here: how this series gets you hired

*Get hired as an AI engineer* is three courses, built from what companies actually post and ask in 2025 and 2026, not from opinions:

| Part | What you'll have at the end |
|---|---|
| **1. The market, your first project and your CV** (this one) | a clear target, a deployed RAG project with evaluations, a CV and LinkedIn built around it, and a job search running |
| **2. The coding interview intensive** | 12 weeks of LeetCode patterns, ML coding from scratch, implementation rounds and mock interviews |
| **3. AI interviews, system design and a production project** | the theory interviewers ask, AI system design, a deployed agent project, a take-home simulation and a full mock loop |

## How to take it

- **Run parts 1 and 2 together:** about 6 hours a week on part 1 and 10 on part 2 for 12 weeks. Applications start in week 8 of part 1, while you keep training.
- **Every module has proof:** something you upload (code, a CV, a recording) and a written answer. Treat the written answers as interview practice: write them as you'd say them.
- **The projects grow step by step** across the modules. Each step ends with a commit and a CV bullet, so your CV improves as the course goes.
- **Prerequisites:** Python you're comfortable with (the library's *Python: the full course*), and ideally an API framework (*FastAPI*). Machine learning theory helps but isn't required to start.

## A word of honesty

No course guarantees a job. What this one does is remove guesswork: you'll build what hiring managers ask for, practise what interviewers test, and measure your progress. The rest is volume and persistence, which module 8 turns into a weekly routine.
