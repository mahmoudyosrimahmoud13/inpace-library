# Run your search like a pipeline

Track every application in a sheet: **company, role, link, date, channel (applied / referral / outreach), contact, stage, next step and date, notes**. Each Friday, count your numbers.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Many applications, almost no replies | CV or targeting | tailor the top third of the CV to each post; get a referral; apply to roles that fit your level |
| Recruiter screens but no technical rounds | story or expectations | practise your pitch (module 9); research salary ranges |
| Technical rounds but no offers | interview skills | parts 2 and 3; ask for feedback; more mocks |

## Weekly targets you control

You can't control replies; you can control inputs. A sustainable start: **10 tailored applications, 5 personal outreach messages and 1 public post a week**, plus your study hours. Adjust after 4 weeks of data. The field guide notes that **top candidates accept offers within 2 to 3 weeks**: when one company moves to final rounds, tell the others and try to cluster your final interviews.
