# Referrals and outreach that get answers

A referral moves your CV to the top of the pile; a short, specific message gets more replies than a long one. Templates to adapt (keep them under 100 words):

**To an engineer at the company (asking for a referral):**

> Hi [name], I saw you work on [team/product] at [company]. I'm applying for the [role] ([link]). I built [DocAnswer], a RAG assistant over [X] that reached [0.88 hit rate@5] with evals in CI ([demo]). Would you be open to referring me, or to a 15-minute chat about the team? Thank you either way.

**To a startup founder or hiring manager:**

> Hi [name], [product] caught my attention because [specific reason]. I build LLM apps with a focus on evaluation: [one line, one number, link]. If you're hiring an AI engineer, I'd love to show how I'd approach [a real problem they likely have].

Personalise the first line every time; generic messages are ignored. Follow up once after a week, then let it go.
