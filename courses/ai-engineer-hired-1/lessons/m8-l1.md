# Where AI jobs are

| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn Jobs and company career pages | set alerts for "AI engineer", "LLM engineer", "applied AI", "ML engineer"; many companies post on Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby boards |
| [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/) and [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/) (Y Combinator) | startups, often open to strong projects over long experience |
| Hacker News "Who is hiring?" | a monthly thread on news.ycombinator.com with many remote and AI roles |
| Remote boards ([We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/), [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/)) | check time-zone and country rules before applying |
| Local boards: [Wuzzuf](https://wuzzuf.net/) (Egypt), [Bayt](https://www.bayt.com/) (Arab region) | good for your first AI role locally |
| Freelance and contract work | real clients and real numbers for your CV |

## The hidden market

Many roles are filled through people before they're widely posted, and the field guide notes **network-based hiring is growing** as AI-written applications flood pipelines. Candidates it quotes found that messages to founders and hiring managers worked well for startups, and that online hackathons and posting their work built their network faster than courses.
