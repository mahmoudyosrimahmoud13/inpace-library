## 1. Income taxes and deferred tax

Accounting profit and taxable profit differ because the two rulebooks differ. The mismatch creates
**deferred tax**.

| 🔀 Difference | 🧠 Meaning | 📝 Result |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary | Reverses later (e.g. faster tax depreciation) | Deferred tax asset or liability |
| Permanent | Never reverses (fines, tax-exempt income) | No deferred tax; changes the effective rate |

- **Deferred tax liability** = you will pay tax later.
- **Deferred tax asset** = you will pay less tax later (warranty provision, tax loss carryforward),
  reduced by a valuation allowance if recovery is doubtful.
- Effective tax rate = tax expense ÷ pre-tax accounting income.

**Worked example:** accounting profit 100,000; tax depreciation exceeds book depreciation by 20,000;
tax rate 25%. Taxable profit = 80,000, tax paid = 20,000, tax expense = 25,000, so a deferred tax
liability of **5,000** builds up.

## 2. Equity transactions

| 🧾 Transaction | 📝 Effect |
|---|---|
| Issue shares above par | Par to share capital, excess to share premium |
| Small stock dividend (under 20-25%) | Capitalise at **market value** |
| Large stock dividend (over 20-25%) | Capitalise at **par value** |
| Stock split | No entry; more shares, lower par |
| Treasury stock (cost method) | Contra-equity; no gain or loss on resale |
| Cash dividend | Dr Retained earnings / Cr Dividends payable |

Stock dividends and splits change neither total equity nor anyone's ownership percentage — they just
slice the same pie thinner.

## 3. Revenue recognition (ASC 606 / IFRS 15)

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  S1[1 Contract] --> S2[2 Obligations]
  S2 --> S3[3 Price]
  S3 --> S4[4 Allocate]
  S4 --> S5[5 Recognise]
```

| 🪜 Step | 🧠 What you do | 💡 Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Contract | Confirm an enforceable deal with probable collection | Signed order |
| 2 Obligations | Identify distinct promises | Phone + 1 year support |
| 3 Price | Include variable consideration only if highly likely to hold | Fixed fee + expected bonus |
| 4 Allocate | Use stand-alone selling prices | Split $900 between phone and support |
| 5 Recognise | Over time, or at a point in time | Phone on delivery, support over the year |

**Worked example:** a $900 bundle with a phone worth $600 alone and support worth $400 alone. Allocate
900 × (600/1,000) = **$540** to the phone at delivery and 900 × (400/1,000) = **$360** to support
over the year. Revenue follows the promises, not the cash.
