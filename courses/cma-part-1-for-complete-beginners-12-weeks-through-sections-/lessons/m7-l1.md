## 1. What a variance is

A variance is the gap between what something should have cost at the actual output and what it did
cost. You only need three quantities:

| 🧩 Term | 🧠 Meaning |
|---|---|
| SQ / SH | Standard quantity or hours **allowed for the output actually produced** |
| AQ / AH | Actual quantity or hours used |
| SP / SR, AP / AR | Standard and actual price or rate |

**U** means unfavourable (cost higher or profit lower than standard); **F** means favourable. A
favourable variance is not always good — cheap material may create waste and rework later.

## 2. Case: Cairo Widgets

| 🧱 Standard cost card | Standard | Rate | Per unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct material | 2 kg | $5.00/kg | $10.00 |
| Direct labour | 0.5 hour | $20.00/hr | $10.00 |
| Variable overhead | 0.5 hour | $4.00/hr | $2.00 |
| Fixed overhead (normal capacity 1,500 units) | 1 unit | $20.00/unit | $20.00 |
| **Total** | | | **$42.00** |

Actuals: produced 1,000 units; material 2,100 kg at $5.20; labour 480 hours at $21.00; variable
overhead $2,100; fixed overhead $31,000. Standard allowance for 1,000 units = **2,000 kg and 500
hours**.

## 3. Material variances

> **Price** = AQ × (AP − SP) · **Usage** = SP × (AQ − SQ)

| Variance | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Material price | 2,100 × (5.20 − 5.00) | **$420 U** |
| Material usage | 5.00 × (2,100 − 2,000) | **$500 U** |
| **Total** | actual 10,920 − standard 10,000 | **$920 U** |

The price variance is usually measured on the quantity **purchased** (the purchasing manager's
responsibility) and the usage variance on the quantity **used** (the production manager's). That is
why the two are reported separately.

## 4. Labour variances

> **Rate** = AH × (AR − SR) · **Efficiency** = SR × (AH − SH)

| Variance | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Labour rate | 480 × (21 − 20) | **$480 U** |
| Labour efficiency | 20 × (480 − 500) | **$400 F** |
| **Net** | | **$80 U** |

Reading: the workers cost more per hour but finished faster. That pattern usually means
higher-skilled (more expensive) labour was used — one decision, two opposite variances.
