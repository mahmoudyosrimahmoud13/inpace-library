## 1. The shape of Part 1

Your guide describes Part 1 as **100 multiple-choice questions plus case-based questions (CBQs)**.
The MCQs carry most of the marks; a CBQ gives one business scenario and several linked questions.

| 📘 Section | 🧠 Name | ⚖️ Weight | 🧠 Beginner's view |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | External Financial Reporting Decisions | 15% | Vocabulary and measurement rules |
| B | Planning, Budgeting and Forecasting | 20% | Number-building, very learnable |
| C | Performance Management | 20% | Variances, transfer pricing, scorecards |
| D | Cost Management | 15% | How product cost is built |
| E | Internal Controls | 15% | Concepts, high marks per hour |
| F | Technology and Analytics | 15% | Understanding, little maths |

**B and C together are 40%.** That is why your calendar gives them more than a third of the time.

## 2. How the guide is built (so you stop feeling lost)

| 🎨 Feature in the guide | 🔎 What it is | ✅ How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| "By the end of this unit you must be able to" | The official outcomes | Read first, tick off last |
| Key definition | The wording examiners expect | Copy into your notebook |
| Formula box | Maths to memorise | Add to your formula sheet |
| Worked example | Numbers done step by step | Redo it with the book closed |
| Exam tip / common trap | Shortcuts and warnings | Highlight — they save marks |
| Exam heat ●●●●● | How often the unit appears (5 = almost always) | Spend time in proportion |

## 3. Your twelve weeks at a glance

| 📅 Weeks | 📚 What you cover | 🎯 Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations and accounting basics | Speak the language |
| 2-4 | Section A (Units 01-03) | Read a set of statements |
| 5-7 | Section B (Units 04-08) | Build a full master budget |
| 8-9 | Section C (Units 09-11) | Calculate and explain variances |
| 10 | Section D (Units 12-13) | Cost a product under two systems |
| 11 | Sections D and E (Units 14-17) | Allocate overhead; design controls |
| 12 | Section F plus revision | Analytics, formulas and exam day |

Each week is four hours. A pattern that works:

| ⏱️ Block | 🎯 Activity | 🧠 Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0-60 min | Read the guide pages shown above the lesson | First contact |
| 60-140 min | Read the lesson text and redo every worked example on paper | Practice beats re-reading |
| 140-200 min | Do the module quiz, then read the explanations | Retrieval is what sticks |
| 200-240 min | Write your one-page summary of the module | Writing forces understanding |

## 4. Five habits that decide the result

1. **Write every worked example by hand once.** Understanding without practice disappears under
   exam pressure.
2. **Keep one formula sheet from day one.** By week 12 it becomes your personal revision bible.
3. **Label every variance U or F as you write it.** Half of all lost marks are labels, not maths.
4. **Judge your quiz answers, not your quiz score.** A lucky guess teaches nothing.
5. **Study the traps, not just the rules.** The guide flags them for a reason.

## 5. What "ready" feels like

| 🎯 Target | 📏 Test |
|---|---|
| Recall | You can state the main formulas from memory |
| Explain | You can describe a concept to a friend in two sentences |
| Apply | You can build a budget, a variance set or an EUP schedule unaided |
| Judge | You can say what a number means for a manager |
