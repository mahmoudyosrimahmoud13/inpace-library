## 1. The family of methods

| 🧾 Method | ❓ Answers | 🧠 How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Incremental | What changed since last year? | Last year's figure ± a % |
| Zero-based | Should we spend this at all? | Every activity justified from zero, ranked in decision packages |
| Activity-based | What drives the cost? | Budget each activity using its cost driver |
| Flexible | What should it cost at the actual volume? | Fix fixed costs, flex variable costs |
| Rolling | What are the next 12 months? | Add a new period as each one passes |
| Kaizen | How much better must we be? | Build continuous improvement into each period |

| 🧾 Method | 👍 | 👎 |
|---|---|---|
| Incremental | Fast and cheap | Bakes in past waste |
| Zero-based | Finds waste | Slow, tiring, can hurt morale |
| Activity-based | Links cost to its real driver | Needs good activity data |
| Flexible | Fair comparison | Only valid inside the relevant range |
| Rolling | Always current | Never finished |
| Kaizen | Drives improvement | Endless targets tire people |

## 2. The flexible budget (the most examinable idea here)

> Flexible cost = (variable cost per unit × **actual** units) + budgeted fixed cost

| Item | Static (10,000 units) | Flexible (12,000 units) | Actual (12,000 units) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | 200,000 | 240,000 | 234,000 |
| Variable costs ($6/unit) | 60,000 | 72,000 | 76,000 |
| Fixed costs | 40,000 | 40,000 | 42,000 |
| **Total costs** | 100,000 | 112,000 | 118,000 |

**Actual vs flexible** shows real overspending: variable 4,000 unfavourable, fixed 2,000 unfavourable,
sales 6,000 unfavourable. **Flexible vs static** shows the volume effect: sales up 40,000, variable
costs up 12,000 → profit up 28,000 because more was sold. That split — volume versus spending — is
the whole point of flexing.

## 3. Rolling, kaizen and zero-based numbers

**Rolling:** keep 12 months at all times; if you re-plan quarterly, you re-plan four times a year.

**Kaizen:** cut cost 3% a year from $25 → $24.25, then $23.52, then $22.82. If the market price is $30
and a 20% margin ($6) is required, the target cost is $24 — kaizen reaches it in year one.

**Zero-based:** rank decision packages and fund down the list until the cap is reached.

| Package | Cost $ | Rank | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website maintenance | 20,000 | 1 | 20,000 |
| Social media ads | 30,000 | 2 | 50,000 |
| Trade show | 45,000 | 3 | 95,000 |
| Brand campaign | 60,000 | 4 | 155,000 |

With a $95,000 cap, the brand campaign is not funded at all, even if it existed last year.
