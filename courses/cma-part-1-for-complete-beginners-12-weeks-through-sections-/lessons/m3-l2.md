## 1. Investments in debt and equity securities

| 🏷️ Classification | 📊 Measured at | 💰 Where value changes go |
|---|---|---|
| Trading (FVTPL) | Fair value | Net income |
| Available for sale (US GAAP) | Fair value | OCI |
| Fair value through OCI (IFRS debt) | Fair value | OCI, recycled on sale |
| Held to maturity (debt only) | Amortised cost | No fair-value changes |
| Equity method (20-50% influence) | Cost ± share of investee profit | Net income, one line |

**Worked example:** buy shares for 100,000; year-end fair value 118,000. If trading, profit rises
18,000. If available for sale, OCI rises 18,000 and profit is untouched. Same facts, two outcomes —
a favourite MCQ.

## 2. The cost of PP&E

Include purchase price, delivery, installation, testing and the estimate of dismantling costs.
Exclude staff training and general overhead. Land is never depreciated.

| 📉 Method | 🧮 Formula | 🧠 Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Straight line | (Cost − residual) ÷ life | Equal every year |
| Units of production | (Cost − residual) × units used ÷ total units | Follows use |
| Double declining | 2 × (1 ÷ life) × opening book value | Big early, small later |

**Worked example:** machine $50,000, residual $5,000, life 5 years.

| Year | Straight line | Double declining |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9,000 | 20,000 |
| 2 | 9,000 | 12,000 |
| 3 | 9,000 | 7,200 |

Depreciation allocates cost; it does not value the asset. **Disposal:** compare proceeds with carrying
amount (cost − accumulated depreciation); the difference is a gain or loss in profit.

## 3. Why the choice matters

| 🎯 Decision | 📈 Effect on early profit | 📉 Effect later |
|---|---|---|
| Accelerated depreciation | Lower | Higher |
| Longer useful life | Higher | Spread thinner |
| Capitalising an item instead of expensing | Higher | Higher depreciation afterwards |

A CBQ often asks what happens to profit and to total assets if the useful life is extended: profit
rises, assets stay higher, and the depreciation expense falls each year.
