# Giving and defending an opinion

Two flatmates, Marta and Karim, argue about working from home. Notice how they give opinions, agree, disagree and link their ideas.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Office or home?**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Marta:** Mi empresa quiere que volvamos a la oficina cinco días a la semana. Me parece un error. | My company wants us to go back to the office five days a week. I think it's a mistake. |
| **Karim:** ¿Por qué? Yo creo que en la oficina se trabaja mejor. | Why? I think people work better in the office. |
| **Marta:** No estoy de acuerdo. En casa me concentro más y, además, no pierdo una hora en el metro. | I don't agree. At home I concentrate more and, besides, I don't waste an hour on the metro. |
| **Karim:** En eso tienes razón. Sin embargo, echo de menos a mis compañeros cuando trabajo solo. | You're right about that. However, I miss my colleagues when I work alone. |
| **Marta:** Bueno, depende de la persona. Por un lado, la oficina es buena para el equipo; por otro, en casa somos más productivos. | Well, it depends on the person. On the one hand, the office is good for the team; on the other, at home we're more productive. |
| **Karim:** Entonces, lo ideal sería un modelo mixto: dos días en la oficina y tres en casa. | So the ideal would be a hybrid model: two days in the office and three at home. |
| **Marta:** ¡Exacto! Por fin estamos de acuerdo. | Exactly! At last we agree. |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Creo que... / Pienso que... | I think that... |
| Me parece que... / Me parece un error. | It seems to me that... / I think it's a mistake. |
| En mi opinión... | In my opinion... |
| (No) estoy de acuerdo. | I (don't) agree. |
| Tienes razón. / En eso tienes razón. | You're right. / You're right about that. |
| Depende. | It depends. |
| además | besides, also |
| sin embargo | however |
| por un lado... por otro... | on the one hand... on the other... |
| Echo de menos a... | I miss... |
| Lo ideal sería... | The ideal would be... |
| Por fin. | At last. |

## Say it right

**Sounding fluent: fillers.** Real speakers fill pauses with small words while they think: *bueno...*, *pues...*, *a ver...*, *o sea...* (I mean), *es que...* (the thing is), *¿sabes?*. Using a few of them instead of silence (or *ehhh* in your own language) makes your Spanish sound natural and gives you time. Don't overdo it: one per sentence or two.
