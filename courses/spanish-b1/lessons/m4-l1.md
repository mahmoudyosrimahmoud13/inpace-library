# Imagining a different life

At dinner, friends play a game: what would you do if you won the lottery? Listen for the conditional (-ía) and si + a past subjunctive (tuviera, fuera).

## Listen first: the conversation

**If I won the lottery...**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lucía:** Vamos a jugar: ¿qué haríais si os tocara la lotería? | Let's play: what would you do if you won the lottery? |
| **Karim:** Yo dejaría mi trabajo y viajaría por todo el mundo durante un año. | I'd leave my job and travel around the whole world for a year. |
| **Marta:** Pues yo no dejaría de trabajar. Me compraría una casa con jardín y ayudaría a mi familia. | Well, I wouldn't stop working. I'd buy a house with a garden and help my family. |
| **Lucía:** ¡Qué sensata! ¿Y tú, Omar? | How sensible! And you, Omar? |
| **Omar:** Si tuviera mucho dinero, abriría un restaurante de comida egipcia aquí en Madrid. | If I had a lot of money, I'd open an Egyptian restaurant here in Madrid. |
| **Karim:** ¡Me encantaría! Yo iría todos los días a comer koshari. | I'd love that! I'd go every day to eat koshari. |
| **Lucía:** Bueno, si encontramos dinero, te ayudaremos. Pero hoy pagas tú la cena, ¿vale? | Well, if we find some money, we'll help you. But tonight you're paying for dinner, OK? |
| **Omar:** ¡Ja, ja! Si me tocara la lotería, os invitaría a todos. | Ha ha! If I won the lottery, I'd treat you all. |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ¿Qué harías si...? | What would you do if...? |
| Si me tocara la lotería... | If I won the lottery... |
| Si tuviera tiempo / dinero... | If I had time / money... |
| Si fuera rico/a... | If I were rich... |
| Me gustaría... | I'd like to... |
| Me encantaría. | I'd love that. |
| ¿Podrías...? | Could you...? |
| Deberías... | You should... |
| Yo en tu lugar... | If I were you... |
| ¡Qué sensato/a! | How sensible! |
| Te invito. | It's on me. / I'm paying. |
| ¿Vale? | OK? / Alright? |

## Say it right

**Two easy-to-confuse forms:** the imperfect *comía* (I used to eat) and the conditional *comería* (I would eat). The difference is *-er-* / *-ir-* in the middle: *vivía / viviría*, *tenía / tendría*. Read these pairs aloud, stressing the í: *trabajaba / trabajaría*, *iba / iría*, *hacía / haría*.
