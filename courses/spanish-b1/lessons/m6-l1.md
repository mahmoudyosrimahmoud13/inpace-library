# Passing on news

Omar tells Lucía what the company said after the interview. Listen to how the verbs change when we report someone's words.

## Listen first: the conversation

**Good news**

| Spanish | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lucía:** ¿Te han llamado de la empresa? | Has the company called you? |
| **Omar:** ¡Sí, esta mañana! La directora me dijo que les había gustado mucho la entrevista. | Yes, this morning! The director told me they had really liked the interview. |
| **Lucía:** ¡Qué bien! ¿Y qué más te dijo? | Great! And what else did she say? |
| **Omar:** Dijo que querían ofrecerme el puesto y que empezaría el uno de marzo. | She said they wanted to offer me the job and that I would start on the first of March. |
| **Lucía:** ¡Enhorabuena! ¿Le preguntaste por el sueldo? | Congratulations! Did you ask her about the salary? |
| **Omar:** Sí, le pregunté si podíamos hablar del sueldo y me contestó que me enviaría la oferta por escrito. | Yes, I asked her if we could talk about the salary and she answered that she would send me the offer in writing. |
| **Lucía:** ¿Y qué te dijo de las vacaciones? | And what did she tell you about holidays? |
| **Omar:** Me explicó que tenían veintitrés días al año y me pidió que le confirmara mi decisión antes del viernes. | She explained that they had twenty-three days a year and asked me to confirm my decision before Friday. |
| **Lucía:** Pues esto hay que celebrarlo. ¡Esta noche invitas tú! | Well, we have to celebrate this. Tonight you're paying! |

## Shadow it

Play the conversation in your head or read it aloud **three times**: first slowly, then at normal speed, then without looking at the meaning column. If you have the audio from the links below, pause after each line and copy the speaker's rhythm and melody, not just the words. This is called **shadowing**, and it trains your ear and your mouth together.

## Words and phrases to keep

| Phrase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Me dijo que... | She told me that... |
| Le pregunté si... | I asked her if... |
| Me contestó que... | She answered that... |
| Me explicó que... | She explained that... |
| Me pidió que + subjuntivo | She asked me to... |
| ¡Enhorabuena! / ¡Felicidades! | Congratulations! |
| la oferta por escrito | the offer in writing |
| Hay que celebrarlo. | We have to celebrate it. |
| Según ella... | According to her... |
| Parece que... | It seems that... |
| ¿Te has enterado de que...? | Have you heard that...? |
| ¡No me digas! | You don't say! / No way! |

## Say it right

**Telling a story aloud.** When you report a conversation, change your voice slightly for the other person's words, pause before the important part, and stress the news: *Me dijo que... ¡me ofrecían el PUESTO!* Practise retelling the dialogue as Omar, as if to a friend on the phone, with pauses and emphasis.
