# can و can't

للقدرة والسماح والطلب. **can** لا يتغير مع he/she، وبعده فعل بدون to وبدون s:

```text
I can swim.       She can speak three languages.      He can't (cannot) drive.
Can you help me?  Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Can I open the window? (طلب إذن)
```

She cans swim ❌ She can to swim ❌ She can swims ❌ ← **She can swim** ✅

## تمرين

1. There ___ two banks in my street. 2. ___ there a hospital near here? 3. The cat is ___ the table (تحت).
4. He ___ (can / play) the guitar. 5. ___ you speak English?

## الإجابات

1. are 2. Is 3. under 4. can play 5. Can
