# اختبار تجريبي A1

## القراءة: اقرأ ثم أجب

*Hi! My name's Nour. I'm 19 and I'm from Mansoura, but I live in Cairo now. I'm a student at Cairo University. I
study medicine. I live with my aunt and my cousin. Every day I get up at 6:30 and I take the metro to university.
I love my classes, but they're very long! At the weekend I meet my friends. Last Saturday we went to the cinema and
we watched a comedy. It was really funny.*

1. Where is Nour from? 2. What does she study? 3. Who does she live with? 4. How does she go to university?
5. What did she do last Saturday?

## الكتابة

اكتب 50 كلمة عن نفسك مثل Nour: من أنت، أين تعيش، روتينك، وماذا فعلت آخر عطلة.

## الإجابات

1. She's from Mansoura. 2. She studies medicine. 3. She lives with her aunt and her cousin. 4. She takes the metro.
5. She went to the cinema with her friends and watched a comedy.
