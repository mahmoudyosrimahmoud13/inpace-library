# أين؟ ومن؟ كلمات السؤال

| كلمة السؤال | المعنى | مثال |
|---|---|---|
| What | ماذا / ما | What's this? It's a pen. |
| Who | من | Who is she? She's my sister. |
| Where | أين | Where is the bank? It's next to the hotel. |
| When | متى | When is your birthday? It's in May. |
| How | كيف | How are you? |
| How old | كم عمر | How old is he? |
| How much | كم سعر | How much is it? It's 50 pounds. |

الترتيب: **كلمة السؤال + is/are + الفاعل**: *Where **is** the station?*

## تمرين: رتّب الكلمات

1. is / where / bathroom / the ?
2. your / who / teacher / is ?
3. are / how / you ?

## الإجابات

1. Where is the bathroom? 2. Who is your teacher? 3. How are you?
