# مراجعة كل القواعد في صفحة

| القاعدة | مثال | الخطأ الشائع |
|---|---|---|
| to be | I **am** a student. | I student. |
| he/she + s | She **works**. | She work. |
| do/does | **Does** he **like**...? | Does he likes...? |
| a/an | **an** engineer | I'm engineer. |
| صفة قبل الاسم | a **big** house | a house big |
| there is/are | **There is** a bank. | It has a bank. |
| can | She can **swim**. | can to swim / cans |
| الآن | I'm **reading**. | I reading. |
| الماضي | I **went**. / I didn't **go**. | I didn't went. |
| ago | two years **ago** | before two years |
| العمر | I'm 20. | I have 20 years. |
