# النفي والسؤال: do و does

## النفي

```text
I don't (do not) like fish.
She doesn't (does not) like fish.     ← بعد doesn't الفعل بدون s
```

She doesn't likes ❌ لأن s انتقلت لـ does.

## السؤال

```text
Do you work here?        Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does he speak English?   Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
Where do you live?       I live in Alexandria.
What time does she start work?   She starts at 9.
```

## الوقت

```text
What time is it?   It's seven o'clock (7:00).   It's half past seven (7:30).
It's quarter past seven (7:15).   It's quarter to eight (7:45).   It's 7:20 (seven twenty).
```

## روتين يومي، اقرأه بصوت عالٍ

*I get up at 7. I have breakfast and I go to work by metro. I start work at 9 and I finish at 5. In the evening I
watch TV or I go to the gym. I go to bed at 11.*

## تمرين

1. She ___ (live) in Giza. 2. ___ you like tea? 3. He ___ (not / watch) TV. 4. ___ she study English? 5. My father ___ (go) to work by car.

## الإجابات

1. lives 2. Do 3. doesn't watch 4. Does 5. goes
