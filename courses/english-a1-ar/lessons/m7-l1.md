# was و were

ماضي to be:

```text
I was / he was / she was / it was       you were / we were / they were
I was at home yesterday.        They were in Alexandria last week.
I wasn't tired.                 Were you at work? Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
```

## كلمات الماضي

```text
yesterday   last night   last week   last year   two days ago   in 2020   this morning
```

**ago** تأتي **بعد** المدة: *three years ago* (منذ ثلاث سنوات).
