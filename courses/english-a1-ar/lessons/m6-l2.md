# الأكل: معدود وغير معدود

- **معدود** (countable): apple → apples, egg → eggs. نقول a/an ونجمع.
- **غير معدود** (uncountable): water, rice, bread, milk, money, information. لا a/an ولا جمع.

```text
an apple, three apples        some water (لا: a water / waters)
some rice                     a bottle of water, a kilo of rice, a piece of bread
```

## How much / How many

```text
How many eggs do you need?     (معدود)
How much milk do you need?     (غير معدود)
How much is this? It's 20 pounds.  (السعر)
```

## في المطعم والمحل

```text
Can I have a coffee, please?          I'd like a chicken sandwich, please.
How much is it?                       Can I pay by card?
Here you are. / Thank you.            Anything else? No, that's all, thanks.
```

**I'd like** (= I would like) أكثر أدبًا من *I want*.
