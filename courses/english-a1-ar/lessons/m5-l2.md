# حروف الجر للمكان والاتجاهات

| الحرف | المعنى | مثال |
|---|---|---|
| in | في (داخل) | in the box, in Cairo |
| on | على | on the table |
| under | تحت | under the bed |
| next to | بجانب | next to the bank |
| between | بين | between the café and the shop |
| opposite | أمام (في الجهة المقابلة) | opposite the station |
| in front of | أمام | in front of the school |
| behind | خلف | behind the door |

## الاتجاهات

```text
Excuse me, where is the station?
Go straight on. Turn left. Turn right at the traffic lights.
It's on your left, next to the bank. It's about five minutes' walk.
```

صيغة الأمر (imperative) = الفعل وحده: *Turn left. Don't turn right. Please sit down.*
