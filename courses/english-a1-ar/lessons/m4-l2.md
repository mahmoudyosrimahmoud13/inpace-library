# my, your... و 's

| الضمير | الملكية | مثال |
|---|---|---|
| I | my | my phone |
| you | your | your book |
| he | his | his car |
| she | her | her bag |
| it | its | its name |
| we | our | our house |
| they | their | their children |

**its** (ملكية) غير **it's** (= it is).

## 's للملكية

```text
Ahmed's car (سيارة أحمد)      my mother's name      the teacher's desk
my parents' house (جمع ينتهي بـ s: نضع ' فقط)
```

## العائلة

```text
father  mother  parents  brother  sister  son  daughter  husband  wife
grandfather  grandmother  uncle  aunt  cousin  nephew  niece
```

```text
This is my family. My father's name is Hassan. He's 55. My mother is a teacher.
I have one brother and two sisters. My brother's name is Karim.
```
