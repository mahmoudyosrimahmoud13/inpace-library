# there is / there are

لتقول **يوجد**:

```text
There is a bank near my house.          There's a problem.
There are two supermarkets in my street.
Is there a pharmacy here?  Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
Are there any restaurants near here?  Yes, there are some. / No, there aren't any.
```

- **some** في الإثبات، **any** في النفي والسؤال (مع الجمع).
- لا تقل It has a bank near my house للتعبير عن "يوجد"، قل **There is**.
