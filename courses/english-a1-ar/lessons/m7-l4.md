# تمرين على الماضي

1. I ___ (go) to Aswan last year. 2. She ___ (not / like) the film. 3. ___ you ___ (call) your mother?
4. We ___ (be) at the beach yesterday. 5. He ___ (buy) a new phone two weeks ___.

## الإجابات

1. went 2. didn't like 3. Did / call 4. were 5. bought / ago

## نطق ed

ed لها ثلاثة أصوات: **/t/** بعد p, k, s, sh, ch (worked)، **/d/** بعد الباقي (played)، و**/ɪd/** بعد t و d
(wanted, needed). لا تقل "work-ed" بمقطع زائد.
