# like + ing، وتمرين شامل

بعد like, love, hate, enjoy غالبًا **ing**:

```text
I like reading.     She loves cooking.     He hates waiting.     Do you enjoy swimming?
```

## تمرين

1. Look! It ___ (rain). 2. I usually ___ (drink) tea, but today I ___ (drink) coffee. 3. How ___ sugar do you want?
4. How ___ bananas do we have? 5. Can I ___ a tea, please? 6. She enjoys ___ (read).

## الإجابات

1. 's raining 2. drink / 'm drinking 3. much 4. many 5. have 6. reading
