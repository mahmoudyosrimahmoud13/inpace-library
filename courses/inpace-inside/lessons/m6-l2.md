# Drawing in Python, and a sky that follows the clock

The island, Tempo, plants, friends and the villain are **SVG strings built in Python** (`garden.py`,
`garden_art.py`, `mascot.py`). No image files, crisp at any size, recolourable by string replacement (your turtle's
shell and scarf colours), and easy to test (`assert "isle-trophy" in svg`).

`sky_at(when)` computes sunrise and sunset from the day of the year (about 7:00-17:00 in December, 5:00-19:00
in June), blends sky colours between keyframes, and moves the sun or moon along an arc.

## The phone's time zone

Python inside the Android app often thinks it's in UTC. `clock.py` fixes it two ways: the app passes
`INPACE_TZ_MINUTES` at start, and every page sends a `tz` cookie with the browser's offset; the middleware calls
`time.tzset()` with a POSIX TZ string. A small, very real cross-platform bug.
