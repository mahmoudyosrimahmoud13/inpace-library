# Contribution margin and break-even

## Contribution margin

Each cake sells for 120 and adds 50 of variable cost. The other **70** "contributes" first to covering fixed costs, then to profit.

> **Contribution margin per unit = Price − Variable cost per unit** = 120 − 50 = **70**
> **Contribution margin ratio = CM ÷ Price** = 70 ÷ 120 = **58.3%**

The contribution format income statement (450 cakes):

| | Total | Per cake |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | 54,000 | 120 |
| Variable costs | (22,500) | (50) |
| **Contribution margin** | **31,500** | **70** |
| Fixed costs | (21,000) | |
| **Operating profit** | **10,500** | |

This layout is the basis of almost every decision in this course.

## Break-even point

Break-even is where profit is zero: contribution margin exactly covers fixed costs.

> **Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ CM per unit** = 21,000 ÷ 70 = **300 cakes**
> **Break-even sales = Fixed costs ÷ CM ratio** = 21,000 ÷ 0.5833 = **36,000**

Check: 300 × 120 = 36,000 sales; variable 15,000; CM 21,000; fixed 21,000; profit 0. ✓

Every cake after the 300th adds **70** of profit. Every cake short of it loses 70.

## Costs and revenue at each volume

| Cakes | 0 | 100 | 200 | **300** | 400 | 500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | 0 | 12,000 | 24,000 | **36,000** | 48,000 | 60,000 |
| Total cost (21,000 + 50 per cake) | 21,000 | 26,000 | 31,000 | **36,000** | 41,000 | 46,000 |
| Profit | −21,000 | −14,000 | −7,000 | **0** | 7,000 | 14,000 |

Sales and total cost meet at 300 cakes. Below that: loss. Above it: profit. Drawn as a chart (the *CVP graph*), the two lines cross there.

## What moves break-even

| Change | Break-even |
|---|---|
| Price up | Down (bigger CM) |
| Variable cost up (flour prices rise) | Up |
| Fixed costs up (new staff, higher rent) | Up |

Example: flour prices push ingredients to 41. Variable cost 56, CM 64, break-even = 21,000 ÷ 64 = **329 cakes**. Nour needs 29 more cakes a month just to stay even, or a price rise.

## Try it

A phone accessories stall: price 50, variable cost 30, fixed costs 8,000 a month. Find CM per unit, CM ratio and break-even in units and sales.

**Answers:** CM 20; ratio 40%; break-even **400 units** or **20,000** of sales.
