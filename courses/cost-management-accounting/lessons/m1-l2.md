# How costs behave: splitting mixed costs

To plan, you need every cost as **fixed part + variable part per unit**:

> **Total cost = Fixed cost + (Variable cost per unit × Units)**

For Nour, total monthly cost = 21,000 + 50 × cakes.

## Mixed costs: the high-low method

Electricity has a fixed charge plus usage. Nour's records:

| Month | Oven hours | Electricity bill |
|---|---|---|
| Jan | 300 | 3,600 |
| Feb | 200 (lowest) | 3,000 |
| Mar | 450 | 4,500 |
| Apr | 500 (highest) | 4,800 |

Take the **highest** and **lowest activity** months:

1. Variable cost per hour = (4,800 − 3,000) ÷ (500 − 200) = 1,800 ÷ 300 = **6 per oven hour**
2. Fixed part = total − variable part at either point = 3,000 − 200 × 6 = **1,800 a month**

So electricity = **1,800 + 6 × oven hours**. Check March: 1,800 + 450 × 6 = 4,500. ✓

Note: pick the months with the highest and lowest **activity** (hours), not the highest and lowest bills.

## Better: a trend line

High-low uses only two points, which might be unusual months. With more data, a spreadsheet's trend line (regression) uses all points. In Excel: `=SLOPE(bills, hours)` gives the variable rate and `=INTERCEPT(bills, hours)` the fixed part. The Excel course shows how.

## The relevant range

"Fixed" holds only within a normal range. If Nour sells 900 cakes a month she needs a second oven and another baker: fixed costs **step up**. Every plan is valid only inside its range.

## Why behavior matters

Look at cost per cake at different volumes:

| Cakes | Fixed cost | Variable cost | Total | Cost per cake |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 21,000 | 15,000 | 36,000 | 120 |
| 400 | 21,000 | 20,000 | 41,000 | 102.5 |
| 500 | 21,000 | 25,000 | 46,000 | 92 |

The same cake "costs" 120 or 92 depending on volume. That's why a single "cost per unit" can mislead: always ask at what volume, and which part is fixed.

## Try it

A delivery van's monthly costs: 800 km → 5,200; 1,500 km → 7,300; 1,200 km → 6,400. Use high-low to find the cost formula, then estimate the cost for 1,000 km.

**Answer:** variable = (7,300 − 5,200) ÷ (1,500 − 800) = **3 per km**; fixed = 5,200 − 800 × 3 = **2,800**. At 1,000 km: 2,800 + 3,000 = **5,800**.
