# Going further: modern options

- **Multivariate Gaussian** captures correlations between features (instead of multiplying independent ones).
- **Isolation Forest**: anomalies are easier to isolate with random splits. Strong, fast default for tabular data.
- **Autoencoders**: train to reconstruct normal data; a large reconstruction error suggests an anomaly.
- In production, combine with rules and human review, and watch alert volume: too many false alarms and people ignore
  them.
