# Family C, part 1: three sound patterns

This is the biggest family. The good news: the past and the participle are **the same word**, so you learn two forms, not three.

## -ought / -aught

| Base | Past simple | Past participle | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **buy** | bought | bought | I bought a new phone. |
| **bring** | brought | brought | Have you brought your laptop? |
| **think** | thought | thought | I thought you were at home. |
| **fight** | fought | fought | The two brothers fought over the TV. |
| **seek** | sought | sought | They sought help from a lawyer. |
| **teach** | taught | taught | She taught me how to swim. |
| **catch** | caught | caught | We caught the last bus. |

**Tip:** *-ought* and *-aught* sound the same (like *sport*). Verbs with **a** in the base (*teach, catch*) take **-aught**; the rest take **-ought**.

## -d becomes -t

| Base | Past simple | Past participle | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **send** | sent | sent | I sent you an email. |
| **spend** | spent | spent | We spent a week in Alexandria. |
| **build** | built | built | This house was built in 1990. |
| **lend** | lent | lent | Can you lend me a pen? I lent him my car. |
| **bend** | bent | bent | He bent down to pick it up. |

## Long ee becomes short e + t

| Base | Past simple | Past participle | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **keep** | kept | kept | She kept all my letters. |
| **sleep** | slept | slept | I slept for ten hours. |
| **feel** | felt | felt | I felt tired after the trip. |
| **leave** | left | left | The train has left. |
| **meet** | met | met | We met at university. |
| **mean** | meant | meant | Sorry, I didn't mean that. |
| **sweep** | swept | swept | He swept the floor. |
| **deal** | dealt | dealt | She dealt with the problem. |

**Tip:** say them in pairs to hear the change: *keep / kept, sleep / slept, feel / felt*. The long sound gets short.
