# The mistakes everyone makes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I **have went** there. | I **have gone** there. | after *have*: participle |
| I **seen** it. | I **saw** it. / I**'ve seen** it. | *seen* needs *have* |
| **Did** you **saw** it? | **Did** you **see** it? | after *did*: base |
| I **didn't went**. | I **didn't go**. | after *didn't*: base |
| It **was wrote** in 2010. | It **was written** in 2010. | passive: participle |
| I **buyed** / **thinked** | I **bought** / **thought** | irregular, not *-ed* |
| I **have saw** him **yesterday**. | I **saw** him yesterday. | finished time: past simple |

The last one is a big one: with a **finished time** (*yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago*), use the **past simple**, not *have + participle*.

## Verbs that are easy to mix up

| Verb | Forms | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **lie** (on a bed) | lie, lay, lain | to be flat: *I lay in bed all morning.* |
| **lie** (not the truth) | lie, lied, lied | regular: *He lied to me.* |
| **lay** | lay, laid, laid | to put something down: *She laid the book on the table.* |
| **rise** | rise, rose, risen | to go up by itself: *The sun rose.* |
| **raise** | raise, raised, raised | to lift something (regular): *She raised her hand.* |
| **fall** | fall, fell, fallen | to drop: *He fell.* |
| **feel** | feel, felt, felt | *I felt sick.* (don't mix *fell* and *felt*) |
| **find** | find, found, found | *I found it.* |
| **found** | found, founded, founded | to start a company (regular): *He founded the company in 2001.* |
