|---|---|---|---|
| **know** | knew | known | I've known him for years. |
| **grow** | grew | grown | You've grown so tall! |
| **throw** | threw | thrown | He threw the ball to me. |
| **blow** | blew | blown | The wind blew all night. |

(*show* is the odd one: its past is regular, *showed*.)

## The odd ones

The most common verbs of all are the strangest. Learn them first, by heart:

| Base | Past simple | Past participle | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **be** | was / were | been | I was tired. Have you been to Aswan? |
| **go** | went | gone | She went home. She has gone home. |
| **do** | did | done | I did my homework. I've done it. |
| **see** | saw | seen | I saw him yesterday. I've seen that film. |
| **lie** | lay | lain | He lay on the sofa all day. |

- **be** has two pasts: *I / he / she / it **was***, *you / we / they **were***.
- **gone** or **been**? *She has **gone** to Cairo* = she's there now. *She has **been** to Cairo* = she went and came back.
- **born** is used as *be born*: *I **was born** in 1999.*

## How to practise (10 minutes a day)

1. **Say all three forms out loud**, like a little song: *buy, bought, bought*. Your ear learns the pattern faster than your eyes.
2. **Cover two columns** of the table with your hand and say them from memory. Then check.
3. **Make one true sentence** for each verb about your own life: *I bought a new phone last month.* Sentences about you stick.
4. **Come back tomorrow.** Five minutes tomorrow beats an hour today. inPace keeps your place, so just press **Continue**.
